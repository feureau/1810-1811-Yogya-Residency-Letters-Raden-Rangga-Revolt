Historical Scope & Archival Records

This digital repository contains complete transcriptions, metadata, and bilingual translations of archival research notes compiled by historian Peter Carey from contemporary 1810–1811 residency files in the Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia (ANRI) in Jakarta, primarily from bundles Djokjo 27, 36, 41, 46, and the Solo Rapporten.

The documents record the dramatic events surrounding the November–December 1810 revolt of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III (Bupati Wedana of Madiun) against Franco-Dutch Governor-General Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, including:

  • Intense court factionalism between Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), the Crown Prince (later HB III), Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Pangéran Natakusuma (Pakualam I), and Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat.
  • Rangga's proclamation as Kangjeng Susuhunan Prabu Ingalaga, military clashes across Sukawati, Grobogan, and Magetan, and the climactic Battle of Sekaran on 17 December 1810.
  • Daendels' forced abdication of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II on 31 December 1810 and the subsequent indemnity treaties.
  • The brief administration of Governor-General Jan Willem Janssens (July 1811) and the arrival of the British expeditionary force under Lord Minto and Thomas Stamford Raffles.
