| AN *'Djokjo Brieven' no. 24* — <span style="color:#a00">**2 Aug. 1811**</span><br>**Eng -> Janssens [Pieter Engelhard to Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens], 2 Aug. 1811:**<br>15 Aug. Party to celebrate Name Day of Napoleon.<br>Janssens plans to Receive Homage from Sultan [Hamengkubuwana II] / Sunan [Pakubuwana IV] (*'Hulde'*) at Semarang Early Sept.; plans to make a journey to the courts. HB II / CP [Crown Prince / Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] appear v. pleased.<br>*(Circled: 20 July 1811)* Decision of Janssens to send **Élèves voor het civiele** to learn Javanese in Yogya / Sala <span style="color:#a00">(red: Élèves voor het civiel)</span>. | **Pieter Engelhard to Governor-General Janssens, 2 August 1811:**<br>Preparations for the 15 August celebration of Napoleon's Name-Day. Janssens plans to receive homage (*hulde*) from the Sultan and Sunan at Semarang in early September and then tour the courts; both Hamengkubuwana II and the Crown Prince appear very pleased.<br>Notes Janssens's 20 July decision to send **élèves voor het civiele** to learn Javanese in Yogyakarta and Surakarta. |
| **Raja Putra Narendra [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] -> Janssens, 7 Aug. 1811 / 17 Rejèb Jimakir 1738:**<br>Receives news that Janssens after 15 Aug. wishes to go to Semarang to Receive Homage, to spare Regent "cost" (Expense) of journey to Batavia.<br>Janssens wishes to meet Regent personally.<br>Regent promises to send Embassy to Semarang; expresses great pleasure that J [Janssens] plans to come to Yogya. | **Raja Putra Narendra to Janssens, 7 August 1811 (17 Rejeb Jimakir AJ 1738):**<br>Acknowledges news that after 15 August Janssens will travel to Semarang to receive homage, sparing the Prince Regent the expense of travelling to Batavia, and wishes to meet the Regent personally. The Regent promises to send an embassy to Semarang and expresses great pleasure at Janssens's planned visit to Yogyakarta. |
