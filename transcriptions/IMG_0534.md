# Transcription of IMG_0534_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0534_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-07-10 to 1810-07-13 (10 Hooimaand to 13 Hooimaand [July] 1810; Carey's bracketed header: `[10 July – 13 July 1810]`)  
**Key Events:**
* The Confrontation with Danureja II (10 Hooimaand [July] 1810): First Clerk Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen reports to Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees regarding Patih DR II's [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] refusal to leave the capital. Accompanied by Adjutant Lammers, Driessen confronts DR II to demand why he neglected to meet Moorrees.
* Palace Watch and Princely Deliberations: DR II explains that at 5:00 PM he was summoned by the Crown Prince (CP [later Hamengkubuwana III]) and Pangéran Mangkubumi, and was subsequently required under royal orders to maintain personal vigilance over the Yogyakarta Kraton during Sultan HB II's [Hamengkubuwana II] absence at Samas.
* Diplomatic Pressure and Coercion: Driessen and Lammers inform DR II that Moorrees urgently requires his presence to discuss Daendels' dispatches and a formal deposition by a Surakarta noblewoman (*Radèn Ayu from Sala area*). When DR II reiterates that he can only meet Moorrees once the Resident returns from Klaten (*Malatten*), Driessen issues a severe threat: if DR II does not come to Klaten, Moorrees will immediately depart for Semarang to report the Patih's insubordination directly to Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels.
* Daendels' Abduction Plan (10 Hooimaand 1810, Yogya): Moorrees reports to Daendels that he received the Governor-General's dispatch of 5 July on 9 July, and intends to execute Daendels' instruction to compel DR II to travel with him to Semarang on 13 July.
* Cavalry Escort and Royal Notification: To force DR II to Klaten, Moorrees plans to dispatch a detachment of European Dragoons to escort/apprehend him, and intends to summon Sindunêgara and Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat so they can inform HB II upon his return from Samas that the Patih was forced under military duress to comply with Daendels' orders.
