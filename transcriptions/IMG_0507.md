# Transcription of IMG_0507_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0507_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 37: *Aankomende Officiële Brieven van den Minister Moorrees aan Resident Solo*, April–May 1810)
- **Date Range:** 1810-04-22 to 1810-05-12
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Deepening diplomatic and jurisdictional crisis surrounding Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III and court ceremonial disputes between Yogyakarta and Batavia. Radèn Rongga feigns illness to evade questioning regarding border raids and plundering, recommending that the matter be examined jointly by all eastern mancanagara bupatis; Sunan Pakubuwana IV dispatches a formal letter of denunciation to Governor-General Daendels condemning Rongga's aggressive conduct; Moorrees requests a Dutch translation of the Sunan's letter from Surakarta; disputes continue over lagging construction of barracks and work huts at Fort Klaten where Surakarta workers have failed to build their complement while Yogya huts have long been ready; Moorrees confers with Patih Danureja II over disputed village lands at Suci; Resident Jacob Andries van Braam arrives in Surakarta; Moorrees reports his complete inability to present the Sunan's letter of complaint to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II because HB II obstinately refuses to accord Moorrees the official ceremonial honors of a Minister mandated by Daendels' ceremonial decree; Moorrees alerts Daendels of HB II's defiance; and fresh agrarian disturbances break out in the district of Pitung, prompting the Crown Prince to dispatch an investigative force.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Jacob Andries van Braam, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Radèn Adipati Danureja II (DR II), Crown Prince (Pangéran Adipati Anom / later HB III).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Sala), Madiun, Klaten (Fort Klathèn), desa Suci, Pitung.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Djokjo Brieven no. 37, Grasmaand (April), Mancanagara (MN) Bupatis, Fort Klaten, Ceremonial of a Minister (Daendels' court etiquette edicts), diplomatic boycott.
