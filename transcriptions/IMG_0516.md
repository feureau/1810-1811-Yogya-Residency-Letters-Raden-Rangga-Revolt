# Transcription of IMG_0516_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0516_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-04-07 to 1810-04-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 recording correspondence of April 1810 on the escalating conflict surrounding Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III; documents Willem Nicolaas Servatius writing to Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees on 7 April 1810 indicating that while documents submitted by Rongga to Radèn Adipati Danureja II matched the originals, they were in total disorder, and reporting intense indignation among Surakarta's eastern *mancanagara* regents over Rongga's haughty pretensions of regional supremacy; notes Servatius' conviction that only decisive counter-measures and central intervention by Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels can halt Rongga's disruptions; transcribes Daendels' furious order of 10 April instructing Moorrees to press the issue directly with Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and warn him that Rongga's actions are poisoning Dutch-Surakarta relations ("I would have hung a European for such vexatiousness"); extracts Sunan Pakubuwana IV's official letter of complaint to Daendels translated by J.W. Winter on 10 April (5 Rabingulawal, Wawu 1737), reporting that the Surakarta village of Ngêbêl was ruined by Rongga's armed forces, condemning Rongga's conduct as a flagrant breach of ancient custom (*adat*), and submitting the final adjudication to Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Johann Friedrich Wilhelm Winter (translator), Jacob Andries van Braam.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Ngêbêl (Panaraga).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Mancanagara regents, Lands Commissie (Territorial Land Commission), Rabingulawal / Wawu 1737 (Javanese calendar), Adat (traditional Javanese law), Capital punishment threat, Official translator (*translateur*).
