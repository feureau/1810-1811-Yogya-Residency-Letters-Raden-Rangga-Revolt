| **Purwadipura to Danureja II, 1 Dec. 1810:**<br>• Purwadipura summons **Radèn Ngabèhi Jayasêntika**, **Mas Tumênggung Kêrtadirja**, and **Alap-Alap**; dispatches them to Magetan to find a secure retreat for the noble ladies (*Radèn Ayus*).<br>• The *Radèn Ayus* are escorted to the safety of **Mount Singa Langu** (6 hours march from Magetan).<br>• Rangga burns rebellious villages in Panaraga that refuse submission.<br><br>**Rebel Appointees with Blue Parasols (*Payung*):**<br>• Radèn Singadrana appointed to title of **Rangga Prawirasêntika** (Bauwêrna / Pinggir / Wirasari).<br>• **Ranamênggala** appointed at Tarik / Waringpati, endowed with blue *payungs*.<br><br>**Assault on Magetan:**<br>• Rangga attacks Magetan simultaneously from three directions: South, East, and North (`S. [E.] N`).<br>• Commanded in person by Rangga himself.<br>• But owing to torrential tropical downpours (*heavy rain*), the assault collapses; Rangga retires to Madiun.<br>• On retreat: rebel commanders **Prawiralaya** and **Prawirabrata**, accompanied by regular *prajurit*, desert Rangga and flee.<br><br>*(red pointer)*:<br>• `> Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara still in Dalêm at Madiun.` | **ANRI Bundel Solo Rapporten 1810–1816 (p. 55):**<br>• **Mas Rangga Tartadiwiria to Ngabèhi Kartarêja in Solo, 2 Dec. 1810 (reporting 25 Nov.):**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Rangga arrives at **Desa Tarik**; advances toward **Kitha Mêndira** and burns down the settlement.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Rangga intercepts the Patih of Radèn Ria; grants him a *piyagem* [royal patent] to assume the title of Tumênggung in Jagaraga; but the Patih later flees from the rebel camp.<br><br>**Coronation of Sunan Prabu Ingalaga (28 Nov. 1810):**<br>• Rangga returns in triumph to his capital at Madiun.<br>• Assembled *priyayi* [nobles] and *dêmang* [village chiefs] present formal congratulations upon his royal accession as **Sunan Prabu Ingalaga**.<br>• **Three artillery cannon shots fired in royal salute.** | **Cakranagara to Pangéran Aria Têpasana & Tumênggung Wirarêja (p. 57):**<br>• Joint commanders of the Surakarta expeditionary force deployed to Jagaraga.<br>• Once the Surakarta *prajurit* are fully assembled in Panaraga, they are strictly ordered to co-operate with the Yogyakarta royal troops operating under the supreme command of Tumênggung Purwadipura.<br><br>**Historical Significance:**<br><br>1. *The Three Cannon Salutes:* Firing 3 cannon shots inside the Madiun residency symbolically enacted Rangga's accession to the imperial dignity of *Sunan*, challenging both Kratons.<br><br>2. *Failure at Magetan:* The staunch resistance of Magetan's loyalist garrison, aided by monsoon floods, marked the strategic turning point that halted Rangga's westward offensive.<br><br>3. *Inter-Court Military Coalition:* Forces from Yogyakarta and Surakarta were officially ordered to join hands on the battlefield under Purwadipura. |
