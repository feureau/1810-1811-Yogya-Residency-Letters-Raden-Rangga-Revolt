| Eng. to Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels],<br>9 Nov. 1810.<br>(Cont.) | Yesterday Rangga [Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III] & v. small following arrives in Yogya [Yogyakarta] (8 Nov.).<br>Brings 7 chained criminals from Madiun = 5 Madurese / 2 Javanese.<br>Handed over by DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to Eng. [Pieter Engelhard] who, after examination sends them on to Landdrost of Sêmarang.<br>Yesterday morning ND [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] / Danukusuma [Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I] / Sindunagara [Kyai Adipati Danureja III] visit Eng. / evening DR II.<br>who says that R.T. Purwadipura [Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura] had reported to HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] that 2,000 Dutch soldiers marching on Yogya already at Bayalali [Boyolali].<br>Eng. does not deny troop movements - but says that Van Braam [Jacob Andries van Braam] coming to Yogya to patch up friendship & HB II. |
| Note of Daendels in margin: | Happy that affairs taking a favourable turn / letter of 10 Nov. 1810 would not arrive in time.<br>Tells Eng. to summon Pangêrans & tell them contents of 2nd letter of Daendels 10 Nov. & Decision to maintain CP [Crown Prince / Pangéran Adipati Anom, later HB III] against all odds / enemies = & enjoin them to loyalty to Yogya Court.<br>Once Rongga & NK [Pangêran Natakusuma / Pakualam I] had been placed *buiten vermogen* [out of commission] no new opposition expected from Yogya Court. |
| Daendels to HB II,<br>29 Oct. 1810 | Reports sending of Van Braam on Commission to Yogya & to have special audience & HB II in Yogya.<br>Enjoins HB II to give Van Braam a ready hearing + Recognise / Respect other Dutch officers / officials in Yogya, officials of King Louis [Louis Bonaparte, King of Holland]. |
