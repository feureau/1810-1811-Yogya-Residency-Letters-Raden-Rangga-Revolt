[House] of Sêtawijaya; Rumour at Clorakan: woman called **Mas Ayu** = Wants to set herself up as a Ratu. If people want to follow her = ask for a `Benang Satukêl`, `Kain Putih`. **Wiratruna** gives place to Mas Ayu, but on 29 Feb. 1816 = told to leave area by R.T. Jagakaria. Wiratruna says Mas Ayu had left area + Become a *Tandhak*. But *Badan alus* remains behind; meets *Demit* of Ratu Kidul = *Sunan Lawu*. Wants to make *Isarat* proclaim Mas Ayu as Queen. Ordered out by R.T. Jagakaria on 29 Feb. 1816, she became a *tandhak* dancer while **Wiratruna** claimed her *badan alus* [subtle body] remained, communing with Ratu Kidul's spirit (*Sunan Lawu*) to proclaim her Queen. |

Purbanegara, and Tum. Wirarija convey the Sunan's compliments to Raffles in Semarang. |
| **PB IV to Raffles, n.d. (? Sept. 1811 / 5 Ramzan 1226) *(p. 973)*:**<br>Receives Instructions of Raffles through Commissioner **W. Robison**:<br>• (i) Peace & Quiet requested for Subjects;<br>• (ii) Return of Burial grounds of *kramats* + *Susuhunans*: Ampel (Surabaya), Giri, Tuban, Batang, Kudus, Kadilangu + peoples of neighbouring provinces (? annexed by Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels]) to be returned to PB IV;<br>• (iii) Return of `10,000 Sp. D.` [Spanish Dollars] in Copper money for *Strandgeld* not paid by Daendels;<br>• (iv) Chinese in Sala to be placed under PB IV's authority *"so that inconveniences of a separate jurisdiction can be avoided"*;<br>• (v) Restoration of Bird's Nest Revenues usurped by van Braam [Jacob Andries van Braam];<br>• (vi) van B + van Naerssen [?] to leave Sala forthwith;<br>• (vii) When new Res. appointed by Brit. = if wants to discuss affairs + PB IV to come + Mangkubumi to Kraton + ceremonies same as V.O.C. pre-1805;<br>• (viii) Prangwedana to be returned from Batavia + placed under PB IV.<br>*(Right margin: PB IV complains people of JKN [Jeksa?] inhabitants to be investigated by PB IV otherwise the inhabitants leave.)* | **Pakubuwana IV to Raffles (via Commissioner W. Robison, Sept. 1811, p. 973):**<br>The Sunan's eight-point wishlist upon British takeover: (i) peace for subjects; (ii) return of sacred *kramat* tombs (Ampel, Giri, Tuban, Batang, Kudus, Kadilangu) and Daendels-annexed provinces; (iii) `10,000 Sp. D.` unpaid *strandgeld*; (iv) jurisdiction over Surakarta Chinese; (v) restoration of bird's nest revenues usurped by van Braam; (vi) immediate removal of van Braam and his associates; (vii) restoration of pre-1805 V.O.C. court etiquette; and (viii) return of Prangwedana from Batavia under PB IV's authority. |
