### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Willem Nicolaas Servatius** (`Servatius`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 308). Assistant Resident at Surakarta (1810–1811) who handled border incident reports with Yogyakarta.
  * **Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees** (`Moorrees`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 333). Resident at Yogyakarta (1810) preceding Pieter Engelhard.
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus)** (`PB IV`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 18). Ruler of Surakarta (reigned 1788–1820).
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III** (`Rangga / Radèn Rongga`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). *Bupati wedana* of Madiun (1796–1810) who launched the November 1810 rebellion against Daendels.
  * **Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat** (`R.M. Aria Wiriadiningrat`): Listed under *Senior Officials (Priyayi)* (line 218). Senior bupati in the eastern mancanagara / Panaraga region.
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Miradirja & Radèn Tumenggung Bratanegara** (`R.T. Miradirja / R.T. Bratanegara`): Regional bupatis in Panaraga reporting to Surakarta.
  * **J.W. Winter** (`trans. Winter`): Renowned Dutch court translator and Javanese linguist stationed at Surakarta.
  * **Radèn Ngabèhi Prawirapraja, Mas Ngabèhi Puspadiwiria, Mas Ngabèhi Prawiralaya, Radèn Ngabèhi Prawirabrata** (`Head of Rangga's force`): Field commanders and lieutenants (*lurah mantri*) in Radèn Rongga's raiding detachment.
  * **Mas Rangga Kartawirya & Ngabèhi Kartareja** (`Mas Rangga Kartawirya Nukawinangun to Ng. Kartareja`): Local regional officials reporting troop movements along the Bengawan Solo border.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Panaraga (Ponorogo)** (`Panaraga`): Listed under *Religious Leaders / Mancanagara* (line 128). Key eastern mancanagara regency.
  * **Madiun**: Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 401). Administrative center of the eastern mancanagara and seat of Radèn Rongga III.
  * **Rèdi Ngèbèl (Mount Ngebel)** (`Rèdi Ngèbèl / Ngèbèl`): Volcanic mountain and border district between Madiun and Ponorogo.
  * **Solo River (Bengawan Sala)** (`Solo river`): Listed under *Geographical Terms* (lines 409, 507). Major river artery crossed by Rongga's marching army.
  * **Pinggir & Nukawinangun**: Villages and crossing points in the Sokawati / Bengawan Solo borderlands.
* **Terms:**
  * **bupati**: Listed under *Glossary & Titles* (lines 173, 195, 218). High-ranking provincial administrator/governor.
  * **demang**: Listed under *Glossary & Titles* (lines 107, 204). District chief or rural tax supervisor.
  * **patih**: Listed under *Glossary & Titles* (lines 176, 181–186, 212). Chief administrative minister of a royal court.
  * **Spanish dollar (Real batu / SpD)** (`Sp.D.`): Listed under *Currencies* (line 674). Silver coinage used for valuing looted goods (`171 1/2 Sp.D.`).
  * **kwaaddoeners**: Dutch colonial term for malefactors, bandits, or political insurgents.
