### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Engelhard to Servatius**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br>(Secretary Solo)<br><br>*Panaraga Alert* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng] to Willem Nicolaas Servatius [W.N. Servatius, Secretary of Surakarta Residency]:<br>• Panaraga regency reports that Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] threatens an immediate assault against their territory; the bupati of Panaraga is currently absent in Surakarta.<br>• Urgent alert dispatched to coordinate border defense between Surakarta and Yogyakarta lands. |
| **Expedition Mobilizes**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br><br>*1,000 Troops & Cannon* | Royal punitive expedition departs Yogyakarta under command of Lieutenant Paulus [Lt. Paulus] and Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura [R.T. Purwadipura]:<br>• **Troop Strength:** 300 riflemen (*schutters*), 700 pikemen (*piekeniers*), total 1,000 men, accompanied by 4 small 1-pounder field cannon.<br>• Expedition marches from Klaten toward Marton.<br>• DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] writes directly to Radèn Tumênggung Cakranagara [bupati of Panaraga] directing immediate defensive maneuvers. |
| **Engelhard to van Braam**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br><br>*Natakusuma Guard* | Resident Engelhard to Commissioner Jacob Andries van Braam [J.A. van Braam]:<br>• The expedition has marched to intercept Rangga and return him alive or dead.<br>• DR II writes to the *Patih* of Solo [Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II] requesting transit and military aid.<br>• HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] issues a royal circular letter to all Mangkunagaran regents commanding non-cooperation with the rebel.<br>• HB II thanks Resident Engelhard personally for his prompt assistance.<br>• **Warning on Natakusuma:** Engelhard advises HB II to place a strict military guard around the residence (*dalèm*) of Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I], who is strongly suspected of being in secret collusion with Rangga. |
| **Engelhard to Winckelmann**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br><br>*Rangga Flees Yogya* | Resident Engelhard to Brigadier F.C.P. von Winckelmann (Semarang):<br>• On the night of 20 November 1810, Rangga fled Yogyakarta with approximately 300 armed followers, heading east toward his stronghold of Madiun.<br>• HB II immediately sent 1,000 troops with 4 Kraton cannon commanded by 9 royal Tumênggungs to pursue him. |
| **Servatius to Engelhard**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br>(Dispatch No. 101)<br><br>*Delanggu Breakout* | W.N. Servatius to Resident Engelhard (Dispatch No. 101):<br>• Pangéran Prangwedana [Mangkunagara II] and Sunan PB IV [Pakubuwana IV] were alerted immediately to block the post road and intercept Rangga.<br>• However, Rangga moved with extraordinary speed: he had already bypassed Delanggu at 10:00 a.m. with 250 horsemen and pikemen before the blockade could close. |
