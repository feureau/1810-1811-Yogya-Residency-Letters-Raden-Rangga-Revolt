# Transcription of IMG_0506_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0506_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 37: *Aankomende Officiële Brieven van den Minister Moorrees en P. Engelhard 1810–1811 aan Resident Solo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-03-11 to 1810-04-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official incoming correspondence sent by Acting Minister J.W. Moorrees at Yogyakarta to Assistant Resident Willem Nicolaas Servatius and Resident Jacob Andries van Braam at Surakarta. Moorrees receives initial Surakarta complaints regarding an armed cross-border raid led by Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III; requests the original Javanese text and Dutch translation by Yogya court translator Vick; receives written report from Patih Danureja II promising to question Radèn Rongga regarding the border conflict in the Roban district (desa Suci); reports on orders to arrest Bèkèl Jetra Wijaya by the Acting Patih of Solo; notes that the approaching Garebeg Mulud religious festival prevents Moorrees from travelling to greet Van Braam upon his return to Solo; reports the arrest of two Chinese subjects involving reports from the Kapitan Cina of Kedu (Sehiangsing) and Tan Jin Sing of Yogya; and presses Patih Danureja II to complete construction work on Fort Klaten pursuant to the 16 October 1808 meeting of the two Patihs, which DR II explains has been stalled awaiting promised corvée laborers from Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Jacob Andries van Braam, Radèn Adipati Danureja II (DR II), Tan Jin Sing (Kapitan Cina of Yogya), Vick (Javanese translator), Gustaf Wilhelm Wiese, Bèkèl Jetra Wijaya, Sehiangsing (Kapitan Cina of Kedu).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Sala), Madiun, Klaten (Fort Klathèn), Kedu (Kedhu), desa Suci (Roban).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Djokjo Brieven no. 37, Lentenmaand (March), Mulud (Garebeg Mulud), Kapitan Cina, Bèkèl, Fort Klaten, cross-border border dispute.
