# Transcription of IMG_0501_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0501_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek 1602–1811*, vol. XVI [1808–1811], ed. J.A. van der Chijs [Batavia: Landsdrukkerij, 1897], pp. 72–73: Government Decrees of H.W. Daendels, February 1810)
- **Date Range:** 1810-02-07 to 1810-02-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official printed decrees of Governor-General Daendels issued at Buitenzorg and Batavia in Sprokkelmaand (February) 1810. Includes: doubling the salaries of native clerks and bookbinder at Buitenzorg secretariat to prevent resignations; granting silver currency allowances (20 rijksdaalders per timber district) to *blandong* forest-labourers across 11 forestry districts (Oosthoek, Tuban, Lasem, Rembang, Juwana/Pati, Demak, Jepara, Semarang, Kendal/Kaliwungu, Pekalongan, Tegal, Cirebon) to celebrate a semi-annual "nationale vrolijkheid" (folk festival) at the start and conclusion of the timber-cutting season; forfeiting 1809 back-pay of a delinquent colonial official; and opening governmental intervention into the heavy debt obligations of Prince Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) of Surakarta stemming from the withheld 1795 inheritance of Mangkunagara I.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II / Ario Praboe Prang Wedono), Pangéran Adipati Mangkunagara I (Amangcoe Negoro).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Buitenzorg, Batavia, Surakarta (Souracarta), Oosthoek, Tuban (Touban), Lasem (Lassum), Rembang, Juwana (Joana) & Pati, Demak, Jepara (Japara), Semarang (Samarang), Kaliwungu (Caliewoengoe), Kendal (Candal), Pekalongan (Paccalongan), Tegal (Tagal), Cirebon (Cheribon), Pamanukan (Pamanoekan), Ciasem (Tji-asem).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek*, Sprokkelmaand (February), Slachtmaand (November), blandong (forest timber cutters / corvée labor), rijksdaalders (silver RD), tractementen (salaries), griffie (secretariat), college van administratie der houtbosschen (Board of Forestry Administration), nationale vrolijkheid.

| **10 Sprokkelmaand<br>[10 Feb. 1810]<br>Disciplinary Action** | **Vervallen verklaring aan den Lande van alle, in 1809 door een ambtenaar verdiende maandgelden:**<br><br>• All 1809 monthly salary payments accrued by a negligent colonial official forfeited to the state as compensation for damage caused through apathy and deceit; official threatened with dismissal (*deportement*) and dishonour (*infamie*) upon further breach. |
| **10 Sprokkelmaand<br>[10 Feb. 1810]<br>Surakarta Court** | **Bemoeijing der Regering met de schulden van een Soloschen pangeran:**<br><br>• Official governmental intervention into the financial crisis and mounting indebtedness of **Pangéran Prangwedana [Mangkunagara II / Ario Praboe Prang Wedono]** at Surakarta:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Notes that Prangwedana had been deprived of a large portion of the inheritance of his grandfather, **Pangéran Adipati Mangkunagara I [Amangcoe Negoro]** (died 1795), while forced to incur heavy expenditures maintaining princely rank commensurate with his birth and station... *(continued on p. 74)*. |
