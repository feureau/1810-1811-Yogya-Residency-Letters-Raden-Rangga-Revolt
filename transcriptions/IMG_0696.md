| **DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to Eng. [Pieter Engelhard], 28 Sapar 1738 / 22 Mar. 1811:**<br>R.P. Narendra [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] always shown respect / love for HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II].<br>Rumour that R.P.N / HB II great enmity + followers told not to mix + each other + orders to respective *prajurits* to be on their guard.<br><span style="color:#a00">Split Kasepuhan Kanoman</span><br>But last Mon. party on Northern *Pasebaan* attended by R.P.N + HB II who rode out of Kraton together. Had never happened before in old *adat*. `∴ DR II denies all knowledge of Rumours.`<br>Reports of increase in robberies / murders in Jav. villages since Jan. 1811. DR II says lawlessness true = But sends out R.T. Cakradiwiria to Mataram lands to inspect situation there as regards *kècus*. | **Danureja II to Pieter Engelhard, 22 March 1811 (28 Sapar AJ 1738):**<br>Raja Putra Narendra has always shown respect and affection for Hamengkubuwana II. Rumors circulated of deep enmity between them (*Kasepuhan* vs. *Kanoman* split), with followers forbidden to mingle and *prajurits* ordered on guard. Yet last Monday both attended a gathering on the Northern *Pasebaan*, riding out of the Kraton together—something unprecedented under old *adat*. Danureja II therefore denies all knowledge of the rumors.<br>Regarding reports of rising robberies and murders in Javanese villages since January 1811, Danureja II admits the lawlessness is real and has dispatched R.T. Cakradiwiria to the Mataram districts to investigate *kècu* [bandit] activity. |
| **DR II to Eng., 1 Mulud 1738 / 25 Mar. 1811:**<br>R.T. Yudakusuma returns to Yogya from Wirasari. R.P. Narendra tells him to return there to put himself at disposal of Dutch *(cf Wirasari annexed)*.<br>Asks Eng. that R.T. Sasrakusuma [Radèn Tumenggung Sasrakusuma] could still be regarded as 1st Reg. of Wirasari / Grobogan because he was the oldest.<br>Asks that MN [Mancanagara] Bupatis should be allowed to pay *Bakti* (homage) money to R.P. Narendra for *Mulud* and until such time as new boundaries had been worked out by G. Wiese [Gustaf Wilhelm Wiese] + R.T. Sindunagara [Kyai Adipati Danureja III], equally with those lands in West MN given to Yogya. Tax Farmer should continue to pay tax to Govt.<br>*(Margin note: See BL Add MS 12342 on apptmts in E. MN 28 Jan. 1811)* | **Danureja II to Pieter Engelhard, 25 March 1811 (1 Mulud AJ 1738):**<br>R.T. Yudakusuma returned to Yogyakarta from Wirasari, and Raja Putra Narendra ordered him back to place himself at the Dutch authorities' disposal (Wirasari having been annexed). Requests that R.T. Sasrakusuma still be recognized as First Regent of Wirasari/Grobogan due to his seniority.<br>Also asks that the Mancanagara Bupatis be allowed to pay *bakti* (homage) money to Raja Putra Narendra for *Mulud* until new boundaries are finalized by Gustaf Wilhelm Wiese and R.T. Sindunagara, including the Western Mancanagara lands ceded to Yogyakarta; the tax farmer should continue paying taxes to the Government. |
| **DR II to Eng., 26 Mulud 1738 / 20 Apr. 1811:**<br>Eng. to pay visit to present compliments to HB II on occasion of *Garebeg Mulud*.<br>R.P.N asks that Eng. should not make a mention of his new position as Regent to HB II.<br>One party at *Kadipatèn* on Mon. evening. Eng. can behave as he likes. | **Danureja II to Pieter Engelhard, 20 April 1811 (26 Mulud AJ 1738):**<br>Engelhard is to pay a complimentary visit to Hamengkubuwana II on the occasion of *Garebeg Mulud*. Raja Putra Narendra requests that Engelhard avoid mentioning his new position as Prince Regent in front of Hamengkubuwana II. An evening gathering will be held at the *Kadipatèn* on Monday, where Engelhard may act at his discretion. |
