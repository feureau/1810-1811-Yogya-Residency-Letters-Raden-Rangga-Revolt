# Transcription of IMG_0522_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0522_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-01-14 to 1810-05-21
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 tracking the diplomatic and border confrontation surrounding Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III in May 1810; opens with Sultan Hamengkubuwana II writing to Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees on 5 May expressing profound astonishment that Sunan Pakubuwana IV bypassed traditional Javanese custom (*adat*) by appealing directly to Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels instead of coordinating bilaterally through Surakarta Resident Willem Nicolaas Servatius and the *Bupati Nayaka* of both courts; records dispatches from Jacob Andries van Braam transmitting reports from Panaraga regent Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat alleging that Rangga had exceeded his jurisdictional authority; transcribes an intelligence report of 20 May 1810 from Panaraga bupatis (Wiriadiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Prawiradirja, and Radèn Tumenggung Bratanêgara) to Solo Patih Radèn Adipati Cakranagara recounting events on 24 Bêsar, Bé 1736, when Radèn Rongga dispatched religious emissaries headed by Haji Bêsari (Kyai Hasan Besari of Tegalsari) and ulama (*ulami*) accompanied by 40 religious officials (*priests / santri*) to complain of robberies in Ngêbêl and Sikêdok; records Wiriadiningrat's reply of 7 Bêsar 1736 (14 January 1810) addressing Rangga affectionately as "my son" and permitting cross-border pursuit of 13 accused bandits, before reporting that on the very same Saturday night Rangga's armed forces attacked and plundered Ngêbêl.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Jacob Andries van Braam, Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Radèn Tumenggung Prawiradirja, Radèn Tumenggung Bratanêgara, Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II), Kyai Hasan Besari (Haji Bêsari), Ulama / Priests (*santri*).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Panaraga (Ponorogo), Madiun, Ngêbêl, Sikêdok (Nêkêdok).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Adat (Javanese custom), Bupati Nayaka, Tegalsari pesantrèn, Religious emissaries (*santri / ulami*), Diplomatic kinship term ("my son"), Cross-border cattle rustling raid, Bêsar Bé 1736 (Javanese calendar).
