### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III** (`Rangga / R.`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). *Bupati wedana* of Madiun who openly assumed royal prerogatives (the sovereign yellow *payong*) and proclaimed himself *Susunan Ingalaga* in defiance of Daendels and the colonial order.
  * **Pieter Engelhard** (`Eng`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 331). Resident of Yogyakarta (1808, 1810–1811).
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 12). Delivered an official letter formally acknowledging his son-in-law's flight.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Pangéran Ratu / Crown Prince)** (`PR II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 13). Reigned 1812–1814.
  * **Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II)** (`PWD / MN`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 27). Head of the Mangkunagaran Legion mobilizing to intercept Rongga.
  * **Radèn Aria Sindunegara (Kyai Adipati Danureja III)** (`Sindunagara`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 183). Yogyakarta court negotiator; later *patih* (1811–1813).
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat (Pakualam II)** (`Natadiningrat`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 24).
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I** (`Danukusuma`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 199).
  * **Radèn Adipati Danureja II** (`P. / Patih`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 182). Patih of Yogyakarta (1799–1811).
  * **Willem Nicolaas Servatius** (`Servatius`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 308). Assistant Resident at Surakarta tracking Rongga's route.
  * **Jacob Andries van Braam** (`v. B.`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 307).
  * **Marshal Herman Willem Daendels** (`D / Daendels`): Listed under *Colonial Officials: Governors-General* (line 282).
  * **Brigadier von Winckelmann**: Senior Dutch infantry commander directing reinforcements from Semarang.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Delanggu**: Strategic relay post on the highway between Klaten and Surakarta through which Radèn Rongga passed.
  * **Jambu**: Mountain pass between Ambarawa and Secang on the post route from Semarang to Yogyakarta, notorious for being impassable during heavy monsoon rains.
  * **Semarang**: North coast military headquarters.
  * **Yogyakarta Residency**: Seat of Dutch administrative authority in Yogya.
* **Terms:**
  * **payong (payung)**: Listed under *Insignia & Cultural Terms* (line 542). Royal ceremonial umbrella; the golden/yellow payong was strictly reserved for reigning sovereigns (*Sultan* / *Sunan*), making Rongga's use of it an overt claim to monarchical sovereignty.
  * **Susunan Ingalaga (Senapati Ingalaga / Panatagama)**: The supreme royal and religious title of Mataram rulers ('Commander in the Field / Regulator of the Faith'), demonstrating the millenarian and sovereign character of Rongga's rebellion.
