### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Pieter Engelhard** (`Eng`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 331). Resident of Yogyakarta (1808, 1810–1811) who enforced Daendels' new ceremonial edict (*Cérémonieel der Residenten*).
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Pangéran Ratu / Crown Prince)** (`PR II / CP`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 13). Reigned 1812–1814. Intermediary between the Kraton and the Residency during the diplomatic impasse.
  * **Marshal Herman Willem Daendels** (`Daendels`): Listed under *Colonial Officials: Governors-General* (line 282). Issued the strict ceremonial decree of 28 July 1810 that sparked the Garebeg crisis.
  * **Jacob Andries van Braam** (`van B / v. B.`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 307). Resident at Surakarta and Government Commissioner sent to arbitrate the dispute.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danureja II** (`P. / Patih`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 182). Patih of Yogyakarta (1799–1811) whose sharp letters inflamed tensions with Engelhard.
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I** (`Danukusuma`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 199). Senior Yogya nayaka and confidant of the court.
  * **Radèn Aria Sindunegara (Kyai Adipati Danureja III)** (`Sindunagara`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 183). Senior court official; later patih (1811–1813).
  * **Willem Nicolaas Servatius** (`Servatius`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 308). Assistant Resident at Surakarta (1810–1811).
  * **Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst** (`L. van Pabst`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 348). Forestry and administrative official in Rembang (1810); later Resident of Yogyakarta (1827).
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Yogyakarta Kraton** (`KRATON`): Royal palace of Hamengkubuwana II.
  * **Surakarta** (`Solo / Sala`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 393). Capital of the Sunanate.
  * **Semarang**: Listed under *Colonial Officials / Places* (lines 210, 296). North coast colonial military headquarters.
  * **Panolan**: Listed under *Places: Mancanagara* (lines 36, 189, 244). Eastern mancanagara regency on the Bengawan Solo border.
  * **Rembang**: Listed under *Places: Coastal Residencies* (line 458). Important teak forestry district.
  * **Cirebon**: Listed under *Places: North Coast* (line 458). West Java coastal residency.
* **Terms:**
  * **Garebeg Puasa**: Major Islamic court celebration marking the culmination of the fasting month of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr); the dispute over artillery salutes and diplomatic seating protocols escalated into an open breach between HB II and Daendels.
  * **Bupati Nayaka**: The core cabinet of senior executive court bupatis (four inner and four outer bupatis) governing the royal realm.
  * **pasisir**: Northern coast regions where travel passes were strictly mandated by Daendels' decree of 16 September 1810.
