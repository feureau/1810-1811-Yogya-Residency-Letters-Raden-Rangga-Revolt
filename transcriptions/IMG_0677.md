### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Margin:** Continuation of printed decree from *Plakaatboek* XVII, pp. 716–717
* **Bottom Left (Faint Pink Pencil):** `p. 717.`
* **Document Subject:** Articles 4 through 10 of Governor-General Janssens's 20 July 1811 decree regulating reporting schedules, uniform attire, 30 silver rijksdaalders monthly stipend, prohibition against bilingual servants, and merit promotions for civil language cadets.
* **Physical Characteristics:** Ruled white notebook folio backing paper; two binder ring punch holes on right margin with tape reinforcement patches and dark rust oxidation rings; printed gazette text mounted with adhesive.
