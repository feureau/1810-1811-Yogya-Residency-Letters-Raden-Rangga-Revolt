| `(sic!)` Horsebury [Horsfield] at Surakarta - great collection of Botanical objects - Bat. [Batavian] Gen. [General] Pays him 300 Sp.D. [Spanish Dollars] p. month.<br>V. Robison deemed it a very valuable collection, authorized Horsfield to stay in Solo [Surakarta], and granted him funds to continue his work. |
| Eng [Pieter Engelhard] / van B [van Braam] to Correspond Direct + Minto | Residents Pieter Engelhard and Jacob Andries van Braam instructed to correspond directly with Lord Minto. |
| `26 Sept.` Leaves Yogya arrives `12 pm` `27 Sept.` Semarang | Departed Yogyakarta on **26 September** and arrived at Semarang at `12 p.m.` on **27 September 1811**. |
| `Note of Temp [Temporary] arrangements made by Capt. Robinson at Sala / Yogya` *(no other enclosures + Dispatch)*:<br>• Troop of 70 Ambonese cavalry at Sala / Yogya. To be continued in their posts until relieved by English *(could be continued thereafter if need be)*<br>• Permission to Horsefield [Horsfield] to stay<br>• Sala / Yogya / Semarang / Pekalongan: old people pensioners - from Dutch govt - many too old to work `∴` pensions continued from Dutch govt.<br>• Pacht / Farms not to be Renewed until orders from Govt. [Government] *(nearly due out)*<br>• Considerable Revenue Farm of Tobacco / opium. Birds' nests from S. [South] Coast. | **Temporary arrangements made by Capt. Robison at Solo and Yogyakarta:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• A troop of 70 Ambonese cavalry at Solo and Yogyakarta to remain at their posts until relieved by the English (and possibly retained thereafter if needed).<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Permission for Dr. Horsfield to remain.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Pensions for aged Dutch-government pensioners in Solo, Yogyakarta, Semarang, and Pekalongan to be continued.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• *Pacht* / revenue farms nearly due not to be renewed without Government orders.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Noted considerable revenue farms in tobacco, opium, and South Coast birds' nests. |
| Van Naak Landrost [Landdrost] of Pekalongan - Frenchman in every thing should be Relieved + sent to his Idol Bonaparte. | **Van Naak, Landdrost of Pekalongan:** Described as a Frenchman in everything who should be relieved and sent back *"to his Idol Bonaparte."* |
