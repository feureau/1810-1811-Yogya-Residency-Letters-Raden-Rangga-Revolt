### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eng. to von Winckelmann**<br>7 Dec. 1810 | Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] to General P.G. von Winckelmann (7 Dec. 1810):<br>• Transmits formal response acknowledging the imminent personal arrival of Marshal Daendels in Semarang to assume supreme command of the Central Java expedition. |
| **Daendels to HB II**<br>5 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Marshal's Indictment* | Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] to Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] (5 Dec. 1810):<br>• Acknowledges HB II's royal letter of 27 Nov. 1810.<br>• Warns the Sultan incessantly against court advisers who are deliberately poisoning his mind against the Dutch Government.<br>• Issues a stern warning against treacherous anti-European cabals entrenched within the Kraton.<br>• Declares that Rangga's flight and self-proclamation as Emperor (*Sunan Prabu Ingalaga*) causes Daendels no surprise whatever: The Governor-General states he had known for two full years that Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] was an inherently evil individual.<br>• Notes that Rangga's rebellion has now conclusively revealed his true subversive motives.<br>• Daendels states bluntly that he can place no confidence or certainty in HB II's sentiments, and still less in his professions of royal friendship. |
| **Hostage Demand & Summit**<br>5 Dec. 1810<br><br>*(Red underline: 10 Dec.)* | Formal colonial demands and conference ultimatum:<br>• Daendels peremptorily demands that Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I / NK], Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat / ND], and Radèn Tumênggung Natapraja [NP] be delivered up immediately into Dutch custody as a material guarantee of peace across Java, and to prevent them from uniting with Rangga during the rebellion.<br>• Announces that a formidable military force has already been concentrated at Semarang.<br>• **Personal Arrival:** Daendels announces he will arrive at Semarang in person on **10 Dec. 1810**.<br>• The Marshal will convene a high diplomatic conference comprising: Resident Engelhard, DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II], [? Suratius], and Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (with HB II permitted to appoint any other royal representative he wishes).<br>• This conference will determine future colonial policy toward the Sultanate.<br>• Emphasizes that HB II can expect no military support from Batavia as long as NK, ND, and NP remain undelivered, which will be taken as the sole genuine test of the Sultan's good faith. |
| **Eng. to Paulus**<br>10 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Sumanêgara's Defection* | Resident Engelhard to Lieutenant Paulus (10 Dec. 1810):<br>• Confirms 5 Dec. reports on the significant military reinforcement of Purwadipura [PWD]'s expedition by loyal Mancanagara [MN] regents.<br>• Reports critical intelligence that Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara has officially deserted Rangga, and that large numbers of Rangga's *prajurit* [soldiers] are deserting daily.<br>• Notifies Paulus of Daendels's arrival at Semarang with heavy expeditionary regiments.<br>• Intelligence from Admiral Pieter van Braam [van B] in Surabaya reports that Radèn Tumênggung Prawirayuda of Sekaran has retreated to Japan. |
