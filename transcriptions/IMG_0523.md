# Transcription of IMG_0523_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0523_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-01-14 to 1810-05-20 (referencing events of Sura–Sapar 1736 / Jan.–Feb. 1810)
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 documenting the detailed judicial inquest conducted by Surakarta regents into cross-border plundering raids launched by Madiun forces into Panaraga; records village inquest results finding two inhabitants dead, three houses burned, multiple homes plundered, and three villagers (Nuriman, Amad Usman, and Bonggotaruna) wounded; catalogues the nine commanders of Radèn Rongga's 800-strong expedition: Radèn Ngabèhi Prawirapraja, Mas Ngabèhi Puspadiwiria, Kartareja, Bratasena (Radèn Prawirabrata), Jayingkara, Tirta-atmaja, Prawiralaya, Ranadirja, and Partakusuma; details border incursions along the Panaraga-Madiun boundary (*tampingan*), including the theft of a complete *gamêlan* orchestra set and possessions from Nêmpu of Ngêbêl on 9 Sura 1736; lists raids by Dimang Nitrajaya and his brother Ranasêntika of Bulak (Lembah) with 30 followers fencing stolen loot through Malilir in Madiun; records the despoiling of Dipadrana of Karang Lo on 24 Sura 1736 (losses evaluated at 77 Spanish Dollars [Sp. D.]) by 40 bandits led by Tambakyuda of Wanut; notes the torchlight robbery of Nêtradrana of Karang-anyar on 30 Sura 1736 (losses evaluated at 87 Sp. D.); records the robbery of Ningadangra of desa Warak on 2 Sapar 1736; concludes with Panaraga Regent Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat's formal legal summons to Radèn Rongga demanding enforcement of order under the terms of the *Surat Anggêr* (inter-court judicial code).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Radèn Ngabèhi Prawirapraja, Mas Ngabèhi Puspadiwiria, Kartareja, Bratasena (Radèn Prawirabrata), Jayingkara, Tirta-atmaja, Prawiralaya, Ranadirja, Partakusuma, Nêmpu, Dimang Nitrajaya, Ranasêntika, Ranawijaya, Pak Naya Bader, Dipadrana, Tambakyuda, Nêtradrana, Radèn Tumenggung Prawiradirja, Ningadangra, Radèn Tumenggung Bratanêgara, Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Panaraga (Ponorogo), Madiun, Tampingan borderland, Ngêbêl, Bulak (Lembah), Malilir, Karang Lo, Wanut, Karang-anyar, Desa Warak.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Gamêlan orchestra theft, Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars / Real de a Ocho], Surat Anggêr (Inter-court judicial treaties / Anggêr Sepuluh), Tampingan (frontier zone), Sura / Sapar 1736 (Javanese calendar).
