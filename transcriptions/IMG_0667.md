| **Rangga's Proclamation & Assembly**<br>Late Nov. 1810<br><br>*Sambong Nlema Camp* | Operational intelligence on rebel movements:<br>• Radèn Tumênggung Sasrakusuma of Grobogan was originally marching with his troops to join Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura [R.T. Purwadipura] against Rangga; however, Sasrakusuma defected and joined Rangga's rebellion.<br>• The bulk of Rangga's dispersed followers reassemble, establishing their main field camp at the village of Sambong Nlema on the borders of Madiun and Sukawati. |
| **Winckelmann to Engelhard**<br>28 Nov. 1810<br>(Dispatch No. 139)<br><br>*(Double red quotes:*<br>*Imperial Proclamation)* | Brigadier von Winckelmann to Resident Engelhard (forwarding Rangga's circular proclamation of 26 Nov. 1810):<br>• **Self-Proclaimed Emperor:** Rangga proclaims himself **Sultan Prabu Ingalaga** (establishing his forest capital at Kuta Pêtik in the alas Kêlanggan), declaring himself Emperor and Supreme Protector of both Javanese and Chinese peoples.<br>• Elevates Sasrakusuma to the royal title of **Pangéran**.<br>• Proclaims impending assaults against the colonial and regency coastal areas (*Pasisir*) of Grobogan, Blora, and Sukawati. |
| **Servatius to Engelhard**<br>30 Nov. 1810<br><br>*Felled Tree Roadblocks* | Willem Nicolaas Servatius [W.N. Servatius] to Resident Engelhard:<br>• All roads leading into Madiun are rendered completely impassable by Rangga's men, who have felled massive teak trees across all major transit routes and defiles. |
| **Wangsadirana Report**<br>Late Nov. 1810<br><br>*Kuta Pêtik Strongpoint* | Wangsadirana to Radèn Tumênggung of Semarang / Panji Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat / Pakualam II]:<br>• Confirms Rangga is entrenched at Pêtik in the Madiun area, heavily fortifying his position with artillery cannon. |
