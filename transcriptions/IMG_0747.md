## Annotated Transcription

| Original Text & Annotations | English Translation / Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Rumour that N.K. [Pangéran Natakusuma] prepared to offer huge BRIBE to get N.D. [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] appointed as PATIH `||`.<br>Engelhard [Pieter Engelhard] tells -> Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels]: N.K. power behind the THRONE = as confirmed by memoirs of van Rhijn, van IJsseldijk [Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk], van Olberg, Waterloo, Morris.<br>Daendels remonstrates over ill-treatment of legal Embassy from Demak. | **October–November 1810 (Retrospective Summary):**<br>Rumor that Natakusuma was ready to offer a huge bribe to secure Natadiningrat's appointment as Patih. Engelhard informed Daendels that Natakusuma was the power behind the throne—confirmed by memoirs of van Rhijn, van IJsseldijk, van Olberg, Waterloo, and Morris. Daendels also protested the mistreatment of a legal embassy from Demak. |
| **2nd Visit of van Braam [Jacob Andries van Braam] (12–15 Nov. 1810):**<br>On arrival of van Braam: 2 letters from Daendels:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1. `[25 Oct.]` Complaining of **Raden Rangga's** [Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III] attack on Chinese Customs House in Demak;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2. `[5 Nov.]` Painting Raden Natakusuma in black colours to the SULTAN [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II].<br>SULTAN replies refusing to believe situation; continues intimacy + N.K.<br>**9 Nov.:** Demand of Daendels that Raden Rangga should come to Buitenzorg to pay allegiance to the Dutch. | **Van Braam's Second Mission (12–15 November 1810):**<br>Brought two letters from Daendels: (1) 25 October, complaining of **Radèn Rongga's** attack on the Chinese customs house in Demak; and (2) 5 November, portraying Natakusuma in dark colors to the Sultan. The Sultan refused to believe the charges and continued his intimacy with Natakusuma.<br>**9 November:** Daendels demanded that Radèn Rongga come to Buitenzorg to swear allegiance to the Dutch. |
| **RANGGA = FLEES 21 NOV.:** *(red note: 1810)*<br>Leaves 2 letters — SUMADININGRAT [Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat] / NATA DININGRAT to explain reasons for flight to SULTAN.<br>**24 NOV. 1810:** Daendels demands immediate justice on Raden Rangga. Daendels writes in margin of 30 Nov. letter that had always foreseen flight of Rangga.<br>**25 NOV. 1810:** Daendels writes to Engelhard that certain that Sultan had no part in Rangga's rebellion; asks Sultan to hand over N.K. / N.D. and *"Hold them in Semarang"* during *"Troubles"*. | **Radèn Rongga's Flight (21 November 1810):**<br>Rongga fled, leaving two letters—addressed to **Sumadiningrat** and **Natadiningrat**—explaining his reasons to the Sultan.<br>**24 November 1810:** Daendels demanded immediate justice against Radèn Rongga (later noting in the margin of his 30 November letter that he had always foreseen Rongga's flight).<br>**25 November 1810:** Daendels wrote to Engelhard stating he was certain the Sultan had no part in Rongga's rebellion, and requested that the Sultan hand over Natakusuma and Natadiningrat to be *"held in Semarang"* during the *"troubles."* |
