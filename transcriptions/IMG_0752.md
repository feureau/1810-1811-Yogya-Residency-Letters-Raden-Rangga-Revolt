| *A.N. Djogja Letters No. 22* — <span style="color:#a00">**1811 (Sept) / DJOGJA 1811 A.N.**</span><br>Orders for murder written on small unsigned pieces of paper which when reading to be sent back to G.G. [Governor-General].<br>WATERLOO [Matthijs Waterloo] waits till arrival of Janssens [Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens], who remits order and improves situation of prisoners. | **Arsip Nasional, *Djokja Letters* No. 22 — September 1811:**<br>The murder orders [against Natakusuma and Natadiningrat] were written on small unsigned slips of paper to be returned to the Governor-General once read; Waterloo waited until Janssens arrived, who rescinded the order and improved the prisoners' conditions. |
| **5th Sept. — Engelhard [Pieter Engelhard] -> Janssens** *(See letter of AN 'Djokjo Brieven' no. 24)*:<br>TROOPS from Djogja [Yogyakarta] — leave at 7.00, 4 Sept. 1811.<br>REGENT [Sultan Radja / Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] unable to take a step without permission of Father [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II].<br>MOBILISATION of DJOGJA TROOPS: dangerous for MILITARY & political situation in the island *(cf MD [Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I] to be appointed commander)*.<br>SULTAN wishes to appoint Pang. MANGKU DININGRAT [Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I] as commander in order to pave way for his accession.<br>**1792 — RATU MAAS *(Kedhaton?)*:** downgrades REGENT [Sultan Radja]'s Mother from 1st to second wife `∴` MD feels bitter about being robbed of position of CP [Crown Prince] `||`.<br>**150 BUFFALOES** to be rounded up for the Army + **100 cows** for army. | **5 September 1811 — Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>Yogyakarta troops departed at 7:00 p.m. on 4 September. The Regent cannot act without his father's permission. Mobilizing Yogyakarta troops poses military and political risks, as the Sultan wants to appoint **Pangéran Mangkudiningrat** as commander to pave his way to the throne—Mangkudiningrat having been embittered ever since Ratu Mas's 1792 demotion from first to second wife deprived him of the Crown Prince title.<br>**150 buffaloes** and **100 cows** are being rounded up for the army. |
| **Semarang, 11 Sept. — Janssens -> Engelhard:** *"Please keep my Secretary + Secretary of Sala [Surakarta] in Semarang."*<br>**11 Sept. — Engelhard -> Janssens:** REGENT [called Sultan Radja] dislikes removal of N.K. [Pangéran Natakusuma] / N.D. [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] to Semarang and commuting of death sentence. Cites reasons of proximity to TROOPS; fears treachery + English.<br>**12 Sept. — Engelhard -> Janssens:** Ideas (feelings) of Djogja court to go over to ENGLISH and get position assured: *"Although they seem to have no particular inclination in that direction at the moment."* | **Semarang, 11 Sept. — Janssens to Engelhard:** Requests keeping his Secretary and the Surakarta Secretary in Semarang.<br>**11 Sept. — Engelhard to Janssens:** The Regent dislikes moving Natakusuma and Natadiningrat to Semarang and commuting their death sentence, fearing proximity to troops and English treachery.<br>**12 Sept. — Engelhard to Janssens:** Notes court thoughts of going over to the English to secure their position, *"although they seem to have no particular inclination in that direction at the moment."* |
