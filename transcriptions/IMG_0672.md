### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bandit Alert at Telang**<br>28–29 Nov. 1810<br><br>*Kécu Threat* | Intelligence from Ngabèhi Kartadipura of Desa Telang:<br>• Warns that the village and surrounding timber establishments are about to be attacked by armed roving bandits (*kécus*) cooperating with rebel forces. |
| **Adimenggala to Grobogan**<br>c. 1 Dec. 1810<br>(Translation of Javanese letter)<br><br>*Demak Admonition* | Kyai Adipati Sura Adimenggala [bupati of Demak] to Radèn Tumênggung Sasrakusuma [bupati of Grobogan] and Radèn Tumênggung Sasranêgara [bupati of Kutha Arga]:<br>• Ordered to pursue and detain Rangga's couriers and emissaries carrying subversive letters from Yogyakarta.<br>• Expresses deep alarm that elements in Grobogan, Sasèla, and Wirasari are harboring hostile, predatory intentions against the colonial and regency territory of Demak.<br>• Instructs village headmen (*patinggi*) to maintain strict surveillance so no outrage is committed.<br>• Identifies the villages of **Gabus** and **Kaiyun** as notorious sanctuaries where bandit gangs (*kécus*) and rebel sympathizers hide out. |
| **Paulus to Engelhard**<br>5 Dec. 1810<br>(Dispatch No. 183)<br>Written at: Winong<br><br>*(Red underline:*<br>*Chinese Proclamations)* | Lieutenant Paulus [Lt. Paulus] (operating with Sultan's troops at Madiun) to Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng]:<br>• **Bilingual Rebel Decrees:** Intercepted captured royal orders issued by Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga], **written in both Javanese and Chinese**, specifically tailored to recruit Chinese communities and merchant guilds to rally to his rebel cause.<br>• **Inclement Weather & Flooding:** Heavy torrential rains have rendered the roads impassable swamps, greatly delaying Willem Nicolaas Servatius [W.N. Servatius, Secretary of Solo] and the Landdrost of Surabaya in reaching Surakarta.<br>• Paulus recommends extending the duration and perimeter of the expedition.<br>• **Defection of Sumanêgara:** Receives confirmed reports that Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara [bupati of Padangan], during Rangga's assault and capture of Magetan, broke away with 5 to 8 personal retainers, escaped from Rangga's camp, and took refuge with Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat II [bupati of Rajêgwêsi].<br>• Confirms that now Mangkunagaran regents possess Sultan HB II's [Hamengkubuwana II] direct orders, there is no longer any danger of eastern regents rallying to Rangga. |
