# Transcription of IMG_0494_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0494_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI, Djokja Brieven, No. 27: Archival Research Notes on Yogyakarta Letters & Kedu Security Orders (1809–1810))
- **Date Range:** 1809-05-31 to 1810-02-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Inquiries regarding the lost son of Surawisuna (Demang of Grogol); extracts of GG Daendels' register (8 & 13 Louwmaand 1810) barring recognition of Surakarta Patih Danuningrat [Mertawijaya] after armed Dalimaas raiders enter Batang/Pekalongan; Javanese order from Patih Danureja II to Resident Wiese (26 Jan. 1810 / 28 Besar Wawu 1737) sending a commission under Mas Tum. Yuda Atmaja, Bup. Ranudipura, and Singaranu to enforce peace, police *brandals*, and coordinate *bekels* and *lurahs* across Yogyakarta districts in Kedu.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Gustaf Wilhelm Wiese, Jacob Andries van Braam, Radèn Tumenggung Ranudipura, Mas Tumenggung Yuda Atmaja & Singaranu, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, W. Driessen
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Kedu, Yogya / Djokja, Surakarta / Sala, Pekalongan, Batang, Grogol, Batavia
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Louwmaand (January), Besar (Javanese twelfth month) / AJ 1737 (Wawu), Mantri / Bekel / Lurah, Kemisepuhan / Kami Seduh, Surungs / Tempurangs
