# Transcription of IMG_0525_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0525_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-05-20 to 1810-09-04
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 tracking the diplomatic maneuvers to establish a tripartite commission of inquiry into the Panaraga-Madiun border war between May and September 1810; opens with Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees delivering the Panaraga regents' report of 20 May 1810 to Radèn Adipati Danureja II for submission to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; records Moorrees briefing Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels in person at Semarang on 5 June, while noting that Radèn Rongga submitted numerous witness statements alleging prior Panaraga aggression against Madiun, leading Moorrees to conclude that interrogating Rongga without judicial coercion was futile; documents new Resident Pieter Engelhard on 1 September 1810 communicating with the Landdrost of Semarang/Demak regarding the commission (including Vendumeester Piepers and translator Krijgsman) and requesting HB II appoint two commissioners; records HB II appointing Pangéran Dipakusuma and Radèn Tumenggung Jayadipura, but insisting they proceed directly to Ngêbêl and Sikêdok rather than first meeting the Surakarta commissioners; tracks negotiations proposing Kartasura or Boyolali (*Bayalali*) as a neutral tripartite assembly point; records Willem Nicolaas Servatius reporting on 4 September that the Surakarta court cannot understand HB II's insistence on unilateral inspection in Madiun and reiterating Kartasura as the most suitable meeting place.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Pieter Engelhard, Landdrost of Semarang/Demak, Piepers (Vendumeester), Krijgsman (translator), Pangéran Dipakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Jayadipura, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Willem Nicolaas Servatius.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Panaraga (Ponorogo), Madiun, Semarang, Demak, Ngêbêl, Sikêdok (Nikêdok), Kartasura, Boyolali (Bayalali).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Commission of Inquiry (Tripartite commission), Judicial interrogation under constraint, Landdrost, Appointed commissioners, Diplomatic assembly location dispute.
