| `P. Mangkudiningrat`:<br>Since Deposition of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] becomes a great Favourite of the Sultan (`cf. Babad DN`). HB II passionately fond of this Prince — great favourite of all uncles / Brothers as well as with the people — Conduct of merit / and Descendant from Blood of Mataram. In event of any Disturbance would Have many Followers — supposedly instigates the Sultan to murder DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II]; active part in dissuading HB II from Treaty + Daendels. | **Pangéran Mangkudiningrat:** Since HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]'s deposition, became the Sultan's great favourite (cf. *Babad*) and popular among uncles, brothers, and commoners; descended from royal Mataram blood and capable of raising many followers; allegedly instigated HB II to murder Danureja II and dissuaded him from signing the treaty with Marshal Herman Willem Daendels. |
| `P. Mangkubumi`:<br>Son of Ratu Kedhaton, Full Brother of Prince Regent, always of the party of Regent. Lately feels Heavily Sultan's Indignation. A man of good sense enough. | **Pangéran Mangkubumi:** Son of Ratu Kedhaton and full brother of the Prince Regent [HB III]; steadfastly in the Regent's party, lately suffering HB II's indignation; a man of good sense. |
