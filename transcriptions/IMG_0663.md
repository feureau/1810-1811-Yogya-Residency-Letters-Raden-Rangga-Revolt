### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Pieter Engelhard** (`Eng / Engelhard`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 331). Resident of Yogyakarta (1808, 1810–1811) during the Daendels administration.
  * **Marshal Herman Willem Daendels** (`Daendels`): Listed under *Colonial Officials: Governors-General* (line 282). Governor-General of the Netherlands Indies (1808–1811).
  * **Jacob Andries van Braam** (`van Braam`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 307). Resident of Surakarta (1808–1810, 1811) and Government Commissioner for the Principalities.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 12). Second Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1792–1810, 1811–1812, 1826–1828).
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Pangéran Adipati Anom / Crown Prince)** (`CP`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 13). Reigned 1812–1814.
  * **Pangéran Natakusuma (Pakualam I)** (`NK`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 23). Brother of HB II suspected of colluding with Radèn Rongga; his residence (*dalem*) was placed under armed surveillance.
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III** (`R. Rangga / Rangga`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). *Bupati wedana* of Madiun.
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura** (`R.T. Purwadipura`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 190). Senior Yogya court official appointed to command the royal expedition against Radèn Rongga.
  * **Radèn Aria Sindunegara (Kyai Adipati Danureja III)** (`R. Aria Sindunegara`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 183). Acting *patih* of Yogyakarta (1811–1813).
  * **Major Tumenggung Wiranagara (Wiraguna II)** (`R.T. Wiranegara`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 187). Commander of the Sultan's bodyguard.
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Mertalaya** (`R.T. Mertalaya`): Listed under *Senior Officials (Priyayi)* (line 223). Outer *bupati* of Yogyakarta.
  * **Panji Suryèngrana** (`R. Panji Suryèngrana`): Listed under *Senior Officials (Priyayi)* (lines 218, 270). Commander (*wedana*) of the royal palace guard (*prajurit kraton*).
  * **Radèn Ngabèhi Jayasentika** (`R. Ngabèhi Jayasentika`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 192, 196). *Patih* of the *Kadipatèn* (Crown Prince's court).
  * **Mas Tumenggung Kertadirja** (`Mas Tum. Kertadirja`): Listed under *Patih of the Kadipatèn / Senior Officials* (lines 196, 236). *Tumenggung Tampung* (field officer/liaison).
  * **Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II)** (`MN`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 27). Commander of the Mangkunagaran auxiliary legion whose eastern mancanagara bupatis joined the royal expedition.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Yogyakarta Kraton** (`KRATON`): The walled palace compound of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II.
  * **Kadipatèn**: Crown Prince's court complex in Yogyakarta.
  * **Klathèn** (`Klaten`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 423). Garrison and post station between Yogyakarta and Surakarta where Lt. Paulus was stationed.
  * **Madiun**: Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 401). Eastern mancanagara capital toward which the expedition advanced.
* **Terms:**
  * **bupati**: Provincial governor / administrative chief.
  * **wedana**: High officer or battalion commander (*wedana prajurit*).
  * **prajurit**: Royal troops / infantry regiments.
  * **dalem**: Walled princely residence / compound.
  * **wachtmeester**: Cavalry sergeant / non-commissioned officer in the colonial forces.
  * **dragoons**: Mounted cavalry soldiers attached to the residency escort.
