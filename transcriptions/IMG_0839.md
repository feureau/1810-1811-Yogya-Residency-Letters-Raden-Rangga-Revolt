Suryabrangta` — d. [daughter] of DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] + Ratu Anggèr (`R. Aj. Kapilah`).<br>• HB III's d. `R. Aj. Sumiah` + son of Reg. [Regent] of Madiun (`(P. Dipakusuma) R. Sumiah to Radèn Prawiraatmaja (son of the Regent of Madiun, Pangéran Dipakusuma); and HB III's daughter R.Aj. Saminah to R.T. Mangundirana (Regent of Kalangbret). |
| `Dimang of Karang Bolong to Col. [Colonel] Adams` (`n.d. ? 1813, p. 895`):<br>Reporting arrival of a Brig. `30 Nov. 1812 2 p.m.` Brig appears from West, passes to and fro in front of Bird's Nests caves. Runs up Red Flag, tries to set sailors ashore in sloop but surf too strong. Sails again to West. | **Demang of Karangbolong to Colonel Alexander Adams (n.d., reporting 30 November 1812, p. 895):** Reports a brig appearing from the west at 2 p.m., cruising off the birds'-nest caves, hoisting a red flag, attempting to land a sloop prevented by heavy surf, and sailing back west. |
| `Report on Yogya` (`n.d., p. 901`):<br>Rumours in Yogya that Paku Alam [Pangéran Natakusuma] aiming to lay waste Sultan's territory; many Yogyanese flee to Desas *(see Babad Bedhah)*. Also that French/Madurese troops to come back + old Sultan [HB II]. Rumour that P.A. [Paku Alam] would attack British during last Garebeg ceremony, but has to abandon idea because *prajurits* run away. Also idea that HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] would arrive + Mangkudiningrat + Madurese from Semarang + French Troops from Cilacap. HB III puts troops on alert = `1000` in Kraton. Reason HB III gives no lodgings in Kraton to `R.T. Pringgakusuma`, `R. Panji Sasra-atmaja`, `R. Ng. Sanapura`: because of fear of arrival of HB II + Mangkudiningrat. | **Report on Yogyakarta (n.d., p. 901):** Rumours in Yogyakarta that Paku Alam intended to ravage the Sultan's territory (causing many to flee to villages; cf. *Babad Bedhah*) and attack the British during the last Garebeg, though abandoned when *prajurits* deserted; also rumours of HB II and Mangkudiningrat returning with Madurese troops from Semarang and French troops from Cilacap. HB III placed `1,000` troops on alert in the Kraton and withheld Kraton lodgings from R.T. Pringgakusuma [Pringgadiningrat], R. Panji Sasra-atmaja, and R. Ng. Sanapura out of fear of HB II and Mangkudiningrat's arrival. |
