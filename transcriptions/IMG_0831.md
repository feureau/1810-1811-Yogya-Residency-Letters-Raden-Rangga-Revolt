| `LXXI Raffles -> Crawfurd Semarang 6 Dec 1811` (`M.L. van Deventer pp 309-311`):<br>Raffles wishes to communicate `PRINCIPAL POINTS of TREATY` which He wishes to make + SULTAN's court.<br>`PRINCIPLE of V.O.C. [Vereenigde Oostindische Compagnie] CONTRACT 1792` *(see Opkomst XII pp 250-256)*:<br>(1) `BIRDS NESTS / Tollgates / Duties + other FARMS` to be collected by the GOVNMT. [Government] to pay for the expenses of the `MILITARY / CIVILIAN est. [establishment] at COURT`. | **6 December 1811 — Thomas Stamford Raffles (Semarang) to John Crawfurd** (`van Deventer`, pp. 309–311): Outlines the principal points of the intended treaty with Yogyakarta based on the 1792 V.O.C. Contract: birds' nests, tollgates, duties, and other farms to be collected by the Government to defray the military and civil establishment at court. |
| `3 REQUESTS of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] / Regent:`<br>(1) `"Strandgelden"` payments for N.E. [North-East] coast — ended by Daendels — But Impossible to allow this to save face of Govnmt. — Could be allowed payment in any other way.<br>(2) Request for Burying Places of Ancestors of N.E. coast — To Be Refused — attempt to get control of `" "` To Be Disallowed —<br>`SEEMS SULTAN / SUNAN encouraged By some INTRIGUE to win Back N.E. coast of which Brit. [British] Govnmt not yet fully aware -` | **Three Requests of HB II & Prince Regent:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1. *Strandgelden* (coastal money) ended by Marshal Herman Willem Daendels: cannot be restored as such to save Government face, though equivalent payment might be arranged otherwise.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2. Ancestral burial places on the North-East coast: to be refused, as Raffles suspects an intrigue encouraging the Sultan and Sunan to reclaim the N.E. coast. |
| (3) `RETURN of Exiled Relations NK / ND [Natakusuma / Natadiningrat]:`<br>Follows Crawfurd's OPINION as Regards PRINCES: could be made `CONFIDENTIAL / TRUSTWORTHY Instruments of BRIT. POLICY should services Be Necessary.`<br>(4) `OPIUM` = Exclusive PRIVILEGE to Retail OPIUM -> Chinese on a Monthly Rent BASIS — each CHEST `1,200 Sp.D.` [Spanish Dollars]. Only one FARMER — Rents to be paid / received at SURAKARTA. | &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3. Return of exiled relatives Pangéran Natakusuma and Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat: Raffles agrees with Crawfurd that they could become confidential, trustworthy instruments of British policy.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4. **Opium:** Exclusive retail privilege to Chinese on a monthly rent basis (`1,200` Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars] per chest), with a single farmer and rents paid at Surakarta. *(Continued on [IMG_0832.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0832.md).)* |
