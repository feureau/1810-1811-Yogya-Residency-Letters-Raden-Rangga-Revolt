| **3** | During the period of **Daendels' [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels]** administration in Indonesia (1808–11) from about 1810 onwards the Dutch names of months from the agricultural calendar:<br><br>* **Louwmaand** (Jan.)<br>* **Sprokkelmaand** (Feb.)<br>* **Lentenmaand** (Mar.)<br>* **Grasmaand** (Apr.)<br>* **Bloeimaand** (May)<br>* **Zomermaand** (Jun.)<br>* **Hooimaand** (Jul.)<br>* **Oogstmaand** (Aug.)<br>* **Herftsmaand** (Sept.)<br>* **Wijnmaand** (Oct.)<br>* **Slachtmaand** (Nov.)<br>* **Wintermaand** (Dec.)<br><br>were used in official correspondence.<br><br>The use of the new calendar in Holland dated from the period of the Batavian Republic (1795–1805) and was probably inspired by the new French Revolutionary calendar introduced in Oct. 1793.<br><br>Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels], as an erstwhile member of the Dutch 'Patriot Party' and admirer of the Jacobins, probably introduced it for political reasons but its use was terminated by Daendels' successor **J.W. Janssens [Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens]** (May – Sept. 1811) in May 1811. |
