# Transcription of IMG_0528_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0528_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-04-12 to 1810-07-28 (Javanese: 26 Sapar Wawu 1737 [12 April 1810] to 28 Hooimaand [July] 1810; Dutch revolutionary/standard calendar months: Grasmaand [April], Bloeimaand [May], Zomermaand [June], Hooimaand [July])  
**Key Events:**
* Murder of a Demang in Grobogan territory reported on 28 Hooimaand [July] 1810 via Javanese correspondence between Tumenggung Sasranêgara (Bupati of Grobogan) and Tumenggung Sasradiningrat at Rajêkwêsi.
* Murder of a Chinese toll keeper / merchant (*bandhar*) at Pasar Colook in Wirasari reported on 26 Sapar Wawu 1737 [12 April 1810] by Javanese official Mangkudiwiria from Ngrandog.
* Gamping tollgate conflict and Tan Jin Sing [Kapitan Cina] petition (22 Bloeimaand [May] 1810): Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels sharply reprimands Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees for signing and condoning an irregular order regarding tolls. Lurah Priyayi, a retainer of Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura and Radèn Aria Sindurêja managing the Gamping tollgate, required Chinese traders to pay tolls of 4 dubbeltjes per *pikulan* of batik cloths; Tan Jin Sing appealed to Moorrees to issue an exemption order.
* Imperial agricultural requisition (20 Zomermaand [June] 1810): Daendels issues a decree ordering the courts of Yogyakarta and Surakarta to supply 20 Chinese families to serve as tobacco planters.
* Desertion and runaway slave sanctuary in Japan [Mojokerto] (20 Zomermaand [June] 1810): Captain Cornelis Coon at Pasuruan alerts the colonial government that the district of Japan has become a primary gathering sanctuary for escaped domestic slaves and military deserters, protected by the young Regent of Japan. Daendels instructs Resident Moorrees to demand that Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III—as Senior *Bupati Wedana* of the Eastern Mancanagara (*Head of MN heads*)—along with Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] and Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II], mount an immediate investigation.
