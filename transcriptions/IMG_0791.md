| **ROBINSON / 26 Sept. 1811** *(red)*<br>`2. Eur. F. 148/17` | Continuation of Captain William Robison's report (`Eur. F. 148/17`, p. 2). |
| Espc. [Especially] Request expulsion of van Braam + Sec. [Secretary] Servatius. *(right margin in pencil: `Rob Req. for upright Eng. [English] Res. [Resident]`)*<br>Little Forms / ceremonies even observed by Daendels discontinued by Pr. [Present] govt. [government].<br>van B [van Braam] Treats PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] as an Equal. | Especially requesting the expulsion of Resident Jacob Andries van Braam and Secretary Willem Nicolaas Servatius (Robison recommending an upright English Resident). Even the few forms and ceremonies observed by Marshal Herman Willem Daendels had been discontinued by the present government, and van Braam treated Sunan Pakubuwana IV as an equal. |
| Rob [Robison] - tells PB IV says not qualified to negotiate limits<br>Requests PB IV to put on paper to forward to Minto *(pencil right: `ask PB IV to commits`)* | Robison told Sunan Pakubuwana IV he was not authorized to negotiate terms or limits, and asked the Sunan to put his requests on paper to forward to Lord Minto. |
| Easy matter to maintain a good understanding + PB IV<br>"He is well-disposed and Tractable"<br>Receives Information / Registers about Residencies from Eng [Pieter Engelhard] / Braam. | Robison remarked it would be an easy matter to maintain a good understanding with Sunan Pakubuwana IV: *"He is well-disposed and Tractable."* Robison received information and registers about the Residencies from Pieter Engelhard and van Braam. |
| Leaves Sala 24 -> Yogya.<br>Road not passable for a carriage:<br>goes on Horseback + in a Palanquin (a *Joli*)<br>Easily passable in one day | Departed Solo [Surakarta] on **24 September** for Yogyakarta. The road was impassable for a carriage, so he travelled on horseback and in a palanquin (a *joli*), easily completing the journey in one day. |
| Fort at Klatèn new work on a fine elevated plain<br>Its situation is a post of importance.<br>One company of Infantry to garrison it. | **Fort at Klathèn:** A new fortification on a fine elevated plain, situated as a post of importance requiring one company of infantry to garrison it. |
| Country much infested by Roving Banditti since Janssens Retreat *(red margin: `'wong durjana' operating on orders of HB II`)*<br>Especially makes it dangerous for small Parties / Posts to travel.<br>`∴` Posts must be garrisoned to maintain communication. | The countryside was much infested by roving banditti (*wong durjana*, noted in red as operating on Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's orders) since Governor-General Jan Willem Janssens' retreat, making travel dangerous for small parties and requiring garrisoned posts to maintain communication. |
| Prambanan - Hindu Temples falling to pieces | Noted Prambanan — Hindu temples falling to pieces. |
| `25 Sept.` Audience + Sultan / Regent.<br>Gives letter from Auchmutty. | **25 September 1811:** Audience with the Sultan [HB II] and Prince Regent [HB III], delivering a letter from Commander-in-Chief Sir Samuel Auchmuty. |
