| Saturday 21 Sept.: Meeting at 7 o'clock civil servants (`Drie Armen` - J. Holzman, Semarang; Andries Homoor - Semarang). Put into requisition to accompany Brit. [British] Comm. [Commissioner] Wm. [William] Robison to Solo + **Krijgsman** as translator of Javanese. | On Saturday, 21 September 1811, at 7 o'clock, civil servants (`Drie Armen` — J. Holzman and Andries Homoor of Semarang) were requisitioned to accompany British Commissioner William Robison to Solo [Surakarta], with C. Krijgsman serving as Javanese translator. |
| Arrive Solo 22nd Sept. House of Mr. van Braam; later 11 o'clock Kraton of Sunan + van Braam + Sec. [Secretary] Servatius, met by Emperor in *Pendopo*; Robison hands over letter to Sunan -> opens it + reads (Javanese). | They arrived at Solo [Surakarta] on 22 September at the house of Resident Jacob Andries van Braam. At 11 o'clock, they proceeded to the Sunan's Kraton with van Braam and Secretary Willem Nicolaas Servatius, where they were met by the Emperor in the *Pendopo*. Robison handed a letter to the Sunan. |
| Late evening: Sunan comes to House of van Braam unannounced accompanied by **Boeminata** to the Princes. Robison requests to speak to Emperor alone `∴` ascends room in N. [North] corner of the house; Sunan takes Crown Prince (*Pang. [Pangéran] Adipati Anom Amangkunegoro*), *Pang. [Pangéran] Aria Boeminoto* (younger brother to Sunan), *Senoor Tjakra Kusumo*, + P.M. [Prime Minister] Raden Adipati **Tjakra Negara**. Shuts windows/doors + takes seats; Robison insists on secrecy. | Late in the evening, the Sunan came unannounced to van Braam's house accompanied by Pangéran Aria Buminata and the princes. Robison requested a private audience with the Emperor, ascending to a room in the north corner of the house. The Sunan brought the Crown Prince (*Pangéran Adipati Anom Amangkunegara*), *Pangéran Aria Buminata* (his younger brother), *Senoor Cakrakusuma*, and Prime Minister Radèn Adipati Cakranegara. Windows and doors were shut, and Robison insisted on strict secrecy. |
| Robison says sent direct from Minto in Bengal -> Emperor to present compliments; Minto British Gov. [Government] Proclaims/assures Brit. [British] Conquest. *Tjakra Negara, Amangkunegara, Boeminata, Tjakra Kusuma* all transactions. Robison asks if Emperor has anything on his mind he wished to say. | Robison stated he was sent directly by Lord Minto in Bengal to present compliments to the Emperor and proclaim the British conquest. Cakranegara, Amangkunegara, Buminata, and Cakrakusuma acted as witnesses. Robison then asked if the Emperor had anything on his mind he wished to share. |
