### Physical Artifact Description

   * Heavy cream wove paper folio serving as a protective mounting folder / divider leaf for the contemporary ANRI manuscript documents that follow.
   * Modern chronological filing date penned at upper right in blue ballpoint ink: `21 Nov. 1810` (underlined).
   * Right center margin bears a faint vertical blue institutional ink stamp: `ARSIP NASIONAL REPUBLIK INDONESIA`.
   * Leaf surface is unruled, clean, and blank across the main field, exhibiting minor handling patina and vertical paper flexing.

2. **Underlying Archival Document (Left Binding Edge):**
   * Contemporary 19th-century Dutch colonial manuscript leaf protruding ~1.5 to 2 cm past the left margin of the cover leaf.
   * Upper left corner inscribed in ink: `21 Nov. 1810`.
   * Official Dutch translation script visible along the exposed margin: `...staat dieze onder Bevel van my Sultthan...` (opening passage of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's royal order outlawing Radèn Rongga).
   * Two filing punch holes fitted with brass reinforcing grommets/eyelets.
   * Severe ferrous oxidation / dark brown staple rust halo at the lower left corner.
