### Main Text & Margin Notes

| Margin Notes | Main Text |
| :--- | :--- |
| **13 Louwmaand [13 Jan. 1810]**<br>`Wiese to Daendels` | Letter of Daendels &rarr; HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II].<br>Decision of HB II to capture chiefs implicated in attack on Pekalongan.<br>Would deliver them up to Daendels:<br>would dispatch them to Prefect Goldbach at Semarang.<br>Commission need 8–9 days to travel over the poor roads: + [and] take the prisoners into custody.<br>In case of difficulties Daendels should be prepared to send in the military. |
| **23 Louwmaand [23 Jan. 1810]**<br>`Wiese to Prefect of Pekalongan. Pinket van Haak.` | Band of Robbers in Desa of Wanakrama. |
| **10 Sprokkelmaand 1810 [10 Feb. 1810]**<br>*(van der Chys, Plakaatboek, Vol. XVI, pp. 73–76)* | **DEBTS OF PRANGWEDANA**<br>*(Red vertical margin line)*<br>==<u>PWD [Prangwedana / Mangkunagara II] apart from debts contracted by MNI [Mangkunagara I] before 1795 forced to borrow heavily from money lenders.</u>==<br>Lend money at very high rates of interest – PWD no prospect of paying money back.<br>Money lenders all govrmt. [government] subjects – who ignore rule that no higher rent than 9% p.a. [per annum].<br>Since arrival of Daendels in Java – PWD always shows loyalty.<br><br>*(Red vertical margin line)*<br>Orders all creditors to give account of capital and interest owed by PWD to Res. [Resident] of Sala [Surakarta] within one month.<br>All claims not made in 1 month to be annulled.<br>No higher interest to be claimed for debts than 6% p.a.<br>*(see Besluiten 31 Jan. 1809 / 20 Jan. 1810)*<br><br>*(Red underline and double red stroke)*:<br>==<u>Sum of 39,800 Sp.D. [Spanish Dollars] in silver recently paid into govrmt. treasury from executors of late van Reede to be used to cover PWD's debts –</u>==<br>New letters of credit on PWD to be given to his creditors at 6% p.a. |
