| AN *'Djokjo Brieven' no. 24* — <span style="color:#a00">**Yogya 1 Sept. 1811**</span><br>**Eng - Janssens [Pieter Engelhard to Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens], 1 Sept.:**<br>Eng hears from von Winckelmann: Fall of Meester Cornelis; Dutch Retreat to Bogor.<br>27 Aug. Proclamation of Janssens — retreating + remainder of army to Semarang (at *Tjianjour* [Cianjur]): Govnmt to be moved to Semarang.<br>Tells Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] that Eng. [English] might too great for Dutch to hold Batavia [Batavia (Jakarta)].<br>Tells Regent that counts on full military help from Kraton. Regent does not know what to answer.<br>Regent thanks Eng for news -> says wishes to remain on good terms + govnmt.<br>I could not tell whether the news greatly affected Regent or DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II].<br>DR II asks if French Reinforcements would come; if *"Groote Heeren"* at Batavia would go to Semarang.<br>Troops ready to march to Batavia / Semarang; drill on square behind the Fort.<br>2 Sept.: Sec. *Geselschap* to be sent to Semarang to pay compliments to Janssens. | **Pieter Engelhard to Governor-General Janssens, 1 September 1811:**<br>Engelhard learned from von Winckelmann of Meester Cornelis's fall and the Dutch retreat to Bogor, along with Janssens's 27 August proclamation (issued at Cianjur) moving the seat of government and remaining army to Semarang.<br>Engelhard informed the Prince Regent that English strength had forced the Dutch to yield Batavia and counted on full military aid from the Kraton. The Regent was at a loss for an answer, thanking Engelhard and expressing a wish to remain on good terms with the Government. Engelhard could not gauge how deeply the news affected the Regent or Danureja II; Danureja II asked whether French reinforcements would arrive and whether the *"Groote Heeren"* [high officials] from Batavia would relocate to Semarang.<br>Troops ready to march drilled on the square behind the Fort. On 2 September, Secretary *Geselschap* will be sent to Semarang to pay compliments to Janssens. |
