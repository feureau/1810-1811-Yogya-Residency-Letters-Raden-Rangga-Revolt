## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Raffles to Minto, 31 Jan. 1811 (Malacca)` (`Folio p. 76`):<br>HB II orders subordinates to stop approach of Daendels if he comes in his official capacity with the troops before him, it being usual for G.G. [Governor-General] on visits to courts to be preceded by `1000` men.<br>+ if Marshal comes just not preceded by usual retinue to be allowed to pass.<br>Daendels: van Braam sent to HB II 3x -> failure; eventually shoots himself!!<br>Obtains `200,000 Sp.D.` [Spanish Dollars] from HB II — But no confirmation of this. | **31 January 1811 — Thomas Stamford Raffles (Malacca) to Lord Minto (`IOL Raffles–Minto Coll. Vol. 4`, ff. 76, 79–81):** Reports that Sultan Hamengkubuwana II ordered subordinates to halt Marshal Herman Willem Daendels if he approached with his usual 1,000-man escort; notes Jacob Andries van Braam was sent three times to HB II without success (rumoured to have shot himself, and Daendels allegedly obtained `200,000` Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars] from HB II — unconfirmed). |
| `Proofs of general disaffection of the Javanese:` (`f. p. 79`):<br>Meets `Tunku Pangeran of Siak` in Malacca: *"a man out of the common line"*; also meets + a `Haji from Mataram`, formerly employed by Sunan.<br>`23 Jan. 1811`: Tunku Pangeran + Mataram Haji dispatched from Malacca. Tunku goes + Mr Scott on `Arethusa` cutter; others go on country vessels.<br>`f. p. 80`: Letters to Sunan / Sultan carried by Haji from Mataram to be landed at Semarang by Tunku Pangeran or carried with him to Banten.<br>Those to Cirebon Rajas given to relatives banished by Daendels. Idea of Těngku Pangeran landing in Banten + proceeding in person to Sala / Yogya.<br>`p. 81`: The agent being a prince and a Sayid from his former habits well acquainted with every part of Java. | **Agents Dispatched (23 January 1811):** Raffles meets **Těngku Pangéran of Siak (Sayid Zin)** (*"a man out of the common line"*) and a **Haji from Mataram** (formerly employed by the Sunan) in Malacca. Both are dispatched on 23 January 1811 (Těngku Pangéran with Mr. Scott aboard the *Arethusa* cutter) carrying letters to the Sunan, the Sultan, and the Cirebon Rajas, intending to land at Semarang or Banten and proceed to Solo and Yogyakarta. *(Continued on [IMG_0864.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0864.md).)* |
