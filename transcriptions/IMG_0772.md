| *AN Djokja Brieven no. 24* — **1811 <span style="color:#a00">(Oct.)</span>**<br>**31 Oct. — Eng -> Raffles [Pieter Engelhard to Thomas Stamford Raffles]:**<br>This afternoon **Dipakusuma** [Pangéran Dipakusuma] comes from HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] to say that DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] dismissed from office because of his many faults + DK [Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I] 1st outer Regent dismissed.<br>**Tum. Sindunagara** [Kyai Adipati Danureja III] appointed ad interim, but because of his advanced age would fulfill the post with difficulty; because of sickness unable to fulfill post of 1st outer Regent for some months.<br>Letter of HB II to Eng. + Comm. of Fort (**Driessen** [Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen]); but refusal of Eng. to inform Comm. about Ministerial affairs in Yogya. Tells DK [Dipakusuma] that Eng. still invested + full power by BRITISH GOVNMT. *(`?` HB II thinks Eng. had been dismissed)*.<br>`∴` after an hour DK -> Kraton, comes back + letter addressed to Engelhard from HB II *(only sent 11 Nov. 1811?)* included *(cf Trans. letter from HB II -> Eng)*. | **31 October 1811 — Pieter Engelhard to Raffles:**<br>That afternoon Pangéran Dipakusuma arrived from Hamengkubuwana II to report the dismissal of Danureja II and First Outer Regent Danukusuma, and the interim appointment of Tumenggung Sindunagara (elderly and ill). Hamengkubuwana II also wrote to Fort Commander Driessen, whom Engelhard refused to brief on ministerial affairs, reminding Dipakusuma that Engelhard still held full British authority (contrary to HB II's apparent belief that he had been dismissed). Dipakusuma returned an hour later with HB II's letter to Engelhard. |
| **HB II's action contravenes 2 articles of 1792 Treaty:**<br>• **Art. 4:** HB II shall appoint or dismiss no one as *Rijksbestierder* without first informing the Gov. in Semarang, who will seek approval of G.G. in Batavia; must give full reasons first to Gov. in Semarang; Holl. [Holland] + Javanese inhabitants must act as one people together.<br>• **Art. 14:** But in case Patih who swears oath of allegiance to V.O.C. + HB II Sultan — guilty of some fault against V.O.C. + subjects or Sultan + subjects, the affair will be investigated by non-partisan persons from side of V.O.C. + Sultanate, but no decision to be taken without approval of G.G. + Council of Indies who had perused all pieces appertaining to guilt of S.S. [Susuhunan/Sultan] hist. | **Contravention of Articles 4 and 14 of the 1792 Treaty:**<br>• **Art. 4:** The Sultan may neither appoint nor dismiss a *Rijksbestierder* [Patih] without first informing the Governor at Semarang and obtaining Batavia's approval.<br>• **Art. 14:** Any alleged offense by the Patih must be jointly investigated by impartial Dutch and Sultanate commissioners, with no verdict executed without the Governor-General and Council of the Indies' approval. |
