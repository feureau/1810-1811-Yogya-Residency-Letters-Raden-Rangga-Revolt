### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Dipakusuma's Advice**<br>14 Nov. 1810<br>(Slagtmaand)<br><br>*Overland Route* | Pangéran Dipakusuma [Pangéran Dipakusuma / Radèn Dipaatmaja] provides detailed travel advice to the Kraton regarding the overland route to Batavia [Jakarta]:<br>• Dipakusuma recalls his own overland expedition to Batavia in 1780, advising on stages, water crossings, and security precautions necessary for escorting Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga].<br>• HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] orders the royal Kraton gamelan to play publicly in the palace yards to signal to the populace that the dangerous crisis and dispute with Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] have been successfully and peacefully resolved. |
| **Engelhard to van Braam**<br>14–15 Nov. 1810<br>(Yogyakarta)<br><br>*Commission Ended* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng] to Commissioner Jacob Andries van Braam [J.A. van Braam] (14–15 Nov. 1810):<br>• Van Braam expresses great satisfaction that affairs with the Sultan have settled so favorably without recourse to arms.<br>• Van Braam's special commissionership regarding Rangga and the eastern mancanagara will not be re-appointed, as ordinary residency channels are deemed sufficient. |
| **Engelhard to Daendels**<br>15 Nov. 1810<br>(Slagtmaand)<br><br>*2,000 Escorts* | Resident Engelhard to Marshal Daendels (15 Nov. 1810):<br>• Transmits Sultan HB II's formal written replies of 12 and 13 November 1810.<br>• Confirms that Rangga's departure date is definitively fixed for **Monday, 26 November 1810**.<br>• A massive royal escort of 2,000 troops and retainers under high-ranking regents will accompany Rangga on his journey to Buitenzorg [Bogor]. |
| **Engelhard to Winckelmann**<br>20 Nov. 1810<br>(to Semarang)<br><br>*"Nulla salus bello..."* | Resident Engelhard to Brigadier F.C.P. von Winckelmann (Semarang, 20 Nov. 1810):<br>• Engelhard expresses profound relief that war has been averted, citing the classical maxim: *"Nulla salus bello pax optima rerum"* [No safety in war; peace is the best of things].<br>• Notes that HB II became "quite another person" upon receiving Daendels's conciliatory dispatch, showing complete compliance.<br>• Brigadier von Winckelmann and Lieutenant Paulus [Lt. Paulus] coordinate military logistics and European dragoon escorts to receive Rangga at the border. |
