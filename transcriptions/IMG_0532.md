# Transcription of IMG_0532_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0532_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-07-06 to 1810-07-10 (Javanese: Sat. 6 Jumadilakir Wawu 1737 [7 July 1810] to 9 Hooimaand / 7 Jumadilakir 1810; Carey's bracketed date: `[6 July – 10 July 1810]`)  
**Key Events:**
* Impasse on Ceremonial Protocol (6 Hooimaand [July] 1810): Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees reports to Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels that a further conference with Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III and Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] yielded no prospect that Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] could be dissuaded from his resistance regarding the throne footstool.
* Sumadiningrat's Diplomatic Role: Moorrees approaches Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat (First Inner Regent / *Bupati Nayaka Jero*), noting that both Rongga and Sumadiningrat enjoy supreme royal favor and are best positioned to dispel the Sultan's suspicions. Moorrees urges Sumadiningrat to present Daendels' purportedly benevolent motives toward the Sultan's person and state (*weldadige oogmerken van Z.E. ten zijnen personeele regarde en van het Rijk*). Sumadiningrat cautiously agrees only to break the news gently, insisting that HB II must be shielded from royal dishonor and public humiliation (*protected from shame*).
* Direct Intercession of Crown Prince (7 Hooimaand [7 July 1810] / Sat. 6 Jumadilakir Wawu 1737): Moorrees directs DR II to approach HB II through the Crown Prince (CP [later Sultan Hamengkubuwana III]) to obtain royal sanction for Radèn Rongga's immediate dispatch to Japan [Mojokerto] to suppress military deserters, issuing written orders to accelerate the process.
* Royal Evasion and Samas Excursion (9 Hooimaand [9 July 1810] / 7 Jumadilakir 1810): DR II reports that while he communicated Moorrees' instructions, HB II expressly forbade Sumadiningrat from visiting the Residency or meddling in external political affairs, stating it was outside his jurisdiction. Furthermore, HB II had departed Yogyakarta for his southern seaside pleasure retreat at Samas, stipulating that Moorrees could only be granted an audience after the Sultan's return.
