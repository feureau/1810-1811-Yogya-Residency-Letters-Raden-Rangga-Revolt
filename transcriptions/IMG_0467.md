### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II)** (`Prang-Wedana / PWD`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 27). Reigned 1796–1835. Created the Mangkunagaran Legion under Daendels and allied with the Franco-Dutch and later the British.
  * **Radèn Mas Said (Mangkunagara I)** (`MN I`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 26). Founder of the Mangkunagaran princely house; grandfather of Prangwedana.
  * **Marshal Herman Willem Daendels** (`King of Holland / govnmt.`): Listed under *Colonial Officials: Governors-General* (line 282). Governor-General of the Netherlands Indies (1808–1811) who organized native auxiliary legions and surveyed Java's road network.
  * **E.G. Vodegel** (`1st Lt. of Engineers = E.G. Vodegel`): Military engineer officer tasked with surveying Central Java roads for the official mapping of Java.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Surakarta** (`Solo`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 393). Capital of the Sunanate.
  * **Yogyakarta** (`Yogya`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 392). Capital of the Sultanate.
  * **Boyolali** (`Bayalali`): Key post station and staging ground on the highway between Semarang and Surakarta.
  * **Klathèn** (`Klaten`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 423). Midway town between Yogya and Surakarta.
  * **Jambu**: Mountain pass station south of Ambarawa on the highway connecting Semarang and Yogyakarta.
  * **Semarang**: Listed under *Colonial Officials / Places* (lines 210, 296). North coast administrative center and military headquarters.
  * **Solo River (Bengawan Sala)** (`Solo River`): Listed under *Geographical Terms* (lines 409, 507). Major inland waterway across Central and East Java.
* **Terms:**
  * **strandgeld**: Listed under *Administrative & Financial Terms* (line 564). Dutch compensation money paid annually to the courts of Surakarta and Yogyakarta for colonial control of the north coast (*pasisir*).
  * **Spanish dollar (Real batu / SpD)** (`Sp.D.`): Listed under *Currencies* (line 674). Standard international silver trade currency valued at Dfl 2.56.
  * **Rijksdaalder (Rix dollar)** (`Rijksdaalders`): Listed under *Currencies* (line 676). Standard Dutch silver currency / accounting unit valued at Dfl 2.40.
  * **pikul**: Listed under *Weights & Measures* (line 484). Unit of weight equal to approximately 61.76 kg (125 Dutch pounds or 100 catties).
  * **kapas**: Raw unspun cotton, subject to internal toll and export excise duties under colonial lease contracts (*Javansche Verpachtingen*).
