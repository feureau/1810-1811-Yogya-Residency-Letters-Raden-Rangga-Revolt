| :--- | :--- |
| `p. 89`: Javanese princes not to dispute + Dutch before arrival of British. But date for expedition not yet fixed. | **Page 89:** Advises Javanese princes not to clash prematurely with the Dutch before the British expedition arrives (date not yet fixed). |
| `p. 91` *(copy in Raffles-Minto Coll. F. 148/4 pp. 93-4 no. 8)*: `Letter to PB IV of Sala from Raffles`:<br>"Emperor of the whole country of Java as well inland as on the seashore who is the chief Rajah of the East(ern) part of Java."<br>Refers to Ld. [Lord] Minto's annoyance at destruction of Rights / customs of Sunan by Dutch. British would not act like Dutch had done. Main interest in establishing a good understanding with PB IV.<br>After receipt of letter PB IV not to enter into any agreements + Dutch. Just to await in anxious expectation arrival of British. Utmost secrecy to be observed. No presents sent but later on meeting + PB IV all presents required would be given *(see Robinson's report)*.<br>`Letter to Sultan of Mataram + Sultan of Madura` nearly same. | **Letter from Thomas Stamford Raffles to Sunan Pakubuwana IV (p. 91, copy in `Eur. F. 148/4`, pp. 93–94, No. 8):** Addresses PB IV as *"Emperor of the whole country of Java"*, notes Lord Minto's displeasure at Dutch violations of the Sunan's rights and customs, urges PB IV to make no agreements with the Dutch and observe utmost secrecy until the British arrive (presents to follow upon meeting). Nearly identical letters sent to the Sultan of Mataram [HB II] and the Sultan of Madura. |
| `pp. 91-97`: `Letter to Raja of Bali Buleleng` (`Letter to Ld. Minto 18 Feb. 1811`):<br>Assurance of friendship from Bali Rajahs received by Raffles' emissary *(p. 95)*. | **Pages 91–97:** Letter to the Raja of Bali Buleleng and dispatch to Lord Minto (**18 February 1811**) reporting assurances of friendship received from Bali rulers by Raffles' emissary (p. 95). |
