### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Battle of Sekaran**<br>17 Dec. 1810<br><br>*(Red circle: 100 + 12)* | Lucas Leberfeld to Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] (17 Dec. 1810):<br>• Rangga attempts to break out of encirclement with his main body.<br>• Leberfeld envelops the rebel flank with his right wing: Intense exchange of rifle fire ensues.<br>• **Rebel Battle Strength:** Rangga's fighting force numbers **100 Javanese retainers plus 12 Chinese riflemen**.<br>• Upon the opening volley, Rangga's rank-and-file panic and flee in all directions.<br>• Only Rangga, Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara, his *patih*, and three servants (*baturs*) bearing the royal parasols (*payungs*) remain standing.<br>• Hemmed in by Leberfeld's troops, the royal regents call upon Rangga to state his demands.<br>• Rangga replies that he intends no harm to ordinary Javanese, but seeks only to kill all those who burden the people and the Chinese in Mancanagara [MN] lands.<br>• Rangga dismounts from his horse and attempts a desperate pike thrust; Sumanêgara's [? weapon] wounds one of Leberfeld's men.<br>• **Death of the Rebel Chiefs:** Both Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III and Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara [SN] are slain in the melee.<br>• Jipang regents arrive upon the battlefield to wash and respectfully bind the corpses. |
| **Leberfeld's Relaas**<br>17 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Eyewitness Deposition* | **Seven-Point Deposition of Lucas Leberfeld:**<br>**1.** Rangga had evacuated Madiun four days prior when Leberfeld was dispatched in pursuit by Lt. Paulus, commanding a column comprising regents Kèrtalaya, Wirunêgara, Yudakusuma, former Tumênggung Sumadiwiria, and 150 infantrymen.<br>**2.** Confirms Regent Pringgalaya had fled Kèrtasana when his palace was gutted and plundered by Rangga.<br>**3.** Rangga marched north while Leberfeld maneuvered west to cut his retreat; overtook Rangga at Baring in the deep forest and found an opportunity to attack him at the rice fields (*sawahs*) near Sekaran.<br>**4.** Rangga's detachment emerged from the abandoned *dalèm* [palace] of Bupati Prawirayuda of Sekaran.<br>**5.** Rangga's *prajurits* fled the moment they realized they were trapped in a semi-circle, with the deep Solo River pinning their backs.<br>**6.** Radèn Tumênggung Yudakusuma attempted to parley and reason with Rangga.<br>**7.** **The Fatal Blows:** Rangga was attacked simultaneously from all sides: Ex-Tumênggung Sumadiwiria was the first to strike, driving a pike (*waos*) directly into Rangga's back; an ordinary Javanese foot soldier delivered the final thrust with a pike through his chest. |
