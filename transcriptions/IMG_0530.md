# Transcription of IMG_0530_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0530_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-07-02 to 1810-07-06 (2 Hooimaand to 4 Hooimaand [July] 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Evasion of Colonial Summons (2 Hooimaand [July] 1810): Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees summons Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III and Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] for early-morning consultations regarding the Sultan's compliance with ceremonial decrees. Rongga and DR II decline, replying that they can only appear in the evening because Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] is personally drilling and reviewing his armed regiments on the northern palace parade ground (*Paséban*).
* Protracted Audiences and Solo River Boat Building: Outer Regents Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat and Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I are kept waiting; Moorrees delays delivering communications from Sunan Pakubuwana IV until the Sultan responds on ceremonial protocol. Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels orders the construction of small boats along the Solo River, an order directed to Radèn Rongga; Rongga gives evasive and obstructive replies, feigning ignorance of conditions in his own mancanagara province. Financial requisition noted: 21% tax for three years dedicated to the minister of the court.
* Retention of Rongga in the Capital (*Rangga in Yogya*) (4 Hooimaand [July] 1810): Moorrees presses HB II to extradite all military deserters sheltered in Japan [Mojokerto] and commands that Rongga travel personally to Japan to execute the arrests.
* Royal Resistance: HB II authorizes DR II to handle the administrative reply but deliberately stalls on authorizing Rongga's mission, declaring that Rongga's unexpected departure for the eastern provinces would incite widespread public panic and unrest (*create too much of a sensation*). The Sultan insists Rongga must conclude his ceremonial duties in the capital before returning east, and directs that the investigation be delegated to Rongga's eastern co-regents: Radèn Tumenggung Pringgalaya (Kertasana), Radèn Tumenggung Sasranêgara (Grobogan), Mas Tumenggung Sumanêgara (Padhangan), and Radèn Tumenggung Pringgakusuma (Bupati of Rawa).
* Daendels' Counter-Order and HB II's Refusal: Daendels issues a second peremptory decree ordering Rongga to proceed east immediately to suppress deserters and facilitate commercial river exports along the Solo River as evidence of Yogyakarta's submission. Sultan HB II refuses to receive the dispatch from DR II, denouncing Daendels' hostile demeanor and rejecting the order in its entirety.
