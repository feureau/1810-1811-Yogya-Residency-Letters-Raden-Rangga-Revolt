| :--- | :--- |
| **Landdrost van Pabst**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Rembang Dispatch* | Pieter Albertus van Lawick van Pabst (Landdrost of Rembang) receives urgent official instructions from Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] and HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]:<br>• Orders immediate mobilization to coordinate northern and eastern militia detachments to attack and encircle Rangga in Madiun. |
| **Relaas of Jayakanda**<br>4 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Surabaya Officers* | Deposition (*relaas*) of Jayakanda (*wêdana* [regimental commander] of the 1st Regiment of Surabaya, *mantri* [official] in Suka Bêndha, Lingker district) and Kandayuda (*wêdana* of the 2nd Regiment, *mantri* in Belu, Lingker):<br>• Encounter Radèn Tumênggung Prawirayuda of Sekaran, who desperately seeks passage to Yogyakarta, but finds all trunk routes blocked by Rangga's rebel forces.<br>• Reports a fight with Rangga at Pacé in the Nala area.<br>• Confirms the loyal regents of Magetan have fled westward.<br>• Pangéran Purbanêgara at Kêdhiri positions defensive detachments at Pagerdhengan in Jagaraga.<br>• **Regents reported aligned with Rangga:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Radèn Tumênggung Pringgalaya (Kèrtasana)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara (Padagangan)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Radèn Tumênggung Sasrakusuma (Grobogan)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat (Rajêgwêsi) |
| **Prawiralêksana's Report**<br>5 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Roadblocks at Sekaran* | Prawiralêksana and Suradrana (*mantris* of Surabaya at Sukabêndha) to the Surabaya Bupatis (5 Dec. 1810):<br>• Officers depart Lingker for Sekaran to reinforce Bupati Wanaprana of Bauwêrna.<br>• Encounter a fugitive from Sekaran reporting that the *kabupatèn* is in ruins and the bupati has fled.<br>• Return to Sukabêndha where they meet Bupati Prawirayuda, who attempted to reach Yogyakarta but was completely cut off by insurgent roadblocks. |

Sasradiningrat (Rajêgwêsi)** $\rightarrow$ elevated to **B.P.H. Purbaningrat**<br>• **R.T. Sasrakusuma (Grobogan)** $\rightarrow$ elevated to **B.P.H. Purbawisésa**<br>• **R.T.
