# Transcription of IMG_0518_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0518_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 36)
- **Date Range:** 1810-04-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 36 detailing residency correspondence of 18 April 1810 between Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees and Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels regarding ceremonial protocol disputes and artillery salutes for the upcoming court festival; records Moorrees' report that a 21-gun artillery salute should be fired for Garêbêg Mulud when Sultan Hamengkubuwana II ascends the royal throne (*dhampar*), in accordance with Article 8 of the accession treaty of 10 July 1796; documents that HB II remains deeply aggrieved and humiliated over having been coerced by Daendels into surrendering Radèn Demang Tirtawijaya of Têrsana (who was executed by firing squad at Weleri), and consequently refuses to modify ancestral court ceremonial to comply with Daendels' *Nieuwe Reglement* (decree of 28 July 1808); notes Daendels' marginal rejoinder agreeing to Moorrees' temporary arrangement provided the Sultan pays for the gunpowder, while demanding strict future enforcement of the 28 July 1808 ceremonial decree in all its aspects; records HB II's agreement to drink a ceremonial toast to the health of Moorrees' wife immediately following toasts to the Minister and Crown Prince, upon which Daendels sharply annotates that women should have no official place in state toasts, defining it as entirely an informal private matter.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Radèn Tirtawijaya (Demang of Têrsana), Crown Prince (Pangéran Adipati Anom, later Sultan Hamengkubuwana III), Mrs. Moorrees (Resident's wife).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Têrsana, Weleri.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 36, Garêbêg Mulud (Prophet's Birthday court festival), Dhampar (royal golden throne), 21-gun artillery salute, Treaty of 10 July 1796 (HB II accession contract), Nieuwe Reglement (ceremonial edict of 28 July 1808), State toasts protocol, Gunpowder expense liability.
