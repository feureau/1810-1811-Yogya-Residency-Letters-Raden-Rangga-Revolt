Engelhard informed the Regent and Hamengkubuwana II of Janssens's capitulation talks with the British. The news deeply distressed the Regent, who had pinned his hopes on Dutch victory and dreaded an agreement with the English; not grasping the nature of capitulation, he was somewhat appeased by Janssens's pledge to look after the Yogyakarta royal house, vowing reliance on the Dutch or else abandoning himself to fate.<br>Col.
