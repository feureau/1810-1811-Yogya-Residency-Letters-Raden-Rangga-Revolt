* **Top Center (Red Ink):** `18 Nov. 1810` / `- 26 Nov. 1810`
* **Top Right (Blue Ink):** `AN 'Djokjo R. Ronggo 1810 - 1811' no. 46.`
* **Physical Characteristics:** Lined notebook leaf, 33 blue ruled horizontal lines, red vertical margin line; 2 punch holes at left edge; blue ballpoint pen text with red ink date heading and double margin bracket. Contains quotes from intercepted Javanese letters written by Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III to his close confidant Radèn Tumênggung Natadiningrat justifying his rebellion (*lêmbara*), promising immunity to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's lands, instructing the destruction of strategic river bridges, and intelligence from the Landdrost of Semarang/Demak regarding the Grobogan regents.
