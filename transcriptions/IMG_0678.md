### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **10 Herfstmaand [Oct. 1810]**<br>pp. 404–405<br><br>*(Double red margin rule)* | **Travel Restrictions & Security Screening:**<br>• Targeted at persons suspected of evil intentions, spreading seditious speech and incitement.<br>• **Pass System:** No travel passes to be issued except to individuals of certified good character.<br>• No *priests* [religious figures / *santri*] to be permitted to journey to the territory of the Sunan [Sunan Pakubuwana IV], and priests in general prohibited from travelling across Java. |
| **16 Herfstmaand [Oct.] 1810**<br>p. 409<br><br>*(Red ink margin)*:<br>**priests in Sala**<br><br>*(Double red margin rule)* | **Prohibition on *Wong Durjana* & Undocumented Travellers:**<br>• Under the guise of travellers, many *slecht volk* [bad people] / *wong durjana* [evil-doers / bandits] travel out of VL [*Vorstenlanden*] to plot nocturnal robberies.<br>• Prohibition on travel for priests in the *Vorstenlanden* and Java extended to all inhabitants of the Sultan [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] and Sunan, as well as all Asiatics (non-Indonesians in general).<br>• **Penalty:** Anyone arrested without a valid travel pass to be sentenced to chained forced labor at **Merak Bay** [*Merak-baai*]. |
| **27 Wijnmaand [Oct. 1810]**<br>p. 455 | **Chinese *Bandars* & Village Renters:**<br>• Chinese *bandars* [tollgate farmers] and renters of villages in Yogyakarta and Surakarta [Sala] must all obtain official *piyagems* [written licenses/permits] directly from the Residents. |
| **21 Louwmaand [Jan.] 1811**<br>pp. 540–544<br><br>*(Red ink heading)*:<br>**Territorial Arrangements post.** | **Boundary Commission Instructions:**<br>• *Instructie voor de commissarissen ter regeling en tot-stand-brenging van de grens-scheiding tusschen de Gouvernements- en Vorstenlanden. Jan. 1811.* [Instructions for the commissioners for regulating and effecting the boundary demarcation between Government and Princely territories].<br>• *(Note)*: `See xerox.` |
| **17 Jan. 1809 ? 1811**<br>p. 682 | **Galuh Interim Administration:**<br>• Second *opziener* [overseer] of Cianjur ordered to remain in charge of Galuh as long as it has not yet been formally handed over to Yogyakarta. |
