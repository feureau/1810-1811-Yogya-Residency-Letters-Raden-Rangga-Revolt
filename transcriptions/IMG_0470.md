### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Jacob Andries van Braam** (`van B. / v. B.`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 307). Special commissioner sent by Daendels to resolve the diplomatic crisis in Yogyakarta.
  * **Pieter Engelhard** (`Eng`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 331). Resident of Yogyakarta (1808, 1810–1811).
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 12). Reigned 1792–1810. Refused to stand from his royal throne (*dhampar*) during residency audiences, resisting colonial dominance.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Pangéran Ratu / Crown Prince)** (`PR II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 13). Reigned 1812–1814. Handled negotiations between the kraton and the Dutch commission.
  * **Marshal Herman Willem Daendels** (`D / Daendels`): Listed under *Colonial Officials: Governors-General* (line 282). Issued categorical ultimatums demanding submission and the removal of Radèn Rongga.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danureja II** (`P. / Patih`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 182). Patih of Yogyakarta (1799–1811).
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat (Pakualam II)** (`ND.`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 24). Reigned 1829–1858; in 1810 served as scribe and confidant in the circle of Pangéran Natakusuma and the court.
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III** (`Rangga`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). *Bupati wedana* of Madiun; HB II reluctantly agreed to send him to Bogor, but Rongga chose open rebellion instead.
  * **Krijgsman**: Official government Javanese translator attached to the Government of Java's Northeast Coast at Semarang.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Yogyakarta Kraton** (`KRATON`): Seat of the Sultan of Yogyakarta.
  * **Surakarta** (`Sala`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 393). Capital of the Sunanate.
  * **Klathèn** (`Klaten`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 423). Post and relay station halfway between Yogyakarta and Surakarta.
  * **Bogor (Buitenzorg)** (`Bogor`): Official country seat of the Governor-General of the Netherlands Indies.
  * **Japan (Mojokerto)** (`Japan`): Listed under *Senior Officials / Mancanagara* (line 265). Eastern mancanagara regency under joint jurisdiction.
  * **Fort Rustenburg / Vredeburg** (`Fort`): Colonial garrison fortress situated directly opposite the Yogyakarta Kraton.
* **Terms:**
  * **dhampar**: The sacred elevated gilded throne of the Javanese Sultan.
  * **Nayakas**: Senior royal executive council of bupatis governing the administration of the realm.
  * **bupati**: High-ranking territorial governor.
