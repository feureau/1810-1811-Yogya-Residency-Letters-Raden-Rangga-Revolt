# Transcription of IMG_0526_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0526_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 (R. Rangga 1810–1811)  
**Date:** 1810-08-01 (Javanese: 29 Jumadilakir [1737] / DR II Remonstrance: 27 July 1810; citing prior events of 29 Jan 1810 and 1 Feb 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Remonstrance from Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees and DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III following complaints from the Bupatis of Panaraga.
* Radèn Rongga's point-by-point rebuttal dated 1 August 1810 (29 Jumadilakir) denying that he sent an armed invading expedition into Panaraga.
* Rongga clarifies that the nine named commanders (R. Prawirapraja, Ng. Puspadiwiria, Ng. Kêrtarêja, Prawirabrata, Jayingkara, Tirta-atmaja, Prawiralaya, Ranadirja, Partakusuma) were a defensive border guard stationed to intercept Panaraga cattle-thieves and bandits from Ngêbêl and Sikêdok.
* Rongga sent *para ulama* (Islamic scholars) as peace emissaries to Panaraga following his letter of 29 January 1810.
* Tragic skirmish at Ngêbêl / Sikêdok: three inhabitants unexpectedly attacked Rongga's patrol (*amuk*), resulting in two dead and one wounded; Rongga denies any pre-planned assault.
* Exoneration of Bratasentana (ennobled as Radèn Prawirabrata): fled Panaraga four years earlier, known for upright conduct; Rongga refutes the accusation that Prawirabrata led a bandit gang that plundered five gamelan sets.
* Rongga vigorously rejects Panaraga's claims that Madiun inhabitants raid Panaraga or that he is secretly conspiring with Surakarta, declaring that many Madiun subjects have in fact been slain by Panaraga robbers.
