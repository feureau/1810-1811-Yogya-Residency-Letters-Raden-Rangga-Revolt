| **Paulus to Engelhard, 26 Nov. 1810:**<br>• Written from **Gabusan**, 3 days journey from Madiun; written in the open air under a straw hut.<br>• 4 days journey from Madiun: meets Javanese traveler **Ongadriya** journeying from Madiun to Yogyakarta.<br>• Interrogated for news: reports that Rangga plans to march to the Pasisir [North Coast]; issues strict orders for all subordinate Bupatis to appear before him.<br><br>**Pinggir Encounter with Blora Nobility:**<br>• At Pinggir during flight from Yogya: Rangga intercepts the son of the Bupati of Blora named **Tirtakusuma**; forces him to swear an oath to follow Rangga until death.<br><br>*(red highlight & underline)*:<br>• `Rangga gives Bêkêl Rangga Prawiradurga a blue payung [state parasol].`<br><br>**Destruction of Royal Halts (*Pesanggrahan*):**<br>• Also burns down all stopping places and rest-houses (*pesanggrahan*) of Surakarta royal prince **Pangéran Purbanegara**. | **Roadblocks & Sovereign Regalia (*Payung*):**<br>• Complete blocking of all major and minor roads leading into Madiun.<br><br>*(double red vertical line)*:<br>• **Rangga carries a yellow *payung*** [royal golden parasol reserved exclusively for reigning monarchs].<br>• Accompanied by **5 green *payungs* with yellow bands** carried by his five chief lieutenants:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1. Puspadiwiria<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2. Prawiralaya<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3. Prawirapraja<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4. Prawirabrata<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;5. Ranadirja<br><br>• Nothing new to report about HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]'s expeditionary troops.<br>• **P.S.:** Rangga reported at **Desa Tarik** in Madiun; intelligence reports he is preparing to march out and launch a counter-attack on the expeditionary column. | **Purwadipura to Danureja II, 25 Nov. 1810 (27 Sawal 1737):**<br>• At the village of **Cêmara** in Madiun: meets a woman named **Anga Pria**.<br><br>*(red underline)*:<br>• `Coming from Madiun = a servant of Ratu Madurêtna [official wife of Radèn Rongga and daughter of HB II].`<br>• Sent by Radèn Ayu Rangga's mother carrying foodstuffs and provisions for Radèn Rongga, with instructions to pay homage unto him.<br>• Met Rangga in Desa Pinggir; delivered the provisions from his mother and paid compliments.<br>• States that **Radèn Ayu Rangga [Ratu Madurêtna]** was profoundly grieved upon hearing the news that Radèn Rongga was to be summoned/sent to Batavia, precipitating the revolt.<br><br>**Historical Significance:**<br>1. *The Golden Parasol:* Rangga's display of the royal yellow *payung* marked his symbolic coronation as sovereign king in the mancanagara.<br>2. *Court Intrigues:* Ratu Madurêtna's domestic support network remained active, secretly provisioning Rangga from within Madiun. |
