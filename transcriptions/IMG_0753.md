| **Semarang, 11 Sept. 1811 — Janssens [Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens] to Engelhard [Pieter Engelhard]:**<br>Reports 24 Aug.: Meester Cornelis taken by STORM.<br>*(Right column)*: Yogya [Yogyakarta] troops called to Jambu Mt.; Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] accords complete trust in Janssens. Eng. [Engelhard] increasingly favourable impression of the Regent: receives news calmly. Janssens asks for `25,000` Silver Guilders credit from Yogya; Regent places complete trust in Janssens to care for his troops. | **Semarang, 11 September 1811 — Janssens to Engelhard:**<br>Reports Meester Cornelis fell by storm on 24 [26] August. Yogyakarta troops were summoned to Mount Jambu; the Prince Regent received the news calmly, placed full trust in Janssens to care for his troops, and Janssens requested `25,000` silver guilders' credit from Yogyakarta. |
| **Djogja, 11 Sept. 1811 — Engelhard -> Janssens:**<br>Receives news about N.K. [Pangéran Natakusuma] / N.D. [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat]; wishes speedily to get oath of allegiance from them.<br><span style="color:#a00">**party at court prejudicial to NK + ND**</span>: REGENT, PATIH [Radèn Adipati Danureja II], members of PARTY **would prefer to see N.K. dead** =.<br>COUNCILS not STATUS QUO should be recognised under ENGLISH.<br>NATA KUSUMA / NATA DININGRAT to be kept away from court `||`. | **Yogyakarta, 11 September 1811 — Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>Received news of Natakusuma and Natadiningrat and seeks a swift oath of allegiance from them. The Regent, Patih, and their court faction **would prefer to see Natakusuma dead**, urging that they be kept away from court and that existing councils rather than the pre-1810 status quo be recognized under the English. |
| **Yogya, 12 Sept. 1811 — Eng - Janssens** *(Received Semarang 14 Sept.)*:<br>TREATY of surrendering LANDS to be REVOKED = and troops returned after fight + ENGLISH.<br>**Yogya, 14 Sept. (`Letters No. 24`) — Eng - Janssens:**<br>12 English ships appear in Semarang Harbour of Rear-Adm. Stopford + Army of Auchmuty -> assures HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] that Janssens able to defend Island against the British (`P.S.` Janssens at Ungaran to direct troop movements from 11 Aug.). Attempt to prevent any treasonable correspondence between Princes / Nobles in Yogya + enemy.<br>**17 Sept. 1811 — Eng - Janssens:** *Sérondol* [Serondol] Ungarang: Janssens still at Serondol = Yogya still calm, no change in situation, no disturbances. *(Boxed: see over, 4 Nov.: Slag Janssens 15 Sept.)* | **12–17 September 1811 — Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>12 Sept.: Land-cession treaty to be revoked and troops returned after fighting the English.<br>14 Sept. (*Letters No. 24*): 12 British ships under Rear-Admiral Stopford and General Auchmuty's army appeared off Semarang; Engelhard assured Hamengkubuwana II that Janssens could defend the island, while moving to block treasonable correspondence between Yogyakarta nobles and the British.<br>17 Sept.: Janssens remains at Serondol (Ungaran); Yogyakarta remains calm *(note: Battle of Serondol/Jatingaleh fought 15–16 Sept.)*. |
| **7 NOV. 1811 — Eng [Engelhard] - Raffles [Thomas Stamford Raffles]:**<br>Report of Dismissal of **D.R. II** + father **Danukusuma** [Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I] (1st Buiten Regent).<br>DR II has NOT appeared outside the Kraton + general rumour that BOTH massacred in the morning.<br>HB II says that DR II sentenced to death; following day body taken out of the Kraton *(crossed out: body appears on walls of Kraton)*.<br>**SINDUNEGARA** [Kyai Adipati Danureja III] proclaimed by HB II as effective PATIH; GOVNMT. [Government] / RES. [Resident] NOT informed. | **7 November 1811 — Engelhard to Raffles:**<br>Reports the dismissal of **Danureja II** and his father **Danukusuma** (First Outer Regent). Danureja II failed to emerge from the Kraton amid rumors both were massacred that morning; Hamengkubuwana II stated Danureja II was sentenced to death, and his body was carried out of the Kraton the next day. **Sindunagara** was proclaimed effective Patih by Hamengkubuwana II without informing the Government or Resident. |
