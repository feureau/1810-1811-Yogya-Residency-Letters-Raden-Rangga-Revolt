* **Document Subject:** Sumanêgara's distribution of opium chests and silver reals to rebel retainers, Daendels dictating a permanent limit of 3,200 royal troops, Natakusuma's removal to Bogor, and Daendels's proposition demanding HB II's abdication.

### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sumanêgara's Order**<br>6 Dulkangidah 1737<br>(4 Dec. 1810)<br><br>*Opium & Silver Subsidies* | Kangjêng Gusti Pangéran Raja Nurca Adiprakasa [Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara] to Mas Ngabèhi Sumarêja (4 Dec. 1810):<br>• Commands Sumarêja, Trunawijaya, and all brothers/sons from Kapadagangan to march and join Sumanêgara in defense of Sunan Ingalaga [Radèn Rongga].<br>• Plans to advance toward Surabaya via Japan.<br>• Admits that currently Sumanêgara has only three remaining faithful retainers: Rênadrana, Nakidin, and Singa Lêmbu, all others having deserted.<br>• Instructs Sumarêja to urgently dispatch reinforcements and evacuate all family property from Padangan into mountain hiding.<br>• **Rebel Finances:** Rangga provided Sumanêgara with 200 guilders (*f.* 200) for travel expenses, one entire chest of raw opium (*candu*), and *f.* 50 of Sumanêgara's own money.<br>• Sumanêgara distributed 130 silver Spanish Reals (*Reels*) among his men, and ordered that opium rations be handed out to induce recruits to follow the rebel banner. |
| **Daendels's Treaty Terms**<br>27 Dec. 1810<br>(Kamalaka)<br><br>*Troop Limitation* | Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] to Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] (from encampment at Kamalaka; see *Staat*, Bijlage 2, Add. Stuk 17):<br>• Daendels dictates terms for a humiliating new treaty reducing the Kraton's military capacity.<br>• Sultan HB II is to be legally permitted a standing royal army of no more than **3,200 troops**, with permission to mobilize between 15,000 and 20,000 only in the event of foreign European invasion. |
| **HB II to Daendels**<br>28 Dec. 1810<br>(Fri. 1 Bêsar 1737)<br><br>*Veiled Abdication Terms* | Sultan HB II to Marshal Daendels (28 Dec. 1810):<br>• Acknowledges Daendels's dispatch of 27 Dec.<br>• Concedes he did not report Rangga's rebellion swiftly enough, responding to formal complaints lodged through DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II].<br>• Pleads that now both Rangga and Sumanêgara are dead, the Governor-General will pardon him.<br>• DR II notifies the Sultan that Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I] and Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] have been transferred under armed guard from Semarang to Buitenzorg [Bogor] to preserve the peace of the Kraton; HB II expresses gratitude.<br>• **The Abdication Demand:** Daendels hints he will present a proposition to HB II designed to bring *"good fortune to HB II's family"*<br>*(Carey notes: i.e. forced abdication in favor of the Crown Prince)*.<br>• HB II expresses readiness to receive this proposal, hoping to conclude a definitive peace for Java. |
