# Transcription of IMG_0543_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0543_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37  
**Date:** 1810-08-21 to 1810-11-21 (21 August [Oogstmaand] to 21 November [Slagtmaand] 1810; citing 21 Sept., 15 Oct., 18 Oct., 29 Oct., and 21 Nov. 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Resolution of the Kapantaran Water Inquest: Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] concludes the examination of Kyai Jayiman of desa Kapantaran, accused of stopping water to the Ampèl estate due to the non-receipt of his 214 Spanish Dollars [Sp. D.] annual salary/stipend (? *pradikan* lands).
* Diplomatic Correspondence Between the Courts:
  - Engelhard to Servatius (21 September 1810): DR II sends formal instructions to Ngabèhi Sasradipa and Natadiwiria, forwarding missives to Radèn Tumenggung Cakranêgara.
  - Surakarta Court Dispatches (15 Wijnmaand [October] 1810): Letters from Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Danuningrat to Yogyakarta officials Ngabèhi Warak, Wirasanta, Suradirana, and Sutamênggala; DR II transmits an official reply on 18 Wijnmaand.
  - Rural Agrarian Violence (29 Wijnmaand 1810): Engelhard reports an attack by a Chinese national on a Yogyakarta farmer near Babatan (? Bagelèn).
* Outbreak of the Revolt: The Flight of Radèn Rongga (21 November [Slagtmaand] 1810):
  - In the early hours of the morning (approximately 2:00 AM), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III flees Yogyakarta for his administrative seat at Madiun!
  - The colonial residency is thrown into intense vigilance, awaiting decisive political action from Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II].
* Royal Proclamation and Cavalry Mobilization (21 November 1810):
  - HB II issues an official royal circular and decree outlawing Radèn Rongga, delivered in person to Resident Pieter Engelhard by the brother of DR II.
  - The proclamation is ordered to be copied and dispatched immediately to all provincial regents (*bupatis*) to prevent them from rallying to Rongga's banner.
  - Lieutenant Paulus is mobilized with a detachment of colonial cavalry from Fort Klaten (*Klathèn*) to track and intercept the rebel army.
