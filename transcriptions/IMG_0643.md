### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Daendels's Dispatch**<br>25 Nov. 1810<br>(corrected from 29 Nov.)<br><br>*Fortress Supply* | Resident receives official dispatch from Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] dated 25 Nov. 1810:<br>• European observers struck by the absolute refusal and unwillingness of Daendels to cease suspecting HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II].<br>• Fresh military supplies ordered shipped to the Dutch garrison inside Yogyakarta fortress (*Fort Rustenburg*).<br>• Confirms intelligence that the Crown Prince [CP / future HB III], Pangéran Mangkubumi, and senior courtiers refuse all attempts to persuade them to abandon HB II.<br>• Reports that Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat [SD] of Rajêgwêsi is preparing to unite his troops with Purwadipura [PWD] against Rangga. |
| **Sasradiningrat to DR II**<br>4 Dec. 1810<br>(6 Dulkangidah 1737)<br><br>*Rajêgwêsi Defiance* | Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat to Radèn Adipati Danureja II [DR II] (4 Dec. 1810):<br>• Sasradiningrat and all fellow regents in the northeastern districts flatly reject Rangga's summons to assemble at Madiun.<br>• Upon receiving HB II's royal proclamation (*undhang-undhang*) of 21 Nov. 1810, Sasradiningrat resolves to march and join forces with Purwadipura.<br>• Notes that while Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara was forcibly coerced into joining Rangga, all his brother regents remain steadfastly loyal alongside Sasradiningrat. |
| **Sasradipura / Sasrawinata to ND**<br>4 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Magetan Evacuation* | Sasradipura and Sasrawinata to Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat / ND] (4 Dec. 1810):<br>• Magetan completely overwhelmed and conquered by Rangga's insurgents on **Tuesday-Kliwon, 27 Nov. 1810** (*Selasa-Kliwon*).<br>• The *Tumênggung* of Magetan is compelled to flee along with his household, family, and remaining loyal troops into the rugged Hill of Tenggulon (*Gunung Tenggulon*). |
| **Relaas of Cakralêksana**<br>Tues. 4 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Courier Mission* | Deposition (*relaas*) of Yogyakarta courier (*oppasser*) Cakralêksana (4 Dec. 1810):<br>• Dispatched on secret mission to Rajêgwêsi by order of Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.], traveling overland via Asinan, Gandaguli, and Ngawi.<br>• En route, is intercepted by an armed rebel scout (*oppasser*) belonging to Rangga's detachment, who interrogates him as to whether he carries dispatches for Europeans or for Chinese merchants.<br>• Cakralêksana shrewdly deceives the insurgent guard by declaring himself an *oppasser* in Chinese employment, whereupon Rangga's messenger allows him to proceed unhindered.<br>• Reaches Rajêgwêsi and delivers the Resident's letters to Sasradiningrat; Bupati Natadiwiria of Puri is likewise present at the conference.<br>• Proceeds onwards to Wirasari to brief the local bupati, who reports that Pangéran Prangwêdana [Mangkunagara II] and his army have marched through Kuto Klawu bound for Blora. |
