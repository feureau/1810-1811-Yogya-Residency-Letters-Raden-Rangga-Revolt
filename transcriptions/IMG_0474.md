### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus)** (`PB IV`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 18). Ruler of Surakarta (reigned 1788–1820) who ordered the dismissals and clandestine executions of his ministers.
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III** (`Rangga`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). *Bupati wedana* of Madiun whose 2,000 Sp.D. bribe brought down the Surakarta prime minister.
  * **Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta)** (`Mangkubumi`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 80). Younger brother of PB IV; later exiled to Ambon (1818–1824).
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana V (Sala Crown Prince)** (`Sala CP`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 19). Reigned 1820–1823; acted with Mangkubumi to denounce Mangkupraja.
  * **Radèn Adipati Mangkupraja II** (`Mangkupraja`): Listed under *Patih of Surakarta* (line 212). *Patih* of Surakarta dismissed in 1804 and murdered in exile.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danuningrat** (`Danuningrat P.M.`): Listed under *Patih of Surakarta* (line 212). Successor to Mangkupraja as *patih* of Surakarta.
  * **Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II** (`Sasradiningrat, son of Mangkupraja`): Listed under *Patih of Surakarta* (lines 212, 231, 257). Son of Mangkupraja II who later attained the Surakarta grand vizierate.
  * **Johan Frederik baron van Reede tot de Parkeler** (`van Reede`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta / Governors of Java's Northeast Coast* (lines 300, 303). Dutch colonial official.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Panaraga (Ponorogo)**: Listed under *Places: Mancanagara* (line 128). Destination of Cakrawinata's fatal exile and site of his burial.
  * **Ayah (Banyumas)**: Remote coastal enclave on the Indian Ocean in southwestern Central Java where disgraced ministers were banished.
  * **Pring Ombo & Pring Tutul**: Locations of the political assassinations in Mangkunagaran and Banyumas territories.
* **Terms:**
  * **patih (P.M. / Prime Minister)**: Chief administrative minister and head of court bureaucracy.
  * **Spanish dollar (Real batu / SpD)** (`Sp.D.`): Listed under *Currencies* (line 674). The currency used for the illicit payment (`2,000 Sp.D.`).
