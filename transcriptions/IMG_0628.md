Pringgakusuma [Bupati of Rawa]:**<br>• First to receive news of the impending flight of Rangga.<br>• On recent return of Rangga from Madiun, tells Pringgakusuma [PK] at meeting at Dêlanggu that he had written to all Bupatis in Madiun that he would probably not appear in court at the *Mulud* festival.<br>• But had not received an answer from the Bupatis.<br><br>*(red underline)*:<br>• `Reason PK does not tell HB II: Out of fear of being punished by HB II. Because everyone knows how much influence Rangga has on HB II.`<br>*(marginal gloss)*: `Collusion of R + HB II` | **Warning to Bupati of Kêrtasana:**<br>• Pringgakusuma informs **R.T. Pringgalaya** [Bupati of Kêrtasana] of his meeting with Rangga.<br>• Warns him that should Rangga give him any orders not confirmed by Pringgakusuma, Pringgalaya must not obey Rangga's orders.<br>• States that if Rangga returned to Madiun without Pringgakusuma, it would be an unmistakable sign that Rangga is rebelling against HB II.<br><br>**New Royal Orders (carried by Dipakusuma):**<br><br>*(red underline & red marginal heading)*:<br>`HB II orders Rangga to be killed.`<br>• DR II informs Resident Engelhard that **HB II orders that if Rangga is captured, he should be killed immediately**.<br>• **Reason:** HB II would be profoundly embarrassed if Rangga were brought back alive as a prisoner to Yogyakarta. *See AN Yogya 49, Engelhard dispatch of 4 Nov. 1808.* | **Ancestral Guarantees of Sultan Mangkubumi [HB I]:**<br>HB I made sacred promises to the prominent commanders of the Giyanti War (1746–1755):<br><br>1. Grandfather of Rangga [Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja I]<br>2. Father of R.T. Sasrakusuma<br>3. Grandfather of Mèrtalaya<br>4. Father of R.T. Sasrawijaya (Bupati of Pacitan; Mas Tumênggung Natawijaya)<br>5. Father of Sindunagara [Kyai Natapraja]<br><br>• Because of their unyielding loyalty to HB I during the Giyanti War, the Sultan vowed **never to harm their descendants or shed a drop of their blood, but always to forgive their transgressions**.<br><br>*(red marginal annotations)*:<br>`Sumanegara v. good footing + Yogya court. Sumadiningrat sister = R.A. Sepuh [Ratu Ageng / Ratu Kedhaton]`<br><br>**Historical Significance:**<br>Explains why HB II secretly wished Rangga dead in the field: executing a direct descendant of the Giyanti heroes at court would violate the sacred oath of Sultan Mangkubumi, whereas dying in battle would spare the dynasty cosmic and political disgrace. |
