### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Left:** `DAENDELS / INSTRUCTIONS TO / VAN BRAAM / WIESE / ENGELHARD`
* **Top Center:** `1812 / 18 Jan.` / *(Red Ink)*: `5 Jan. 1811`
* **Top Right:** `Dj. Br. 42. / See Xerox` / `BOUNDARY DIVISION pp. 102–4`
* **Margin (Left):** `(1)` / `p. 102` / `STRANDGELD` / `KÈDHU` / `FINANCIAL INDEMNITY`
* **Document Subject:** Upper notes record the 18 Jan. 1812 preliminary treaty articles concluded by John Crawfurd with the Hereditary Prince of Yogyakarta calling for Sultan HB II's removal; lower notes detail Daendels's 5 Jan. 1811 instructions to Commissioners van Braam, Wiese, and Engelhard for a rational boundary settlement, abolition of *strandgeld*, partial Kèdhu road cession, and debt/indemnity terms.
* **Physical Characteristics:** Ruled white notebook folio paper; two binder punch holes on left margin with rust oxidation stains; blue ink handwriting with red ink date headings, double vertical margin marks, and *Plakaatboek* cross-reference.
