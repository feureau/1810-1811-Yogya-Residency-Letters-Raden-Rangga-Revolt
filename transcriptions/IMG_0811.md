| `Letter of PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] to General Auchmuty` | **Letter of Sunan Pakubuwana IV to Lieutenant-General Sir Samuel Auchmuty** (Romanized Javanese *Turunan*, Part 1 of 2). |
| `Turunan.`<br>`Ingkang serat pratonda tulus mijos saking éklasing ati sutji Kangdjeng Susuhunan Paku Buwana, Sénapati ing Ngalaga, Ngabdurrahman Sajidin Panatagama ingkang kaping Sekawan, ingkang akadaton ing Nagri Surakarta Hadiningrat... datenga ingkang pawong sanak kang temen-temen, Kangdjeng Tuwan Djèndral, Litnan Atmijoté [Auchmuty], kang kautus Kangdjeng Radja ing tanah Inggrisman...` | **Salutation:** Sincere letter from Sunan Pakubuwana IV at Surakarta Adiningrat to his true friend General Lieutenant Auchmuty, envoy of the King of England, praying for his long life, safety, and health. |
| `Receives letter of Auchmuty`:<br>`Sasampuning ingkang kadya punika awijos Sahudara, sampun pandjenengan kula atampi serat idjengandika...`<br>Explains difficulty sending reliable messengers to the Governor-General because French troops are along the road; expresses desire for lasting friendship with the English Governor and English people, and clarifies he had only aided General Jan Willem Janssens out of prior duty before receiving Auchmuty's letter. | **Content:** Acknowledging receipt of Auchmuty's letter, the Sunan explains that he had been unable to send a trustworthy messenger to the British Governor-General because the French were on the roads. Now that the English Governor desires lasting friendship, the Sunan pledges true friendship with the English for the welfare of all Java, asks Auchmuty not to be offended by any shortcomings in his correspondence or lack of a skilled scribe, and excuses his earlier assistance to General Janssens. *(Continued on [IMG_0812.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0812.md).)* |
