### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Appeal to Chinese**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Anti-European War* | Continuation of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga]'s royal proclamation to North Coast Chinese leaders:<br>• Declares his sole royal aim is to protect and assist both Chinese and Javanese inhabitants who are being terribly oppressed (*"ontzaggelijk treated awfully"*) under European colonial rule.<br>• Exhorts the two Kapitans Cina (*Kyai Kapitan Kuci* of Lasêm and *Baba Baeng Sing* of Rêmbang) to actively assist in the total destruction and extermination of all Europeans throughout Java.<br>• Once Europeans are eliminated, Java will return to universal peace and prosperity.<br>• Pledges that cooperating Kapitans Cina will be permanently confirmed in their coastal administrative offices for themselves and their descendants in perpetuity.<br>• Offers royal protection to all Chinese who aid the revolt, proposing a personal summit at Lasêm.<br>• Designates rendezvous: On Thursday, 1 Bêsar, Rangga will arrive at Desa Putih in the Lasêm district.<br>• Demands that the Kapitans send two Chinese envoys with their formal reply.<br>• **Stern Ultimatum:** If Chinese fail to cooperate in exterminating Europeans, their homes, shops, and properties will be mercilessly sacked and plundered by Rangga's army. |
| **Leberfeld to Eng.**<br>17 Dec. 1810<br>(Sekaran)<br><br>*Pursuit Column* | Field dispatch of interpreter (*taalkundige*) Lucas Leberfeld to Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] (17 Dec. 1810, from Sekaran):<br>• The road from Madiun to Caruban is found completely abandoned and empty.<br>• Advances to Anjuk: The local *dêmang* [district head] has fled into deep forests in terror of Rangga.<br>• Rangga plundered the entire district and carried off Chinese hostages with him.<br>• Follows Rangga's trail to Kèrtasana: Similarly empty; Regent Pringgalaya has fled into the woods.<br>• Rangga passes Kèrtasana, presses on to Munpêng [Munèng], camps overnight, and marches to Poong.<br>• Leberfeld pursues Rangga through the dense forests of Caruban until tracking him to Sekaran.<br>• *Margin note:* None of the Javanese regents attached to Leberfeld's column know the forest roads; Leberfeld is forced to navigate and lead the vanguard in person. |
| **Battle Preparations**<br>17 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Sekaran Standoff* | Encirclement at Sekaran:<br>• **6:00 p.m.:** Leberfeld halts at Pandan Jaya; three allied regents muster 150 armed retainers.<br>• **7:00 a.m. (17 Dec.):** Leberfeld's column emerges from the forest edge.<br>• Rangga's force discharges two cannon shots: Leberfeld deploys his troops in battle line and orders field trumpets and drums to sound the advance.<br>• Rangga's force immediately answers with military trumpets and drums.<br>• **Rangga's Battle Standards:** The rebel force displays two royal pennants, two gold parasols (*payung emas*), and one royal green parasol with gold bands. |
