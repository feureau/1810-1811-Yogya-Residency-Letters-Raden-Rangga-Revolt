### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Winckelmann to Engelhard**<br>1 Dec. 1810<br>(Semarang)<br><br>*"Robber Not Emperor"* | Brigadier F.C.P. von Winckelmann to Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng]:<br>• Forwards report from Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst [van Pabst] in Rembang.<br>• Denounces Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga]: instead of conducting himself with imperial dignity as a self-styled sovereign (*prabu*), he behaves as a common bandit and robber.<br>• Expresses persistent anxiety that Rangga might succeed in seducing the Grobogan regents (hitherto loyal) under Radèn Tumênggung Sasrakusuma [R.T. Sasrakusuma]. |
| **HB II Edict at Alun-Alun**<br>1 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Public Royal Decree* | Royal proclamation of Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] is formally affixed at the northern square (*Alun-Alun Lor*) of Yogyakarta:<br>• The royal edict denounces Rangga's rebellion, strips him of all offices and fiefs, and commands all subjects to seize him.<br>• Dutch translation executed by colonial translator Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen / Diestrée. |
| **DR II to Cakranagara**<br>1 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Pacitan Reconnaissance* | Radèn Adipati Danureja II [DR II] writes to Radèn Tumênggung Cakranagara [bupati of Panaraga]:<br>• Radèn Tumênggung Pringgakusuma [R.T. Pringgakusuma] of the Mangkunagaran is dispatched by DR II to track down Rangga, advancing along the southern coastal route through Pacitan.<br>• In his fraternal diplomatic greeting, DR II warmly addresses Cakranagara as his revered "elder brother" (*kakang*). |
| **van Pabst to Winckelmann**<br>1 Dec. 1810<br>(1 Wintermaand)<br><br>*Surakarta Desas Burned* | Van Pabst to Brigadier von Winckelmann:<br>• A spy dispatched to Wirasari observes no rebel concentration; the Demang of Wirasari is sent to actively track Rangga's movements.<br>• Rangga is confirmed absent from Grobogan and Wirasari proper; the inhabitants of these districts refuse to follow or support the rebel banner.<br>• **Surakarta Villages Sacked:** On his flight from Yogyakarta, Rangga's men burned five Surakarta villages (*désa*): Valen, Bugan, Palwakan, Suka, and Wewangen / Kebon Purwa.<br>• **Rebel Desertions:** Of the four Tumênggungs who originally joined Rangga on his flight, two have abandoned him and returned submissively to Yogyakarta. |
