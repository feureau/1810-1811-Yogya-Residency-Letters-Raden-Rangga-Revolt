Sunan Pakubuwana IV writes to Janssens sending **3,000 troops** (excluding officers). Van Braam (? 11 Sept.) reports the Sunan's fear regarding the return of Natakusuma and Natadiningrat to Yogyakarta. |
| <span style="color:#a00">**Yogya, 11 Sept. 1811**</span> — **Eng - Janssens, 11 Sept.:**<br>News of capture of **Sumênêp** by English — Promotion of Pan. [Panembahan] Sumênêp to Sultan.<br>News causes a sensation: Regent says it was an *"akal"* (Invention) of the enemy to get the Sumeneppers on their side.<br>Relationship of Regent + Madura: *"Madurese blood to blame"* `||` *(gustia permeates his Madurese blood)*. Hopes that would take counter measures as speedily as possible.<br>Tells Regent of arrival of Yogya Troops in Semarang; tells Regent that if they acquit themselves well, Regent would be Rewarded with some land — which seems to please him very much. | **11 September 1811 — Pieter Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>News of the English capture of **Sumenep** and promotion of the Panembahan of Sumenep to Sultan caused a sensation. The Prince Regent dismissed it as an enemy stratagem (*"akal"*) to win over the Sumeneppers, remarking on his Madurese ties ("Madurese blood to blame"). Engelhard informed the Regent of the Yogyakarta troops' arrival in Semarang and promised that if they acquitted themselves well, the Regent would be rewarded with territory, which greatly pleased him. |
