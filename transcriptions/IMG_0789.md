| **ROBINSON (4-26 Sept. 1811)** *(red)*<br>`Eur. F 148/17 'Captain Robinson Java 1811'` | Extracts from Captain William Robison's correspondence (`Eur. F 148/17`, *Captain Robinson Java 1811*), 4–26 September 1811. |
| `Robinson -> Minto, 4 Sept. 1811 (Cêribon)`: `[Proclamation of 26 Aug. sent to both Ceribon Sultans —]` aims to forward the proclamation in a letter to Sala [Surakarta] / Yogya [Yogyakarta]. Prang Wedana Legion: 500 cavalry / 1,000 Infantry countermarched by Janssens to Semarang (20 days march from Cêribon) — earlier 2 days march from Cêribon. | **Robison to Lord Minto, 4 September 1811 (Cirebon):** Having sent the 26 August Proclamation to both Cirebon Sultans, Robison aims to forward it in a letter to Surakarta and Yogyakarta. He reports that Pangéran Prangwedana's [Mangkunagara II] Legion (500 cavalry and 1,000 infantry), previously two days' march from Cirebon, has been countermarched by Governor-General Jan Willem Janssens to Semarang (20 days' march from Cirebon). |
| `Robinson -> Minto, 6 Sept. 1811 (Cêribon)`: This morning Ceribon Sultan dispatches Messenger to Semarang / Sala / Yogya + Minto's Proclamation 26 Aug. 1811 + letter from Robinson to send letter to Mataram *(See Below p. 3.)*. Note right: `(can speak poor Malay - eager to learn Dutch / Malay, uses Interpreter)`. | **Robison to Lord Minto, 6 September 1811 (Cirebon):** The Cirebon Sultan dispatched a messenger this morning to Semarang, Surakarta, and Yogyakarta carrying Lord Minto's 26 August 1811 Proclamation and a letter from Robison for Mataram. *(Note: Robison speaks poor Malay, is eager to learn Dutch/Malay, and uses an interpreter.)* |
| `Robinson -> Minto, 7 Sept. 1811, no. 4`: As above: to circulate proclamation Sala / Mataram. | **Robison to Lord Minto, 7 September 1811 (No. 4):** Reiterating efforts to circulate the proclamation to Surakarta and Mataram. |
| `R [Robison] -> M [Minto], 10 Sept. 1811, Ceribon`: Idea of Aims to spread Eng. [English] Respect for *Adat*. | **Robison to Lord Minto, 10 September 1811 (Cirebon):** Aiming to publicize English respect for *Adat* (customary law). |
| `13 Sept. 1811, Janssens -> Landdrost Pekalongan (Ungarang)`: Eng. [English] at Semarang preparing to disembark. | **13 September 1811, Janssens to Landdrost of Pekalongan (from Ungaran):** Reporting that the English at Semarang are preparing to disembark. |
| `R -> M, 20 Sept. 1811`: 17 Sept. write to M. concerning armistice signed + Janssens. C-in-C [Commander-in-Chief] Auchmuty instructs R to carry Tidings to Sala / Yogya (to leave 21 Sept. 1811): (1) to communicate Minto desires same relationship as Dutch; Eng [Pieter Engelhard] / van Braam to continue as Ministers under Brit. [British] Protection. | **Robison to Lord Minto, 20 September 1811:** Refers to his 17 September letter on the armistice signed with Janssens. Commander-in-Chief Sir Samuel Auchmuty instructed Robison to carry the tidings to Surakarta and Yogyakarta (departing 21 September 1811) and communicate that Lord Minto desires the same relationship as under the Dutch, with Pieter Engelhard and Jacob Andries van Braam continuing as Residents/Ministers under British protection. |
