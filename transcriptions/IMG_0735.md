[English] 7 ships sail to East (? Surabaya / ? to take Madura). Eng. [Engelhard] does not tell DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II].<br>DR II informed, tells of possible Eng. [English] landing at Semarang.<br>Eng asks for Reinforcements; DR II tells that Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] prepared to give them.<br>**1,000 Troops prepared under:** DR II, R.T. Danukusuma [Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I], P. Dipakusuma [Pangéran Dipakusuma], Tum. [Tumenggung] Jayadipura.<br>Eng says DR II must Remain in Yogya.<br>Eng to Negotiate on land which should be annexed from Yogya. | **Pieter Engelhard to Governor-General Janssens, 12 August 1811:**<br>Report on Batavia (5–7 August): seven English ships sailed eastward (possibly toward Surabaya or Madura); Engelhard withholds this from Danureja II, instead warning him of a possible English landing at Semarang and requesting reinforcements.<br>Danureja II reports the Regent is ready to supply **1,000 troops** under Danureja II, R.T. Danukusuma, Pangéran Dipakusuma, and Tumenggung Jayadipura; Engelhard insists Danureja II must remain in Yogyakarta. Engelhard is also to negotiate over lands to be annexed from Yogyakarta. |
| *(Margin Note of Janssens, 20 Aug. 1811)*: V. pleased at news from Yogya. Would only use Troops in case of extreme urgency. Since 10 Aug. no change in position of enemy `∴` our position is improved. Enemy do not wish to attack Meester Cornelis, entrenching themselves. If anything occurs would tell Eng. | *(Janssens's note, 20 August 1811)*: Very pleased with news from Yogyakarta; will only deploy court troops in extreme urgency. Since 10 August the enemy has not changed position (improving the Dutch position), entrenching rather than attacking Meester Cornelis. |
| Court is very Tranquil -> As far as I could judge the appearance of so many English ships made not the least impression on them; also no curiosity to know more about situation.<br><span style="color:#a00">**HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] exercising Amazon Corps**</span> early on in the morning.<br>Thursday 15 Aug.: Sultan invited to participate in Dinner for Birthday of Napoleon at the Residency House.<br>From outward appearances the old gentleman is extraordinarily well, good spirits.<br>But I hear from the side *(note: "Bijzonder wel gehumeurd." [extraordinarily well-humored])* that *(he is)* very embittered with DR II for procuring a girl for the Regent (Regent for giving a woman to DR II ? from his family). HB II at variance + Regent; HB II extremely bitter against DR II *(continued on `IMG_0736`)*. | The court appears tranquil—the English fleet made no visible impression and aroused no curiosity. **Hamengkubuwana II is exercising his "Amazon" corps** [female guard] early in the morning, and was invited to Thursday 15 August dinner at the Residency House for Napoleon's birthday. Outwardly he seems in extraordinarily good spirits, though Engelhard hears privately that he is deeply embittered against Danureja II over procuring a girl for the Regent (or the Regent giving a woman from his family to Danureja II). |
