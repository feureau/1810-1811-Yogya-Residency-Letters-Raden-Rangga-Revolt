### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eng. to Paulus**<br>13 Dec. 1810<br><br>*(Marked in red box with double line)* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] to Lieutenant Thomas Paulus (13 Dec. 1810):<br>• Resident conveys urgent desire to see Rangga captured without further delay; stresses Daendels's extreme impatience at the snail's pace of the expedition.<br>• **Balinese Reinforcement Scare:** At the southern coastal port of Pacitan, numerous Balinese sea vessels (*praus*) are observed continuously arriving and departing.<br>• Notes that Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara hails from a prominent Balinese lineage (*"from a Balinese family"*).<br>• Asks whether Rangga is actively attempting to land auxiliary warrior reinforcements from the island of Bali.<br>• Instructs Paulus to take immediate counsel with Purwadipura [PWD] and Pringgakusuma (Regent of Rawa) regarding this maritime threat.<br>• Announces that Daendels has arrived in Semarang with the main colonial army, and Engelhard will join him in a few days. |
| **Command Shakeup**<br>14 Dec. 1810 | Engelhard to Paulus (14 Dec. 1810):<br>• Formal royal orders issued by HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] removing Purwadipura from supreme command and appointing Pangéran Adinagara in his stead. |
| **HB II to Expedition**<br>14 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Royal Decree* | Sultan HB II to the Yogyakarta Expeditionary Force (14 Dec. 1810):<br>• Notifies Lieutenant Thomas Paulus of the formal reshuffle:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Adinagara** appointed supreme operational commander.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Radèn Panji Jayèngrana** assigned special command over the elite *prajurit Jagakarya* [court bodyguard regiment].<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Command over the *prajurit gèdhong Têngên* [right court treasury corps] is summarily revoked from Purwadipura due to his shameful indolence and for allowing Magetan to be sacked and plundered. |
| **Eng. to HB II**<br>14 Dec. 1810 | Engelhard to HB II (14 Dec. 1810):<br>• Suggests that Radèn Tumênggung Sumadiningrat would be far more effective in replacing Purwadipura. |
| **Daendels to Eng.**<br>10 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Natapraja Exception* | Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] to Engelhard (10 Dec. 1810):<br>• Formally demands custody of Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I] and Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat], but indicates willingness to make an exception for Radèn Tumênggung Natapraja [NP].<br>• Nevertheless cautions that Natapraja's characteristically rash and brutal political advice to HB II renders him inherently dangerous. |
| **Eng. to van Braam**<br>11 Dec. 1810 | Engelhard to Admiral Pieter van Braam [van B] (11 Dec. 1810):<br>• Notes the retreat of the wife of the Regent of Sekaran to Japan.<br>• Instructs DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] never to read Daendels's letters in the presence of Natakusuma or Natadiningrat. |
