[Janssens] 15, 16, 17, 18 [Aug.] from Batavia.<br><u>No change in attitude of the court.</u><br>von Winckelmann asks for a party of Best Cavalry from Kraton to march + others (`1,000`) to Semarang.<br>DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] enters Kraton to speak + Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] about it.<br>Despite sickly condition Eng. would spare no pains to arouse good help from HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] (*"goede gezindheid"*). | **Pieter Engelhard to Governor-General Janssens, 24 August 1811:**<br>Acknowledges Janssens's letters of 15–18 August from Batavia; reports no change in the Yogyakarta court's attitude. Von Winckelmann requests a contingent of the Kraton's best cavalry to march with the other 1,000 troops to Semarang; Danureja II entered the Kraton to consult the Prince Regent on this. Despite his own ill health, Engelhard promises to spare no effort in securing Hamengkubuwana II's goodwill (*goede gezindheid*) and support. |
