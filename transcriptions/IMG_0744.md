Danukusuma**, commander of the troops marching to Janssens in Semarang, while an *"oppasser"* [orderly/messenger] carried a copy directly from Jambu. |
| **Letter of Regent -> Janssens, 15 Ruwah Jimakir 1738 / 4 Sept. 1811:**<br>After heavy cannonade 24/25 Aug., 26 Aug. Meester Cornelis taken; many troops attacked by fever / wounded.<br>Janssens asks **NOT** less than **5,000 Troops** from Yogya -> Semarang; asks for `3,000` immediately led by capable officers; would only be made use of in extreme necessity.<br>Would refuse to give shelter to Deserters from Janssens' army in VL [*Vorstenlanden*].<br>News of Birth of *Roi de Rome*, 20 March 1811: 101 cannon shots to greet event.<br>Can only send **2,400 Troops** not counting officers. | **Letter of the Prince Regent to Janssens, 4 September 1811 (15 Ruwah Jimakir AJ 1738):**<br>Acknowledges news that after heavy cannonading on 24–25 August, Meester Cornelis fell on 26 August with many troops struck by fever or wounded.<br>Janssens requested at least **5,000 troops** from Yogyakarta for Semarang (with `3,000` immediately under capable officers, to be used only in extreme necessity). The Regent pledges to deny shelter to deserters from Janssens's army in the *Vorstenlanden*, notes the 101-gun salute honoring the 20 March 1811 birth of the *Roi de Rome*, and states Yogyakarta can only send **2,400 troops** excluding officers. |
