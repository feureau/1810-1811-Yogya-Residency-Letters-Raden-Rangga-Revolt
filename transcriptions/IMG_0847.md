Body taken to `Banyusuměrěp`, 3 Hrs Distant from Yogya. | **Murder of Danureja II (28 October 1811):** Crawfurd notes DR II was universally disliked by the Javanese for oppressive conduct and family intrigues, his only friends in the Kraton being the Prince Regent [HB III], Pangéran Mangkubumi, and Ratu Kedhaton. While with Acting British Resident Pieter Engelhard on **28 October 1811**, DR II was summoned by HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] and seized at the palace door by seven men detailed by the Sultan (Sumadiningrat, Wiriawinata, Mertalaja, Kirtawiyana, Prawiranata, and Jaya Ngusman / ? Ahmad Bahwi). Sumadiningrat seized his kris; DR II was held for an hour, strangled, and his body taken to Banyusuměrěp (3 hours from Yogyakarta). |
| `Danukusuma`:<br>Father of DR II about `60` — not distinguished for His ability nor remarkable for His sincerity or good faith —<br>`Sinduněgara [DR III]`:<br>Present R. Adipati: `70 years of age` — Brother of Danukusuma: many Talents more solid than shining. Remarkable for caution and prudence. Age has certainly Blunted His faculties and at present unfit for the Transaction of public Business. | **Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I** (c. 60, father of DR II): Neither distinguished for ability nor remarkable for good faith.<br>**Kyai Adipati Danureja III (Sindunagara I)** (70, brother of Danukusuma): Solid talents, cautious and prudent, but age has blunted his faculties and rendered him unfit for public business. |
