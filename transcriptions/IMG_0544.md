# Transcription of IMG_0544_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0544_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37  
**Date:** 1810-11-21 to 1810-11-25 (Javanese: Wednesday 23 Sawal, Wawu, 1737 [21 November / Slagtmaand 1810] to 25 November 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Sultan's Proclamation of Outlawry Against Radèn Rongga (23 Sawal Wawu 1737 / 21 November 1810): Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] issues an official royal proclamation condemning the rebellion of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III following his pre-dawn flight from the capital.
* Purwadipura's Military Commission: Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] issues comprehensive operational instructions commissioning Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura to lead the state punitive expedition. The decree is ordered to be promulgated across all royal territories to mobilize the entire populace against the insurgents:
  - Pajang
  - Mataram
  - South of Gagatan
  - Sukawati
  - Kêdhu
  - Bagêlèn
  - Lidhok [Ledhok]
  - Gowong
  - Eastern Mancanagara (*MN lands*).
* Dead or Alive Royal Warrant: The decree commands that Radèn Rongga and his armed adherents must be captured wherever they attempt to take shelter, dead or alive (*alive or dead*). Should Rongga violently resist arrest or launch an attack (*amok maakte*), the royal forces are ordered to execute him on the spot. All subjects in the Mancanagara and the northern coast (*Pasisir*) are strictly forbidden from providing food, quarter, or lodging to the rebels under penalty of treason.
* Encirclement via Pacitan (Engelhard to Servatius, 25 November 1810): Radèn Tumenggung Pringgakusuma, Regent of Rawa, departs Yogyakarta with royal forces to advance across the southern mountains into Pacitan, tasked with cutting off Rangga's retreat toward the south coast.
