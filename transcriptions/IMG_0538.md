# Transcription of IMG_0538_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0538_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-07-13 to 1810-08-08 (13 July 1810 to 8 August 1810; citing 1 Oogstmaand [August] 1810; Carey header: `1810 / (July)`)  
**Key Events:**
* Palace Dispatch Following Royal Samas Excursion (13 July 1810, Yogya): First Clerk Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen transmits a sealed dispatch from Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, reporting that Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] returned midday yesterday from his pleasure palace at Samas.
* Royal Coastal Annuity (*Strandgeld*) (1 Oogstmaand [1 August?] 1810, Yogya): Moorrees notifies Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels that the monthly coastal rent (*strandgeld*) amounting to 12,500 Spanish Dollars [Sp. D.] is due to HB II, suggesting transmission from Semarang; Daendels appends an emphatic marginal decree: *"it will be sent!"*.
* Resistance to Chinese Resettlement Edict (Tan Jin Sing to Moorrees, 7 August 1810, Yogya): Daendels decrees that 20 Chinese families from Yogyakarta must be forcibly deported to the North Coast (Batavia, Cirebon, Banten) to establish new Chinese quarters (*kampongs*). Kapitan Cina Tan Jin Sing reports that every Chinese household approached adamantly refuses to relocate, protesting that forced removal is futile.
* Mancanagara Deserter Roundup and Daendels' Ultimatum (Moorrees to Daendels, 8 August 1810, Yogya):
  - HB II dispatches court Tumenggungs to scour the Eastern Mancanagara; 12 military deserters captured in Japan [Mojokerto] are marched to Surabaya under the custody of a Dutch police peon (*oppasser*), with all provincial thoroughfares placed under intensive roadblock inspection.
  - Regarding Tan Jin Sing's failure to furnish the 20 Chinese families, Daendels issues a furious personal rebuke:
    *"The Capitan Cina must produce them or he will forfeit his post. I will not allow myself to be ('ringslooven' [duped/bamboozled]) by any Capitan Cina. I want a written reply from the Capitan Cina once this message has been received."*
* Armed Clashes with Deserters (Undated dispatch): Moorrees reports on violent skirmishes during deserter roundups: 21 fugitives managed to escape, while 11 were killed in hand-to-hand combat after launching a desperate *amuk* charge.
