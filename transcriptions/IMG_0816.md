| `Crawfurd to Adams, 15 Nov. 1811`:<br>Nothing to Report since arrival in Yogya.<br>"HB II a sulking old Rogue" | **John Crawfurd to Adams, 15 November 1811:** Nothing to report since arriving in Yogyakarta; describes HB II as *"a sulking old Rogue."* |
| `Letter -> HB II by Crawfurd 15 Nov. 1811` (`Crawfurd's protest to HB II`):<br>"The undersigned elected Brit. Res. [British Resident] to the Court of Yogya has learnt with much surprise the unwarranted steps pursued by that court without the knowledge or sanction of the legitimate Representative of the Brit. Govmnt., Mr Engelhard.<br>The undersigned is in duty bound to enter his solemn protest against all the measures of the court of Yogya since the 31 October and to decline all intercourse with it until he receives the commands of that Govmnt. which manages the affairs of the conquerors of Java.<br>The Brit. Res. avails himself of this opportunity to assure the Royal family of Yogya of his high esteem and consideration.<br>Done at the Res. Ho. [Residency House] of Yogya this 15th Day of Nov. 1811." | **John Crawfurd's Formal Protest to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Residency House of Yogyakarta, 15 November 1811:**<br>Crawfurd, as incoming British Resident, registers his surprise at the Yogyakarta court's unwarranted actions taken without the knowledge or sanction of Pieter Engelhard (legitimate representative of the British Government), enters a solemn protest against all measures of the court since **31 October 1811**, and declines all intercourse until receiving orders from the Government. |
