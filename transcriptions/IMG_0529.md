# Transcription of IMG_0529_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0529_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-06-22 to 1810-07-02 (Javanese: Fri. evening 21 Jumadilawal, Wawu, 1737 [22 June 1810] to 2 Hooimaand [July] 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* The Ceremonial Crisis (*Q of Ceremonial*) and Daendels' Ultimatum (22 Zomermaand [June] 1810): Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees communicates to Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] the unyielding decision of Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels reached at Semarang. Daendels adamantly refuses any softening of the 1808 ceremonial protocols; if HB II fails to comply, Moorrees is commanded to evacuate Yogyakarta immediately.
* Daendels warns that severe retaliatory measures will follow if Dutch colonial officials are further harassed by armed militias operating under the Sultan's covert sanction, referencing the violent interdiction of Mrs. Moorrees' carriage en route to Semarang, which was surrounded and forced back to Yogya.
* Requirement of Royal Seal: Daendels mandates that HB II must validate all official communications to the Resident under the royal seal (*cap dalem*), mirroring the Resident's use of the seal of the King of Holland.
* Sanctuary of Slaves and Deserters in Japan [Mojokerto] (2 Hooimaand [July] 1810): Moorrees reports to Daendels regarding the investigation into desa Soari in the territory of Japan. According to the 1773 Land Register, the village is located north of the River Pêketon, named Kriyaman (popularly termed Simual/Simi), under Demang Kyai Nitikrama; in the absence of the Regents of Japan, Lurah Mas Prawirasêksana [Prawirahexana] administers the district capital.
* Yogyakarta Commission to Japan: A joint delegation comprising Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura, Radèn Tumenggung Mangunkusuma (?), Radèn Wiriawijaya, and Kertadipura is assigned to investigate the gathering of runaway slaves and armed military deserters.
* Radèn Rongga's Defiance: Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III disavows all prior knowledge of the deserters and staunchly refuses to conduct any inquiry without direct royal commands from HB II; Moorrees uses Daendels' ultimatum to pressure the Sultan to issue mandatory search warrants.
