[Lieutenant-Governor]`: `OPIUM FARMS at Yogya / Sala TO BE SOLD SEPARATELY: at the 2 COURTS for the NEXT year.` | **14 December 1811 — Raffles (Semarang) to Crawfurd (No. 3):** Sends a translation of the 1792 Treaty, noting that other requested papers were removed from the Semarang offices by Marshal Herman Willem Daendels in 1808, and that the 1773 (Land?) Register is likely among the papers transferred by Jacob Andries van Braam to Colonel Alexander Adams at Solo. Secretary J. Eckford advises that the Yogyakarta and Solo opium farms be sold separately for the coming year. |
| `23 Dec 1811` (`Raffles -> CR Surakarta`):<br>`TREATY of PERPETUAL FRIENDSHIP and alliance Executed / Ratified By BRITISH GOVNMT. | `25 Dec 1811` (`Sec Eckford -> CR. Surakarta`):<br>Raffles to leave for Klaten at `4 a.m.` `26 Dec.` | **25 December 1811 — Eckford to Crawfurd (Surakarta):** Raffles to depart for Klathèn at 4 a.m. on 26 December. |
| `26 Dec 1811 3 p.m.` (`Raffles -> CR Fort Klaten`):<br>`Resolved on REPORT of commission to Yogya -` Commission to proceed again to Yogya + Letter of Raffles -> Prince Regent. | **26 December 1811, 3 p.m. — Raffles (Fort Klathèn) to Crawfurd:** Following the commission's report on Yogyakarta, the commission is to return to Yogyakarta with a letter from Raffles to the Prince Regent [HB III]. |
