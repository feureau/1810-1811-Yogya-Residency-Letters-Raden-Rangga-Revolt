| **PROCLAMATION OF RAFFLES TO INHABITANTS OF JAVA / SENT FROM MALACCA 14 MARCH 1811** | **Proclamation of Thomas Stamford Raffles to the Inhabitants of Java, Sent from Malacca, 14 March 1811** |
| "Inhabitants of Java !<br><br>The fortunate period is arrived when the English authorities meditate an attack on Java - they will appear soon on your coasts with a Naval and Military force which must render [xx] resistance vain - already has the (island of) Mauritius and the whole of the French Naval and Military forces serving in this country surrendered to British arms - it is now intended to establish the British Interests in the Island of Java ! !<br><br>You cannot be ignorant of the much boasted strength of the Mauritius - you have heard with what facility it fell before English prowess and discipline - be not therefore deceived in your own strength, nor calculate too highly on the means of resistance adopted by your chief, who to effect a temporary military stand may be heedless how far he involves you and the whole island in misery and ruin - the English are fully aware of his resources and are prepared to meet them - for your own interests be cautious how you involve yourselves.<br><br>Inhabitants who have been long settled in Java with your families ! It is to you that the Proclamation is more particularly addressed, altho' it deserves the consideration of all ! What has been your situation for the last three years ! How long, after the failure of every attempt and clandestine trade, have not the precious productions of your island been blocked up within your settlements and mouldering in your stores ! How long have you not been deprived of every intercourse with neighbouring states and of every advantage from commerce ? And for what ? Are you not at this day subjected to the grossest military oppression and tyranny, to a system of forced loans and levies destroying alike every shadow of security either for person or property ?<br><br>Alas ! The politics that have latterly disturbed the peace of Europe have been fatally transplanted to your otherwise happy island - but the period of their termination is fast approaching - the English are at hand, all they require is that you should individually consider the state of affairs- deliberate thereon - and act for yourselves - your future happiness or misery as well as that of your families is in your own hands - the English character is not unknown to you - it is for you to decide -<br><br>Inhabitants ! Remain neutral - reside on your estates, maintain order and respect among your slaves, let not the public peace be disturbed, each of you maintain and be ready to deliver a good and true account of the duty with which your are now respectively charged - neither suffer the sequestration not the destruction of property." | "Inhabitants of Java!<br><br>The fortunate period is arrived when the English authorities meditate an attack on Java — they will appear soon on your coasts with a Naval and Military force which must render resistance vain — already has the (island of) Mauritius and the whole of the French Naval and Military forces serving in this country surrendered to British arms — it is now intended to establish the British Interests in the Island of Java!!<br><br>You cannot be ignorant of the much boasted strength of the Mauritius — you have heard with what facility it fell before English prowess and discipline — be not therefore deceived in your own strength, nor calculate too highly on the means of resistance adopted by your chief [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels], who to effect a temporary military stand may be heedless how far he involves you and the whole island in misery and ruin — the English are fully aware of his resources and are prepared to meet them — for your own interests be cautious how you involve yourselves.<br><br>Inhabitants who have been long settled in Java with your families!
