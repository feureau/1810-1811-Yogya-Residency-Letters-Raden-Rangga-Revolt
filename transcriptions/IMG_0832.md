## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Date for Arrival of AMBASSADORS NOW passed at Semarang`:<br>No ambassadors arrive from Yogyakarta:<br>`Refusal of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] / Regent to send AMBASSADORS in DIRECT VIOLATION of 24th ARTICLE of Treaty of 1792` *(see Ricklefs)*<br>`ASSUMPTION of POWER / GOVNMT [GOVERNMENT] By HB II NOT to Be COUNTENANCED`<br>`CR [Crawfurd] NOW to address Regent only as Holding chief authority in Yogya -` `∴ address Regent alone in all future correspondence + COURT:` | **15 December 1811 — Thomas Stamford Raffles (Semarang) to John Crawfurd** (`van Deventer`, pp. 311–313): Because no Yogyakarta ambassadors arrived at Semarang by the deadline (violating Article 24 of the 1792 Treaty), Raffles refuses to countenance Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's assumption of power and orders Crawfurd to address only the Prince Regent [HB III] as chief authority in all future court correspondence. |
| `p. 312`: `NK [Natakusuma] Leaves Semarang on MORNING of 15 Dec + Lt. [Lieutenant] Hunter.`<br>Tells NK Terms on which HB II would Be Recognised which would Be by a letter from HB II explaining his Reasons for Murdering DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] and reseizing authority.<br>HB II should as a PROOF of SINCERITY resign his Throne to the Regent and BRIT GOVNMT to make arrangements as Advisable.<br>If HB II accedes to these conditions — would Be again Restored to Favour and allowed to succeed to the Throne.<br>`Letter Signed By HB II to be Dictated By Crawfurd:` | **Terms Conveyed via Pangéran Natakusuma (departing Semarang 15 December with Lt. Hunter):** HB II must send a letter (dictated by Crawfurd) explaining his reasons for murdering Radèn Adipati Danureja II and reseizing authority, and resign his throne to the Regent as proof of sincerity, leaving arrangements to the British Government; if he complies, he may be restored to favour and the throne. |
| `p. 313`: `ON VISIT to Yogya` — Regent should meet Raffles and HB II should not appear until called for and Declared Sovereign by Raffles.<br>"It must be laid Down as a PRINCIPLE in all our Dealings with the NATIVE Courts that in no case must we demand what we cannot enforce." | **Protocol for Raffles' Visit:** The Regent is to meet Raffles first, and HB II is not to appear until summoned and declared Sovereign. Principle stated: *"in no case must we demand what we cannot enforce."* |
