## Metadata

- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI, Djokja Brieven, No. 27: Archival Research Notes on Yogyakarta Residency Letters (February 1810))
- **Date Range:** 1810-01-25 to 1810-02-06
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] replies to Daendels regarding extradition demands for Yogyakarta subjects accused of raids on Pekalongan, Demak, and Semarang; cites 1808 Treaty provisions requiring prior investigation; Wiese transferred to Inspector of North Coast Teak Forests and J.W. Moorrees [Morris] appointed Resident of Yogyakarta; joint inquiry by Lt. Wm. Driessen, R.T. Danukusuma I, and Tum. Kertawijaya clears local villagers but implicates Lurah Jagawarana and Saidja.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Herman Willem Daendels, Gustaf Wilhelm Wiese, J.W. Moorrees / Morris, Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I, Tumenggung Kertawijaya, Lt. Willem Driessen, Dietrée, Lurah Jagawarana & Saidja

  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Sprokkelmaand (February), Besar / AJ 1736 (Bé), Mantri / Gunung / Tamping, Teak Forests (Boschwezen / Houtvesterijen)
