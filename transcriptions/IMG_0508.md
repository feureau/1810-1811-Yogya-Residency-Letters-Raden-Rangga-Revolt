# Transcription of IMG_0508_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0508_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37)
- **Date Range:** 1810-05-12 to 1810-05-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes summarizing official residency correspondence from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37 between 12 and 22 May 1810; details communication between newly confirmed Patih of Solo, Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II), and Yogya Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja II; records the visit of the Dutch commandant of Fort Klaten to Solo requesting Surakarta corvée labor assistance for fort construction works; plans a joint diplomatic/investigative commission assisted by Europeans to investigate the border incursions and military posture of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III; documents Marshal Herman Willem Daendels' demands regarding court ceremonial protocols, carriage encounters, and mandatory toasts to be drunk with Sultan Hamengkubuwana II; notes tollgate (*bandar*) administration on the Siment/Magelang highway under Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat (Bupati Wêdana Kidul); records the public punishment of five Mandrapratamas sentenced by Danureja II to be exposed bound in the sun at the northeastern corner of the northern alun-alun for five days (7:00 to 10:00 AM); extracts a report from Jacob Andries van Braam to Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees regarding the Crown Prince's (Pangéran Adipati Anom) estate at Kadawangan near Sanagara, Jabaran, and Pasar Jêkaran, documenting illicit opium selling and abusive extortions by tollgate keepers (*bandhar*) who forcibly detain merchants overnight on frivolous pretexts, leaving them vulnerable to highway robbery (*kécu*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Jacob Andries van Braam, Radèn Tumenggung Cakranagara (Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II), Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat (later Paku Alam II), Crown Prince (Pangéran Adipati Anom, later Sultan Hamengkubuwana III).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Fort Klaten (Klathèn), Siment, Magelang, Kadawangan, Sanagara, Jabaran, Pasar Jêkaran, Alun-alun (northern alun-alun).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37, Patih, Fort construction corvée, Investigative commission, Ceremonial protocol & toasts, Bupati Wêdana Kidul, Mandrapratama, Rabingulakir / Wawu 1737 (Javanese calendar), Appanage estate, Opium trade (*madat*), Tollgates (*bandhar*), Highway robbery (*kécu*).
