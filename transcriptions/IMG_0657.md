### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Aftermath at Sekaran**<br>17 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Wrapping the Corpses* | Post-combat events at the Solo River bank:<br>• The *Dipati* (*patih* of Rangga) is wounded during the melee but manages to escape into the dense undergrowth alongside three parasol-bearers (*baturs*).<br>• Two hours after the fighting ceases, while Leberfeld remains standing vigil over the bodies, the Rajêgwêsi regents suddenly emerge onto the field: Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat, Prawirasêntika, Natadiwiria, and Prawirayuda of Sekaran (see BL Add. MS 12341 f. 18r).<br>• Prawirayuda had been hiding nearby throughout the battle, too terrified to show himself until Rangga was dead.<br>• **Shrouding:** The bodies of both Radèn Rongga and Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara are reverently washed and wrapped in clean white linen shrouds (*kain mori*).<br>• Lt. Paulus joins Leberfeld at Rajêgwêsi: Both officers return toward Yogyakarta, committing the shrouded corpses to Purwadipura [PWD] for transport to the capital.<br>• Flooded roads delay Purwadipura at Pinggir, making arrival in Yogyakarta impossible before Tuesday. |
| **HB II to Daendels**<br>30 Dulkangidah 1737<br>(26 Dec. 1810)<br><br>*Official Combat Account* | Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] (published in Daendels's *Staat van de Oost-Indische Bezittingen*):<br>• The Sultan formally reports the slaying of Rangga and Sumanêgara:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Rangga was struck with a pike in the chest by apprentice courtier (*magang*) Sumadiwiria, and finished off by royal infantrymen.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Sumanêgara wounded a cousin of Yudakusuma with a pike thrust in the back; Yudakusuma shot Sumanêgara with a firearm (matchlock / *bedhil sundhut*), after which royal *prajurits* killed him.<br>• HB II petitions Daendels that Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I] and Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] be granted immediate permission to return home to Yogyakarta.<br>• **Impending Invasion:** Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] warns HB II that Daendels is already marching on Yogyakarta at the head of a massive colonial army, and strictly demands to be received without customary royal court ceremonial. |
| **Conspiracy with Baba Nun**<br>18 Dulkangidah 1737<br>(16 Dec. 1810)<br><br>*(Marked with large red asterisk)* | **Intercepted Letter from Sumanêgara:**<br>Written under his rebel peerage title: *Kangjêng Gusti Pangéran Raja Suria Adiprakasa*, addressed to his "brother" Baba Nun (prominent Chinese merchant/leader):<br>• Summons Baba Nun to an urgent midnight meeting at the village of Telang to deliberate on joint strategies for exterminating all Europeans across Java.<br>• Informs Baba Nun that the courier, Prawiradiguna, is fully briefed and can explain the detailed justification for destroying European rule. |
