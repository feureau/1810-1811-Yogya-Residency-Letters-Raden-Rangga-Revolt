## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `HB II / Raja putra Narendra to G.G. [Governor-General] J.W. Janssens in reply of letter of Janssens 1 Sept. 1811` | **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and Crown Prince Raja Putra Naréndra [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] to Governor-General Jan Willem Janssens**, replying to Janssens' letter of **1 September 1811**. |
| `Turunan.` *(Paragraph 1 — Salutation):*<br>Punika ingkang serat pratanda tulus éklas kawijosing manah sutji saking ingkang wajah ingkang Sinuhun Kangdjeng Sultan Hamengku Buwana, Sénapati ing Ngalaga, Ngabdurrahman Sajidin Panatagama, Kalifatullah, saha ingkang putra Kangdjeng Radja Putra Naréndra, Kangdjeng Pangéran Adipati Anom Hamengku Negara, ingkang angrenggani Kedaton Negari ing Ngajogjakarta Hadiningrat, datenga ingkang pawon sanak ingkang temen-temen ingkang Ejang Kangdjeng Tuwan Jan Willem Jansens Divisi Djéndral, saha Gurnadur-Djéndral... | **Salutation:** Formal greeting from Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and Crown Prince Raja Putra Naréndra (*Pangéran Adipati Anom Aměngkuněgara*) at Yogyakarta Adiningrat to their grandfather Jan Willem Janssens, Division General and Governor-General, Commander of the *Orde der Unie*, wishing him long life and safety. |
| `Janssens asks for troops at Semarang / arrival of the English / 2,400 prajurits of HB II sent / R.T. Danukusuma in command Bupati Agěng Kliwon Bupati` | **Troop Dispatch (`2,400` *prajurits*):** In response to Janssens' request for troops at Semarang following the arrival of the English enemies on the coast (*Pasisir*), HB II dispatches **2,400 *prajurits*** (excluding *bupati kaliwon miji*, *mantri*, and *lurah prajurit*). |
| **Commanders & *Bupatis* Listed:**<br>• `radèn tumenggung Danukusuma` (*bupati agěng kaliwoning Pepatih*, overall commander)<br>• `radèn rija Sindurdja`, `radèn tumenggung Djajadipura`, `radèn tumenggung Ranadiningrat` (*bupati Mantjanegari*), `radèn tumenggung Judakusuma`, `radèn tumenggung Sasrakusuma`, `mas tumenggung Tjakradipura`, `radèn tumenggung Sumaredja`, `radèn tumenggung Wirjakusuma`, `radèn tumenggung Wirjadipura` (*bupati Mantjanegari*), `radèn tumenggung Sasradiningrat`, `radèn tumenggung Wirjanegara`, `ngabèi Mangundiprana`, `radèn tumenggung Mertadiwirja`, `ngabèi Mangunrana`, `ngabèi Ranawidjaja`, `mas ngabèi Wiraguna` *(margin: `Patih of the Kadipatèn`)*, `radèn tumenggung Dipadirdja`, `ngabèi Judapenantang`. | **Command Structure:** Led by **Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma** (*Bupati Agěng Kaliwoning Pepatih*), accompanied by *bupatis* of the interior and *Mancanegara* (including Radèn Tumenggung Ranadiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Sasrakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Sasradiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Sumareja, Mas Tumenggung Cakradipura, Mas Ngabèhi Wiraguna [Patih of the *Kadipatèn*], and fellow *bupatis* and *ngabèhis*) serving as *titindih* (commanders) of the troops. *(Continued on [IMG_0803.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0803.md).)* |
