| `Sumadiningrat`:<br>`50 years of age` `Tuměnggung Keparak of the Dalěm (Kori?)`. One of 2 in immediate confidence of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] *(other NK [Natakusuma] / MD [Mangkudiningrat])*. A man of no talent / His acquirements below those of most Javanese. So illiterate can neither Read nor Write. But nevertheless chief adviser of HB II. Haughty + Insolent. | **Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat (50):** *Tumenggung Keparak* of the *Dalem*; one of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]'s two closest confidants (the other being Natakusuma / Mangkudiningrat). Illiterate, haughty, and insolent, yet HB II's chief adviser. |
| `Sindurěja II`:<br>Other Tuměnggung Keparak of the Dalěm: Son of HB I [Sultan Hamengkubuwana I] by a concubine who was given to Sindurěja I by HB I (had no children `∴` adopted the son + gives Him His name). Many `1st Talents` in the country: fittest person to be made R. Adipati. But unlikely to accept position (ailing) Because sincere sickness `//` not much above `40` `//` confines Him to His House. | **Sindureja II:** The other *Tumenggung Keparak* of the *Dalem*; natural son of Sultan Hamengkubuwana I by a concubine given to the childless Sindureja I, who adopted him. Possesses the finest talents in the country and is the fittest person to be made *Radèn Adipati*, but is unlikely to accept as illness (just over 40) confines him to his house. |
| `Rexaněgara [Rěksaněgara]`:<br>Holds for long Farm of Customs. Unpolished manners — good ability. Age near `70` `∴` no other official position possible. | **Rěksaněgara (near 70):** Long-time holder of the customs farm; unpolished manners, good ability, too old for another post. |
| `Pringgakusuma [Pringgadiningrat]`:<br>Tum [Tumenggung] of Rawa in MN [Mancanegara]: of excellent talents — well acquainted with Malay — thought to be fit to be Raised as R. Adipati. Married to Best of HB II's daughters, `Ratu Ayu`. | **Pringgakusuma [R.T. Pringgadiningrat]:** *Tumenggung* of Rawa in *Mancanegara*; excellent talents, well acquainted with Malay, considered fit to be raised as *Radèn Adipati*; married to HB II's finest daughter, Ratu Ayu. |
