## Tabular Transcription

| Daendels's Munitions Ban & Kraton Isolation | General von Winckelmann's Mobilization | Eyewitness Intelligence from Maospati Fortifications |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engelhard to Daendels, 4 Dec. 1810 (9:00 p.m.):**<br>• Refusal of Marshal Daendels [D] to allow HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] to receive any ammunition.<br>• Resident Engelhard notes he does not include in this prohibition the yearly routine delivery of 500 lbs gunpowder for the firing of ceremonial salutes (order of 19 Jan. 1809) + 100 lbs for CP [Crown Prince / later HB III] (order of 21 Feb. 1809).<br><br>**Isolation of the Residency:**<br>• Extreme difficulty of getting reliable news from within the Kraton.<br>• HB II appears to have had his eyes opened by Rangga's open defiance.<br>• No Pangérans or Bupatis except for DR II [Danureja II] and Outer Regents ever come to visit the Resident. *Ricklefs see MS p. 352.*<br>• Resident only sees court princes at major court festivals (*Mulud*, *Puwasa*, *Bêsar*).<br>• Expects possible support from royal princes: **Panular**, **Ngabèhi**, **Mangkudiningrat**, **Dêmang**, **Adikusuma**, and **Adiwijaya**; but cannot guarantee anything.<br>• Sends Daendels extracts from the Residency's *Memorie van Overgave*. | **Engelhard to Servatius, 2 Dec. 1810:**<br>• News of attack on Magetan by Rangga.<br>• News that Rangga proceeds to the desa of **Nambang Nima** on 29 Nov.<br><br>**General P.G. von Winckelmann to Engelhard, 3 Dec. 1810:**<br>• Colonial infantry and cavalry ordered to march immediately into Central Java.<br><br>**P.H. van Pabst to General von Winckelmann, 1 Dec. 1810:**<br>• Eyewitness report from a Chinese traveler arriving from Maospati: reports tremendous commotion amongst inhabitants there.<br>• Rangga expected to arrive momentarily; Rangga's close confidant **Pangéran Ngabèhi** is already present at Maospati. | **Rebel Encampment & Fortifications at Maospati:**<br>• Present at Maospati: Bupati of Padangan [Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara], 4 other Mancanagara Bupatis, and the Patih of Panolan (as the Bupati of Panolan was sick).<br>• Bupati of Magetan flatly refuses to obey Rangga.<br><br>*(red underline & marginal heading)*:<br>`Kraton of Rangga` / `Kraton of Rangga at Maospati not yet completed: placing puris (spiked bamboo) on E. wall.`<br><br>**Historical Significance:**<br><br>1. *Total Arms Blockade:* Daendels cut off all weapons and gunpowder shipments to Yogyakarta, suspecting that royal munitions would be diverted to Rangga's forces.<br><br>2. *Maospati Palace Architecture:* Eyewitness intelligence confirms that Rangga began converting his official residence at Maospati into a fortified sovereign royal court (*kraton*), erecting sharp bamboo palisades (*puris*) along the eastern wall.<br><br>3. *European Troop Surge:* General von Winckelmann moved colonial regulars directly toward the borders of Madiun and Surakarta to crush the insurrection. |
