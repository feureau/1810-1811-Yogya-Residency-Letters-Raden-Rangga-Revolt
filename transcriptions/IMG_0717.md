Janssens is reviewing the files to discover the reason for their imprisonment, noting they had behaved well toward the Government, and promises further details in another letter.)* |

Malaria).<br>Goes today to the Court: somewhat improved recently. Talks to Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] to get immediate permission for:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Extraction of Teak wood from Panolang + Padagang woods for Building of ships at Surabaya.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Tells of Janssens's Intention to Visit Sala / Yogya — greatly pleases the Regent.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Hopes to get Better Repairs to Roads / Bridges; still much needs to be Done.<br>Sends Project Ceremonial of *Hulde* [Homage] works of Daendels. | Engelhard received Janssens's letter of 4 June last Monday, as well as the letter of 31 May reporting Daendels's arrival at Semarang en route to Surabaya; Engelhard could not meet Daendels due to daily fever (malaria).<br>Feeling somewhat improved, Engelhard went to Court today and spoke with the Prince Regent to secure immediate permission for:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Teak extraction from the Panolan and Padangan forests for shipbuilding at Surabaya;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Janssens's planned visit to Surakarta and Yogyakarta, which greatly pleased the Regent;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Improved repairs to roads and bridges, where much remains to be done.<br>He also forwards the draft ceremonial protocol for homage (*hulde*) drawn up under Daendels. |
