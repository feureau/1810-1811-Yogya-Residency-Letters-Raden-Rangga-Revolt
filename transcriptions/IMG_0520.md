# Transcription of IMG_0520_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0520_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-04-28
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 tracking the Panaraga-Madiun border clashes and court diplomatic maneuvers in late April 1810; top dispatch transcribes Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III reporting to Radèn Adipati Danureja II that Surakarta regent Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat had agreed to permit Rongga's men to track fugitives from Ngêbêl; Rongga's patrol pursued the fugitives as far as Sikêdok, found only local mantris/cripples (*kruppels*), and was suddenly ambushed from all sides, resulting in an armed melee (*amuk*) in which two Surakarta inhabitants were killed; Rongga informs Wiriadiningrat that Ngêbêl was plundered and three houses burned, inviting a summit with three Surakarta *bupatis* of Panaraga after Garêbêg Mulud in either Solo or Yogya; second section records Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees writing to Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels on 28 April 1810 reporting Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's willingness to institute a formal inquiry, while noting HB II's fierce indignation over Sunan Pakubuwana IV's accusations, which HB II viewed as a deliberate Surakarta plot (*Sala Policy*) to foment enmity between Yogyakarta and the Dutch Government; records HB II recalling the diplomatic disputes of 1808 (blamed on PB IV), citing as proof a letter from Cakranagara to Danureja II wherein PB IV referred to Daendels as his "Brother" (*Kakang*), arguing PB IV should properly have addressed the Governor-General with filial deference as "Grandfather" (*Eyang*).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Cakranagara (Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Panaraga (Ponorogo), Sikêdok, Ngêbêl.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Border ambush / armed melee (*amuk*), Garêbêg Mulud, Joint court commission, *Sala Policy* (Surakarta diplomatic intrigue), Diplomatic kinship honorifics ("Brother" vs "Grandfather" for Daendels).
