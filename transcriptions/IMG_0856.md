## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Raffles to Adams, 6 Dec. 1811`:<br>Request of Sunan that `van Braam / Servatius` not to be allowed to remain in Solo. Raffles to construe this as wish of Sunan to have a British Resident. | **6 December 1811 — Thomas Stamford Raffles to Colonel Alexander Adams (pp. 36–37):** Raffles construes the Sunan's request for the removal of Jacob Andries van Braam and Willem Nicolaas Servatius as a wish to have a British Resident. |
| `Request no. 7`:<br>Observation of old ceremonial to be positively Refused.<br>`p. 36`: Patih to continue on same footing as Before; Brit. [British] Resident to be treated with more respect if anything than was shown to Dutch Minister. | **Request No. 7:** Return to old ceremonial positively refused; the *Patih* continues on the same footing as before, and the British Resident is to be treated with even greater respect than the Dutch Minister. |
| `Request no. 8`:<br>Prang Wedana [Pangéran Prangwedana] to be placed under Sunan's Jurisdiction: Sunan's Request to be refused *in toto*. | **Request No. 8:** Placing Prangwedana [Mangkunagara II] under the Sunan's jurisdiction is refused *in toto*. |
| `Request no. 9`:<br>Return of *Kalangs / Pinggir* people on N.E. [North-East] coast antecedent to `1757`? Cannot be complied with. | **Request No. 9:** Return of *Kalang* and *Pinggir* people on the North-East coast (pre-1757 claim) cannot be complied with. |
| `p. 37` `Request no. 10`:<br>"That whereas he knows from experience that all disputes between the govnmt. [government] and his subjects are troublesome and often hard to decide, he requests that the Java(nese) law may in no wise be interfered with but the Java(nese) to be judged by their own laws."<br>`Raffles' Decision`: Sunan to administer justice to Javanese except in cases involving Chinese / Europeans when British Resident to see fair play. | **Request No. 10 (Javanese Justice):** The Sunan may administer justice to Javanese subjects under Javanese law, except in cases involving Chinese or Europeans, where the British Resident is to ensure fair play. *(Continued on [IMG_0857.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0857.md).)* |
