### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Daendels to HB II**<br>24 Nov. 1810<br>(15 & 16 Sawal)<br><br>*Demak Restitution & Reinstatement of DR II* | Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] to Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]:<br>• Acknowledges receipt of two royal letters from HB II dated 15 and 16 Sawal 1737.<br>• Pleased that HB II acquiesces to the terms and demands conveyed by Admiral Pieter van Braam [van B], but still insists upon full restitution of royal and merchant goods plundered at Demak.<br>• Daendels expresses severe annoyance that the Sultan gave no response whatsoever to the explicit advice regarding Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I / NK] contained in Daendels's letter of 3 Nov. 1810.<br>• The Governor-General expresses satisfaction that, following receipt of his letter, HB II formally reinstated DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to his full prime ministerial authority, which had been temporarily usurped by ND [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat / Pakualam II], who had briefly been entrusted with the royal seal of state. |
| **Rangga & Natakusuma Threat**<br>24 Nov. 1810 | Daendels warns HB II against the dual danger posed by Rangga and Natakusuma:<br>• Notes that while Rangga was ostensibly journeying toward Buitenzorg [Bogor], Daendels intended to ensure he was neutralized and put out of action for a considerable time.<br>• Warns that as long as Natakusuma remains influential at court, HB II's own royal throne continues to be directly undermined.<br>• The colonial Government explicitly reaffirms that it will never abandon its treaty obligation to protect the Crown Prince [Pangéran Adipati Anom / future HB III] and DR II. |
| **NK Dangerous v. HB II**<br>*(Marked with double red vertical rule)* | **Explicit Threat of Dynastic Dethronement:**<br>• Natakusuma would never dare attempt a coup against HB II so long as the Sultan relies firmly on Dutch protection.<br>• Warns HB II that Natakusuma's primary objective is to drive an irreparable wedge between HB II and the colonial Government.<br>• Daendels bluntly warns HB II that if he does not immediately alter his political attitude and distance himself from anti-European court advisers, he might soon have to contend with a far less indulgent French Governor-General, who would *"force HB II off his throne + loss of kingdom."*<br>• Therefore, HB II must cast aside all intriguing advisers and genuinely seek Dutch friendship.<br>• Concludes that HB II must formally renounce Natakusuma; only then will Daendels regard the Sultan as a true and loyal ally. |
| **von Winckelmann to Eng.**<br>6 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Expedition Mobilization* | General P.G. von Winckelmann to Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] (6 Dec. 1810):<br>• Relays intelligence from Daendels that the Marshal departs Bogor for Semarang on 6 Dec., expecting to arrive on 10 Dec.<br>• Deployment timeline:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **1 Dec. 1810:** Two squadrons of cavalry and two companies of horse-drawn artillery march overland from Batavia.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **2 Dec. 1810:** 3,000 regular infantry troops embark on transports to land at Semarang. |
