| **Translation of Report of Sasringgala (24 Nov. 1810):**<br>• Trusted confidante of the **Dêmang of Dêlanggu**.<br>• On Friday morning: **Purwadipura** [Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura] and other Yogya commanders pass Dêlanggu.<br>• Column strength: **15 Europeans**, **10 cannon on gun carriages**, and **2,500 armed *prajurit*** [infantry/cavalry warriors].<br><br>**Engelhard to Daendels, 26 Nov. 1810 (9:00 p.m.):**<br>• At 1:00 a.m. in the morning, Princes and Bupatis emerge from the Kraton after swearing an oath of loyalty to HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] and promising to support the expedition against Rangga.<br>• Royal conference concluded with a formal supper. | **HB II & Patih Danureja II's Predicament:**<br>• HB II did not inform Resident Engelhard immediately about the flight of Radèn Rongga.<br>• Patih Danureja II [DR II] was unable to inform HB II immediately because the Sultan was sleeping.<br>• DR II feared informing Engelhard without prior royal authorization from HB II.<br>• DR II feared that Rangga's flight would bring misfortune and ill luck upon him.<br><br>*(red underline)*:<br>• `DR II excuses Rangga to HB II because knows that HB II v. fond of Rangga / would hear no evil spoken of him.`<br>• With tearful eyes, DR II relates the news of the flight to the other royal princes, expressing profound shame and grief at the crisis. | **Expedition Reinforcements from Yogyakarta:**<br>• Early tomorrow morning: **Pangéran Dipakusuma**, **Pangéran Adinagara**, and **R.T.
