# Transcription of IMG_0524_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0524_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-05-20 to 1810-08-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 detailing the deepening diplomatic crisis between Yogyakarta and Surakarta over Radèn Rongga's protection of outlaws between May and August 1810; transcribes a dispatch of 20 May 1810 from Panaraga Regent Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat to Solo Patih Radèn Adipati Cakranagara reporting that Rongga evaded formal inquiries with evasive platitudes, flatly refusing to extradite the Surakarta deserter Radèn Bratasena; notes that Rongga ennobled Bratasena with the title Radèn Prawirabrata, appointed him *Lurah Mantri* of Kajineman, celebrated him as a direct descendant of the legendary Majapahit prince Batara Katong (founder of Panaraga), granted him cannons, and placed a network of cross-border robber captains under his command (Nitrajaya, Ranasêntika, Ranawijaya, Pak Naya, Tambakyuda, Dêmang Walêngpati, Ningadrana, Nuraliksana, Padanala, and Netrawani); records that captured *gamêlan* orchestra sets stolen from Panaraga were presented to Rongga in Madiun; notes Jacob Andries van Braam transmitting the report of the three Panaraga regents to Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels on 25 May; concludes with Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees warning Sultan Hamengkubuwana II on 2 August 1810 that Rongga's depredations are plunging Yogya into dangerous conflict with Daendels and Sunan Pakubuwana IV, bluntly observing that while PB IV demonstrates due deference toward Dutch authority, the Sultan does not.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat, Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Bratasena (Radèn Prawirabrata), Batara Katong, Nitrajaya, Ranasêntika, Ranawijaya, Pak Naya, Tambakyuda, Dêmang Walêngpati, Ningadrana, Nuraliksana, Padanala, Netrawani, Jacob Andries van Braam, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Panaraga (Ponorogo), Madiun, Bulak, Lembah Lèr, Bader, Wanut, Grogol, Nirêm, Gradarah, Katitang, Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Deserter protection, Lurah Mantri of Kajineman, Descendant of Batara Katong, Artillery endowment (cannon), Stolen gamêlan orchestra sets, Colonial diplomatic reprimand.
