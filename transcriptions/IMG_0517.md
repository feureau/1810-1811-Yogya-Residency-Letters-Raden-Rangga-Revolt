# Transcription of IMG_0517_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0517_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-04-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 detailing the local origins of the border clashes between Yogyakarta's eastern *mancanagara* (Madiun) and Surakarta's regencies (Panaraga) in April 1810; transcribes dispatches from Surakarta's Panaraga regents (Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Wiradirja, and Radèn Tumenggung Bratanêgara) to Solo Patih Radèn Adipati Cakranagara reporting and denouncing Rongga's armed incursions into Sunanate territory; records Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees warning Sultan Hamengkubuwana II on 22 April 1810 that Rongga's actions jeopardize Yogyakarta's standing with Sunan Pakubuwana IV and Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels (reminding him that Daendels previously executed a European in Banten by hanging for similar insubordination); records Moorrees informing Willem Nicolaas Servatius that Rongga's report was delayed due to illness; extracts Radèn Rongga's formal explanation to Yogya Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja II (translated by Pietrée) stating that Yogyakarta desas in Madiun were repeatedly plundered by Panaraga inhabitants led by a 12-man robber band (Kêrtajaya, Jaya Leksana, Nowijaya, Cakrajaya, Jurajaya, Wirakrama, Kêrta Leksana, Kramadikara, Kêrtayuda, Putradangra, Jayatuna, and Pancadilaga) based in the Surakarta village of Sikêdok (Rêdi Ngêbêl area); explains that when his subordinates tracked stolen water buffaloes to Sikêdok and retrieved three, they were ambushed by Solo chiefs, prompting Rongga to deploy troops to the frontier to intercept the bandits.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Radèn Mas Aria Wiriadiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Wiradirja, Radèn Tumenggung Bratanêgara, Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II), Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Pietrée (translator), Kêrtajaya, Jaya Leksana, Nowijaya, Cakrajaya, Jurajaya, Wirakrama, Kêrta Leksana, Kramadikara, Kêrtayuda, Putradangra, Jayatuna, Pancadilaga.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Panaraga (Ponorogo), Surakarta (Sala), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Banten, Madiun, Sikêdok, Ngêbêl.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Border cattle rustling (buffalo theft / *kêbo*), Robber band ringleaders, Capital punishment in Banten, Cross-border extradition, Bupati Mancanagara.
