| `6 Sept 1811` / `Capt. Robinson. Proclamation to Sala / Yogya` | **6 September 1811 — Capt. William Robison's Proclamation to Solo [Surakarta] and Yogyakarta:**<br>The whole province of Cirebon, Jacatra, and West Java is in English hands. Any further resistance by French fugitives from Meester Cornelis is in vain. The courts should not be deceived by their ministers. Lord Minto desires good relations with Sunan Pakubuwana IV. |
| `HB II -> Regent -> Minto 26 Sept. 1811` (`7 Ramělan 1226 / 26 Sept. 1811`, `Eur. F. 148/18`):<br>• Request Burial ground of Tegal Arum + Keeper of the grounds.<br>• A Place cleared by Daendels for a park (? at Tegal Arum) which it is requested your Highness Return to its Former state.<br>• People of Frontier oppressed / plundered by Daendels - request a stop should be put to those proceedings.<br>*To Restore in the country former usages so that the Heart of the King + Princes may Receive Consolation.* *(underlined in red)* | **Requests of Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and the Prince Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] to Lord Minto, 26 September 1811 (7 Ramelan 1226 AH):**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Restoration of the burial ground at Tegal Arum and its keeper.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Return to its former state of a place cleared by Marshal Herman Willem Daendels for a park (at Tegal Arum?).<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Cessation of the oppression and plundering of frontier inhabitants initiated under Daendels.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Restoration of former usages in the country *"so that the Heart of the King and Princes may receive consolation."* |
| `Engelhard / Regent -> Minto 25 Sept. 1811`:<br>Receives letter of Minto - Eng. [Pieter Engelhard] to be allowed to Remain in Yogya to continue office under the Eng. [English] govt. [government]. To consult + W. Robinson over Yogya. | **Pieter Engelhard / Prince Regent [HB III] to Lord Minto, 25 September 1811:** Acknowledges Minto's letter; requests that Pieter Engelhard be allowed to remain in Yogyakarta to continue in office under the English government and consult with William Robison over Yogyakarta. |
| `HB II -> Minto 25 Sept. 1811`:<br>Requests Return of NK / ND [Natakusuma / Natadiningrat] "wish of the Rest of the Family in Yogya". | **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II to Lord Minto, 25 September 1811:** Requests the return of Pangéran Natakusuma and Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat, stating it is the *"wish of the rest of the family in Yogya."* |
