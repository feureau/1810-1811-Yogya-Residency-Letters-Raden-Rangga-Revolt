# Transcription of IMG_0533_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0533_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-07-08 to 1810-07-10 (8 Hooimaand / 6 Jumadilakir 1810 to 10 Hooimaand [July] 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Delivery of Surakarta Missives and the Throne Platform (*Gilang*): Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] requests Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees to deliver Sunan Pakubuwana IV's letter to Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] accompanied by royal heralds (*gandhêk*) from Surakarta in strict conformity with ancient custom (*adat*). Regarding the elevated royal stone throne (*gilang*), DR II asserts it cannot be modified, having served for the reception of numerous previous Dutch dignitaries.
* Resident's Ultimatum Before Royal Departure to Samas (8 Hooimaand / 6 Jumadilakir 1810): Moorrees protests that he has waited fruitlessly since 5 Hooimaand for the Sultan's compliance regarding ceremonial protocols. Despite laying out the dire consequences to Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III and Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat, Moorrees learns that Sumadiningrat has already departed for the southern royal retreat at Samas and HB II prepares to follow immediately. Moorrees declares he can wait no longer, commanding DR II to secure an immediate royal audience before the Sultan departs.
* Summons to Klaten and Danureja II's Defiance (10 Hooimaand [July] 1810): First Clerk Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen receives a remonstrance from DR II, translating the Javanese text through translator Dietrée. Acting on instructions from Moorrees (who has retired to Klaten on the border), Driessen places a carriage at DR II's disposal to bring him to Klaten for interrogation.
* Danureja II refuses to leave Yogyakarta, explaining that because Sultan HB II is absent at Samas, he is under paramount royal duty to maintain security and keep watch over the Kraton (*keep watch in the kraton*).
