### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Kedhu Danger**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Natadiningrat & Natakusuma* | Danureja II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II / DR II] warns Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]:<br>• Suggests Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] might attempt to raise a rebel army in the fertile province of Kedhu to join Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga].<br>• In response, HB II contemplates ordering Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I] to join the eastern expeditionary force under Purwadipura [Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura / PWD].<br>• Secret royal instructions: Natakusuma is to be watched vigilantly and executed on the spot at the slightest indication of treachery or defection. |
| **Ratu Kencana & R. Ayu Anom**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Court Hostage Plan* | Waning influence of Ratu Kencana and dynastic security:<br>• HB II expresses growing displeasure with Ratu Kencana, although she openly denounces Rangga (partly embittered over unpaid personal loans and missing court jewelry entrusted to Rangga).<br>• Security contingency: Should DR II march to the front, Radèn Ayu Anom (daughter of Ratu Kencana and wife of Natadiningrat) must be immediately removed from Natadiningrat's residence and sequestered inside the Kraton inner precinct as a political hostage. |
| **DR II's Surety**<br>Dec. 1810 | Danureja II's testimony and personal pledges:<br>• DR II declares himself fully prepared to take a solemn religious oath that HB II had absolutely no prior knowledge of or complicity in Rangga's rebellion.<br>• DR II offers personal surety for Natadiningrat's loyalty (arguing that Natadiningrat and Rangga were bitter personal rivals), but admits he cannot vouch for Pangéran Natakusuma. |
| **Crown Prince's Pledge**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Filial Oath* | Filial commitment of the Crown Prince [Pangéran Adipati Anom / future HB III]:<br>• The Prince solemnly vows before his father that he will never desert or betray him under any colonial pressure.<br>• Declares that he will never seek or agree to ascend the throne before his father's proper time or death. |
| **Elderly Princes**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Physical Debility* | Critical assessment of senior court nobility and Dutch warnings:<br>• Senior court figures are physically decrepit: Pangéran Ngabèhi is excessively aged and frail; Kyai Tumênggung Sindunagara and Radèn Tumênggung Danukusuma I are likewise very old and incapable of vigorous military exertion.<br>• Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] privately discloses to the Crown Prince that Marshal Daendels harbors deep suspicions against both the Sultan and his entourage. |
