# Transcription of IMG_0515_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0515_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo 1810–1811*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-03-03 to 1810-04-18
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Archival research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 tracking the diplomatic escalation of the Radèn Rongga affair in March–April 1810; records Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees writing to Willem Nicolaas Servatius on 3 April 1810 reporting that Javanese letters regarding Rangga could not yet be translated because Yogya Patih Radèn Adipati Danureja II was incapacitated by sickness, promising to demand an accounting directly from Rangga and transmit it to Surakarta for the inspection of the Sunanate's eastern *mancanagara* regents; documents Servatius' concurrence of 31 March expressing relief that Moorrees shares his view of the matter; transcribes a Javanese dispatch from Radèn Rongga to Danureja II dated Saturday, 3 March 1810 (26 Sura, Wawu 1737), stating he had corresponded with Surakarta's Bupati of Panaraga, Radèn Tumenggung Aria Wiriadiningrat, concerning border criminals and submitting the dossier to Danureja II for executive decision; concludes with Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels' directive to Moorrees of 18 April 1810 acknowledging receipt of complaints from Sunan Pakubuwana IV and a Surakarta regent regarding Rongga's armed incursions into Sunanate territory, ordering that both letters be formally communicated to Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and demanding what satisfaction Yogya will render to Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Radèn Tumenggung Aria Wiriadiningrat, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Panaraga (Ponorogo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Patih, Sickness of Danureja II, Cross-border jurisdiction, Mancanagara regents, Sura / Wawu 1737 (Javanese calendar), Border criminal tracking, Formal satisfaction (*satisfactie*).
