# Transcription of IMG_0535_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0535_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-07-09 to 1810-07-13 (9 Hooimaand to 13 Hooimaand / 11 Jumadilakir 1810 [Friday 13 July 1810])  
**Key Events:**
* Danureja II's Defiance and Moorrees' Departure (9–10 Hooimaand [July] 1810): Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees writes to DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to review dispatches from Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels and grievances lodged by a Surakarta noblewoman (*Radèn Ayu from Sala area*), summoning the Patih to appear the following morning at Klaten (*Malatten*).
* Military Summons Rebuffed: Moorrees instructs First Clerk Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen and Adjutant Lammers to compel DR II's attendance, but DR II flatly refuses.
* Diplomatic Despair: Moorrees receives three Javanese letters from DR II, forwarding the originals and Dutch translations to Daendels. Recognizing that it is impossible to coerce the Patih to accompany him to Semarang while Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] is present, Moorrees resolves to depart for Semarang that very evening.
* Royal Audience and Detained Surakarta Heralds (11 Jumadilakir / 13 Hooimaand 1810, Friday / Translation by P.D. Stekkinger): DR II writes to Driessen reminding him of Moorrees' letter of 6 Jumadilakir begging for an interview with HB II before the Sultan's departure for Samas.
* HB II had consented to receive Moorrees upon his return from Samas, on condition that Sunan Pakubuwana IV's letter and the Surakarta royal heralds (*gandhêk*) were formally admitted to the Kraton in accordance with custom.
* Crisis of Absence: HB II returns from Samas, but Moorrees has fled to Semarang. DR II urgently presses Driessen: if the Sultan asks why the Surakarta letters have not been delivered, what is the Patih to answer? DR II protests that Moorrees refused to reply despite the Surakarta heralds having waited in vain for an extended period, begging Driessen for an immediate resolution.
