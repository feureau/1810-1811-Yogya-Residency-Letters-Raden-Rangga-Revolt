### Header & Archival Classification

* **Far-Left Margin Annotation (Blue Ink running vertically along margin line):** Daendels' note to Surabaya regarding the royal state barge (*prahu pangulu*) which he had forbidden HB II to use on the Bengawan Solo river.
* **Physical Characteristics:** Lined notebook leaf verso/continuation, 33 blue ruled horizontal lines, red vertical margin line; 2 punch holes at margin edge; blue ballpoint pen text with red ink headings. Contains Daendels' comprehensive 21-point intelligence questionnaire to Pieter Engelhard and Van Braam regarding court factions, the royal women, and the impending submission of Radèn Rongga.
