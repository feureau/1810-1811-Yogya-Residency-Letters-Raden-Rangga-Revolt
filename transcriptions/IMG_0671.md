### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Shipyard Raid at Telang**<br>Late Nov. – Dec. 1810<br>(Tuban/Blora Border)<br><br>*Verhaagen Plundered* | Raid on the timber establishments of Mr. Verhaagen [naval timber shipyard overseer]:<br>• Located at Desa Telang on the borders of Tuban, Blora, and Padangan, near the Rembang timber reserve of Raajverlang [Rappeling], where a new government naval shipyard was being established.<br>• Rebel raiding party assaults Telang and expropriates all government supplies and personal effects belonging to Verhaagen.<br>• **Assault Commanders:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• 2 Radèns from Grobogan: **Radèn Somitra** and **Radèn Mertakusuma**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• 5 Radèns from Madiun: including **Radèn Wirawijaya** and **Radèn Cakrawinaya**.<br>• The local chief (*Ngabèhi*) of Telang initially submits to Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] and swears a coerced oath of allegiance, but subsequently flees to the coastal post of Bancar.<br>• Reports note uncertainty whether Rangga was personally present in the assault column or operating from his rear headquarters. |
| **Blora Bupati Intelligence**<br>Early Dec. 1810<br><br>*Forest Dwellers Queried* | The newly appointed Tumênggung of Blora arrives on site from Surakarta [Sala]:<br>• Unable to elicit reliable intelligence from the forest inhabitants (*wong kalang* / forest dwellers) of Lasem and Tuban regarding Rangga's precise position.<br>• Suspicions raised that timber workers and forest communities are actively sheltering or assisting Rangga.<br>• Dispatch copies forwarded to Commissioner Jacob Andries van Braam [J.A. van Braam], Willem Nicolaas Servatius [W.N. Servatius], and the Residents at the royal courts. |
| **Mangkunagaran Legion Mobilized**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Lt. Col. Smith Command* | Lieutenant-Colonel Smith and the Legion of Pangéran Prangwedana [Mangkunagara II] intercept dispatches and deploy auxiliary detachments across the border roads.<br>• **Postscript (P.S.):** Danjing, a Chinese merchant from Lasem left in charge during Verhaagen's absence, allegedly permitted the pilfering and expropriation of government timber goods at Telang without resistance. |
