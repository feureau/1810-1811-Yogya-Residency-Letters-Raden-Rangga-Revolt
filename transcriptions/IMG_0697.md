But Raja Putra Narendra not mentioned in letter. | **Danureja II to Pieter Engelhard, 28 April 1811 (4 Rabingulakir AJ 1738):**<br>A letter from Sunan Pakubuwana IV to Hamengkubuwana II was delivered by a *Priyayi Lurah Gandhèk* [court courier], making no mention of Raja Putra Narendra. |

| **DR II - Eng., 3 May 1811 / 8 Rabingulakir 1738:**<br>Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels] asks RPN to deliver *Garuti* Threads for *Oosthoek*. 25% advance to be paid. 2 ¾ silver money per *pikul*.<br>RPN orders Prawirasentika / Dipakusuma [Pangéran Dipakusuma] to supply *Garuti* from E. MN [East Mancanagara] from *Arèn* Trees. | **Danureja II to Pieter Engelhard, 3 May 1811 (8 Rabingulakir AJ 1738):**<br>Daendels requests that Raja Putra Narendra supply *garuti* [sugar-palm] fiber threads for the *Oosthoek* [Eastern Salient], with a 25% advance at 2¾ silver money per *pikul*. Raja Putra Narendra orders Prawirasentika and Dipakusuma to supply *garuti* from *arèn* trees in East Mancanagara. |
| **Raja Putra Narendra to Eng., 4 May 1811 / 9 Rabingulakir 1738:**<br>Letter of Eng to RPN, 30 Apr. 1811 postponing boundary / frontier + new demarcation until after British had mounted attack on Colony.<br>Daendels asks RPN to keep subjects in best order / peace:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• that if on prompting of HB II robberies take place in VL [*Vorstenlanden*] = after Dealing + English — D [Daendels] would turn on those involved + would come to Yogya with as many troops as previous time.<br>RPN does not suspect HB II of such activities / gives necessary orders to DR II.<br>Daendels knows about happenings at Yogya court — prayers of *Hajis* + assembling of large numbers of *prajurits* at *Garebeg Mulud*. | **Raja Putra Narendra to Pieter Engelhard, 4 May 1811 (9 Rabingulakir AJ 1738):**<br>Acknowledges Engelhard's letter of 30 April postponing the new frontier demarcation until after the British attack on the colony.<br>Daendels urges Raja Putra Narendra to keep his subjects in peace and order, warning that if robberies occur in the *Vorstenlanden* at Hamengkubuwana II's instigation, after dealing with the English Daendels would turn on those responsible and march on Yogyakarta with as many troops as before.<br>Raja Putra Narendra states he does not suspect Hamengkubuwana II of such plots, has issued the necessary orders to Danureja II, and notes that Daendels is already informed of court events including the *Hajis*' prayers and the large assembly of *prajurits* at *Garebeg Mulud*. |
