### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HB II Edict Dispatched**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br><br>*MN Bupatis Alerted* | Royal order (*ordonnanz*) of Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] for the arrest of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] forwarded urgently to all bupatis of the Mangkunagaran [MN]. |
| **Danukrama Alerts Engelhard**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br>(3:30 a.m.)<br><br>*[Earlier than prior letters]*<br>[Eng - Daendels] | DR II's [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] scribe Kyai Ngabèhi Danukrama [Danukrama / later Radèn Tumenggung Danuningrat I] arrives at 3:30 a.m. to inform Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng]:<br>• Danukrama was just informed by Radèn Aria Sindunegara [Sindunagara / later Danureja III], who obtained intelligence from Mangkunagaran sources, that Rangga has fled Yogyakarta.<br>• Resident Engelhard awaits HB II's formal decision regarding the dispatch of the punitive pursuit expedition. |
| **Servatius to Engelhard**<br>22 Nov. 1810<br>(Surakarta) | Willem Nicolaas Servatius [W.N. Servatius] to Resident Engelhard:<br>• Official warning letters dispatched to all Surakarta [Sala] and Mangkunagaran regents commanding border vigilance. |
| **Lt. Paulus to Engelhard**<br>22 Nov. 1810<br>(Dispatch No. 107)<br>Dated: Pakis<br><br>*(Red vertical rule:*<br>*Forest difficulties)* | Lieutenant Paulus [Lt. Paulus] to Resident Engelhard from Pakis:<br>• Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura [R.T. Purwadipura] refuses to take responsibility for feeding and maintaining the 9 European cavalry dragoon horses attached to the expedition (out of 16 horses in total).<br>• Purwadipura states he will provide grass but refuses to cover the operational costs.<br>• **Crisis in Teak Woods:** The column is in great difficulties in the middle of dense forests; there are no Chinese merchants available to lend ready cash or provide provisions. |
| **Engelhard to Paulus**<br>24 Nov. 1810<br><br>*(Double red margin quotes:*<br>*Imperial Title)* | Resident Engelhard sends emergency funds to Lt. Paulus.<br>• **Imperial Pretension:** Radèn Rongga formally changes his name and assumes the royal messianic title: **Kangjeng Susuhunan Prabu Bragalaja** in order to attract a wider religious and popular following across the eastern districts. |
| **Hallwachs to Winckelmann**<br>28 Nov. 1810<br>(Semarang Command) | Lieutenant-Colonel Hallwachs to Brigadier F.C.P. von Winckelmann:<br>• Radèn Rongga proclaims that he will march to attack the *Pasisir* [northern coastal] districts.<br>• Landdrost Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst [van Pabst / van Haak] of Japara is alerted to the impending coastal threat.<br>• **Entrenchment at Pete:** Rangga is strongly entrenched at Pete (near Wirasari); a colonial and royal force of 4,000 men is mobilizing and marching to attack him. |
