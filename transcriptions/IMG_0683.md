### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Center (Red Bleed-Through):** Reverse show-through of `2 Jan. - 20 Feb. 1811`
* **Margin (Left):** `HB II to Daendels 4 Jan. 1811 / 8 Besar 1737` / `Jav. letter. Proclamation of Kangjeng Raja 9 Sura 1738, 20 Feb. 1811` / `Similarly letter No. 20 / No. 37` / `? Muh. Idris See Ricklefs p. 154`
* **Document Subject:** Roster of Outer Regents and Mangkunagaran regents; HB II's 4 Jan. acceptance of Daendels's terms; the Prince Regent's 20 Feb. 1811 attempt to style himself Sultan (*Kangjeng Raja*) and opposition from Mangkudiningrat and Abubakar; competing troop recruitments; HB II's *sêdêkah* gatherings with Tuwan Haji Idris and 40 *hajis*; and the unresolved dispute over Rangga's and Purwadipura's confiscated property.
* **Physical Characteristics:** Ruled white notebook folio paper; two punch holes on right margin with rust stains; blue ink handwriting with red ink cross-reference to *Babad Bêdhah*, double vertical margin marks, and underline.
