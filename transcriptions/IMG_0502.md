## Metadata

- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek 1602–1811*, vol. XVI [1808–1811], ed. J.A. van der Chijs [Batavia: Landsdrukkerij, 1897], pp. 74–75: Government Decrees of H.W. Daendels, 10 February 1810)
- **Date Range:** 1810-02-10
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Continuation and statutory terms of Governor-General Daendels' comprehensive intervention into the debt crisis of Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II) of Surakarta. Condemns unscrupulous private moneylenders (*geldschieters*) for extracting extortionate interest from the prince; enforces the statutory colonial interest cap of 9% per annum (*negen ten honderd 's jaars*); mandates that all creditors submit their loan notes (*schuldbrieven*) to the Minister at Surakarta within one month on penalty of complete forfeiture (*nulliteit*); recalculates and caps all arrears and future interest at 6% per annum (*zes ten honderd 's jaars*); and appropriates the 39,800 Spanish Dollars (*Spaansche matten*) silver inheritance held by the estate executors of the late Governor and Resident Johan Frederik Baron van Reede tot de Parkeler into the government treasury to fund structured, state-guaranteed debenture repayments.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II / Ario Praboe Prang Wedono), Johan Frederik Baron van Reede tot de Parkeler.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Souracarta), Batavia, Java.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek*, Sprokkelmaand (February), Spaansche matten / Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.), schuldbrieven (promissory notes), schuldbewijzen (debentures), interest caps (9% statutory / 6% restructured), Van Reede inheritance fund.
