| **18 Nov. 1811 — Eng - Dutch (L.W. Meyer, *Hol. Secretaris van het Britsche Gouvernement*):**<br>(Thurs.) **15 Nov. 1811**: Crawfurd [John Crawfurd] arrives from Sala; letter of 25 Oct. 1811 instructs Eng to give over Post to Crawfurd.<br><span style="color:#a00">**Eng. Oct-Nov 1811:**</span> Engelhard in POOR Health, NOT able to WORK or WRITE -> sec. [Secretary] has to write letter; thanks Raffles for Relieving him of Post. Very sick during Period at Residency, for some time NO correspondence + Princes / HB II; NOT in a position to get to know the Personalities of the COURT at all well.<br>*(Margin: Since period of Daendels NOT custom to write long memories van overgave)*: **Waterloo** because of long stay in Yogya (as 2nd Res. then 1st Res.) in a much better position to give Information: *Mémoires* left in Archives, also Eng adds personal elucidations.<br>Eng: SUFFERING from a weak Heart; arming of Res. House / garden by Cannon (*Besluit of 7 Sept. 1810*); would spend Rainy Season in Semarang, too weak to travel over Land. | **18 November 1811 — Engelhard to L.W. Meyer (Dutch Secretary to British Govt.):**<br>John Crawfurd arrived from Surakarta on 15 November with Raffles's 25 October order to hand over the Residency. Engelhard, too ill to write himself, thanks Raffles for relieving him, noting his prolonged sickness prevented correspondence with the princes and Hamengkubuwana II or deep acquaintance with court personalities. Citing Waterloo's longer tenure and archival *mémoires*, Engelhard notes he suffers from a weak heart, armed the Residency with cannon (per 7 Sept. 1810 decree), and will spend the rainy season in Semarang, being too weak for overland travel. |
| **14 Oct. 1811 — Eng - Capt. W. Robison of British Commission:**<br>Band of 40 Jav. Bandits below walls of Fort at Klathèn storm a House belonging to a pensioned Sergeant (11 Oct.). DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] summoned by Eng but says that he could NOT come. Strange Happenings here (Robber Bands): *"I do not know what to ascribe them to"* -> Letter from Res. of Sala, but difficult to take action because of juxtaposition of lands. | **14 October 1811 — Engelhard to Capt. W. Robison:**<br>On 11 October, a band of 40 Javanese bandits stormed a pensioned sergeant's house beneath the walls of Klathèn Fort. Danureja II failed to appear when summoned; Engelhard notes difficulty acting across intermingled Surakarta–Yogyakarta borderlands. |
