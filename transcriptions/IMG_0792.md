## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **ROBINSON / 25 Sept 1811** *(red)* | Continuation of Captain William Robison's Yogyakarta report (`25 September 1811`). |
| Delicate / Troublesome Task for the minister of Yogya.<br>To Keep balance between Sultan + son the Regent who live in the same Dalěm<br>HB II allowed precedence / + all Honours of Superiority<br>But admin [administration] wholly in hands of Regent.<br>*(pencil right: `out see letter Eng. to Raffles money`)* | The Resident at Yogyakarta faced a delicate and troublesome task: maintaining the balance between Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and his son, the Prince Regent [HB III], living in the same *Dalem* (Kraton). HB II retained precedence and all honours of superiority, while administration lay wholly in the hands of the Regent. |
| So jealous is old Sultan of prerogatives. that constantly interfering + Regent who is too weak + Fearful to Resist.<br>Frequent inconveniences / his commands.<br>and Troubles ensue though they may appear in public the most cordial Friends. | The old Sultan was so jealous of his prerogatives that he constantly interfered with the Regent, who was too weak and fearful to resist, causing frequent inconveniences and troubles even though they appeared in public as the most cordial friends. |
| Eng. [Pieter Engelhard] considers it unsafe to Restore the old Sultan + Bring back NK / ND [Natakusuma / Natadiningrat] From Surobaya.<br>But acknowledges cruel unfairness of NK / ND's exile. | Resident Pieter Engelhard considered it unsafe to restore the old Sultan [HB II] or bring back Pangéran Natakusuma and Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat from Surabaya, though he acknowledged the cruel unfairness of their exile. |
| HB II Represented as extremely Head strong<br>Difficult to Deal with: cause of Daendels measure against Him. | HB II was represented as extremely headstrong and difficult to deal with, which had provoked Marshal Herman Willem Daendels' measures against him. |
| *(red margin: `Foreshadows policy of the Raffles..`)* "Son on contrary more pliant + if est. [established] Beyond the Reach of the Sultan Things would go well enough." | *(Margin: Foreshadows Raffles' policy)* — *"Son on contrary more pliant and if established beyond the reach of the Sultan things would go well enough."* |
| Rob [Robison] begs HB II to put Request in writing | Robison urged HB II to put his requests in writing. |
| Eng. [Pieter Engelhard] Bad Health Consumption.<br>But exceedingly fit for Function - gives satisfaction at the Court. | Pieter Engelhard suffered from bad health (consumption), but was exceedingly fit for his function and gave satisfaction at the court. |
| `HB II / Regent` = Request: Return NK / ND [Natakusuma / Natadiningrat]<br>Eng [Pieter Engelhard] - counsels against Return.<br>But income from lands to be remitted to Surabaya. | Both HB II and the Regent requested the return of Pangéran Natakusuma and Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat. Engelhard counselled against their return, recommending instead that the income from their lands be remitted to Surabaya. |
