# Transcription of IMG_0521_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0521_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 / Character Appraisal of Radèn Rongga)
- **Date Range:** 1810-04-28 to 1810-05-02
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 detailing Dutch administrative correspondence and a famous contemporary colonial character appraisal of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III; opens with Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels deferring military intervention to let Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and Sunan Pakubuwana IV appoint a joint commission of inquiry; records HB II defending Radèn Rongga, noting that based on Rongga's report he acted fully within his legal rights to eliminate three notorious bandits (*perampok*), refusing to grant satisfaction to Surakarta without contradictory proof; highlights a remarkable contemporary Dutch assessment of Rongga's character: "a proud man, but for a Javanese has a very lively intelligence (*zeer vlug en vatbaar van begrip is*), considered in Yogya to be a very good Regent who never extorts his subjects, is well-disposed toward Europeans, and bold enough to challenge even his father-in-law HB II" (referencing Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk's 1799 reports); tracks dispatches between Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees and Willem Nicolaas Servatius on 28–29 April 1810 regarding Servatius' refusal to furnish a copy of Pakubuwana IV's letter to Daendels; concludes on 1–2 May with HB II ordering Radèn Adipati Danureja II to cooperate with Moorrees, and Moorrees requesting an audience with the Sultan to present Pakubuwana IV's complaint concerning the Panaraga border attack.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Wouter Hendrik van IJsseldijk, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Radèn Adipati Danureja II.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Panaraga (Ponorogo), Madiun.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46, Character appraisal (*karakteristiek*), Extortion of subjects (*kecu / perampok*), Dutch-Javanese relations, Joint court inquiry commission, Accession resistance (1799), Bilateral residency diplomacy.
