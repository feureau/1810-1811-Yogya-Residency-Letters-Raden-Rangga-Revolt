## Transcription of Manuscript Text

### Main Essay Text
```text
for religious tolerance can be associated with political and social needs in terms of ensuring the maintenance of a large workforce. This is particularly true in the case of Pennsylvania whereby the immigration by persecuted minorities, particularly German Protestants and French Huguenots was actively encouraged in order to maintain a workforce of indentured white servants and not become subject to the mass appropriation of black slaves which was proving destabilizing in neighbouring New York. In addition to this, tolerance of religious minorities in the British American colonies can be seen as a direct reflection of British policy and encouragement, thus further implying that religious tolerance, when seen as socially and politically beneficial or when part of a ideology, can result as much from strong as from weak government.

To understand religious tolerance, it is thus important to contextualise the nature of a political machinery or a society. Although defined religious intolerance can be associated with increased sophistication of a persecuting mechanism, religious tolerance - defined by legislation or legislation such as in the colonies, can also result from decisive political action of or communitarian decision. In each case therefore, the nature of the tolerated - what they have to offer or what they threats they may cause to society in terms of political of doctrinal action - must be taken into account as much as the nature of tolerance itself.
```
