| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ngawi Tollgate Strategic Value**<br>Late Nov. 1810<br><br>*Chinese Bandar* | Intelligence on Rangga's strategic positioning:<br>• Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] remains headquartered at Maospati, but spends substantial time at Desa Ngawi, site of a lucrative and heavily trafficked Chinese tollgate (*bandar*).<br>• Speculation whether Rangga will advance eastward along the Bengawan Solo [Sala River] toward Surabaya, or double back westward along the *Pasisir* [coast] to rally support from Blora (currently quiescent).<br>• A Chinese emissary from Maospati arrives but provides inconclusive information when interrogated.<br>• **Postscript:** Blora reinforcements under a newly appointed Tumênggung fall into Rangga's hands; Rangga releases them unharmed to return home, having ties with the elder Tumênggung line rather than the new appointee. |
| **Engelhard Refuses Coup**<br>30 Nov. 1810<br><br>*Daendels's Fury* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng] to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels]:<br>• Resident Engelhard boldly states that he is **not prepared to take action or orchestrate an arrest against Sultan HB II** [Hamengkubuwana II].<br>• Cites the Sultan's cooperative, peaceful behavior and prompt settlement of grievances, insisting that colonial and royal forces must maintain absolute unity to suppress Rangga.<br>• **Daendels's Scathing Marginal Note:** Daendels writes angrily in the margin, commanding that the ultimatum be handed to HB II immediately. Daendels holds the Sultan directly culpable for Rangga's rebellion: *"Even before the flight his actions were equivocal."* |
| **Christiaans to Daendels**<br>1 Dec. 1810<br>(Wintermaand)<br><br>*Summary Execution* | A.M. Christiaans to Marshal Daendels:<br>• **Execution by Firing Squad (*Fusilading*):** Reports the summary military execution in Semarang of the Yogyakarta village head / tax collector (*bèkêl*) of Desa Gabus (located in Demak), shot for suspected complicity with the rebels.<br>• Two regents are dispatched into Grobogan to reconnoiter rebel positions and report on Rangga's true strength, though incoming reports remain highly conflicting. |
