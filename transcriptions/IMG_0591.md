### Email Body (Peter Brown to Peter Carey)
```text
Dear Peter,

The correct text is clearly 'Nulla salus bello; pax optima rerum' ('There is no safety [or 'deliverance'] in war; peace
is the best of things').

This is a cento of phrases from two authors of Latin epic: 'nulla salus bello' is from Virgil, Aeneid 11.362 (repeated
at 399); 'pax optima rerum' from Silius Italicus, Punica 11.592.

If you look up each phrase separately on Google, you will see that they are (or have been) both quite well known. But
Google offers no hits for the combination of the two.

Best wishes,

Peter
```

At 11:22 09/08/2006, you wrote:
