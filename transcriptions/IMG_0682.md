### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Center (Red Ink):** `2 Jan. - 20 Feb. 1811`
* **Top Right:** `AN 'Djokjo Brieven' no. 46 R. Ronggo 1810-11`
* **Margin (Left):** `Daendels to HB II, 2 Jan. 1811 (cont.)` / *(Red Ink)*: `New arrangements for Kraton.` / `DR II to Daendels, 8 Besar 1737, 4 Jan. 1811.`
* **Document Subject:** Daendels's four strict constitutional conditions stripping Sultan HB II of executive authority within 24 hours; the 196,320 Sp. D. (*ronde maatten*) gratification payment for Batavia troops; Daendels's 4 Jan. mission to Surakarta; and Danureja II's report on the formal notification of the *Putra Sentana* and Inner Regents regarding HB II's abdication.
* **Physical Characteristics:** Ruled white notebook folio paper; two binder punch holes on left margin with rust halos; blue ink handwriting with red ink headings, double margin vertical emphasis marks, and numbered conditions `(1)`–`(4)`.
