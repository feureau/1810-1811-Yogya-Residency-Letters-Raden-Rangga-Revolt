The garden featured an illumination reading **"Leve de Keizer"** ("Long Live the Emperor"). Hamengkubuwana II and the Regent (wearing his diamond star) came to the Residency House for the feast; the Regent watched the fireworks in the garden while Hamengkubuwana II excused himself and stayed in the Throne Room with the civilian families amid blue illuminations. |
