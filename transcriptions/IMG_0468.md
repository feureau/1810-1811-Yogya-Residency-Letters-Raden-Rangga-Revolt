### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Jacob Andries van Braam** (`van Braam`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 307). Resident at Surakarta (1808–1810, 1811) who complained about unregistered wandering Islamic scholars/priests entering the royal capitals.
  * **Louis Napoléon** (`Louis Napoléon`): King of Holland (1806–1810) under Napoleonic suzerainty; brother of Emperor Napoleon Bonaparte.
  * **Chinese Captain of Kedu / Yogya** (`Capitan Cina Kedhu / Yogya (Magelang)`): Listed under *Chinese Captains of Yogya* (lines 193, 194). Chinese officers administered local Chinese communities and tax farms under residency jurisdiction.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Surakarta** (`Solo`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 393). Capital of the Sunanate.
  * **Yogyakarta** (`Yogya`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 392). Capital of the Sultanate.
  * **Batavia**: Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 394). Colonial capital of the Netherlands Indies.
  * **Semarang**: Listed under *Colonial Officials / Places* (lines 210, 296). North coast capital; location of the regional Chinese hospital.
  * **Kèdhu (Magelang)** (`Kedhu (Magelang)`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (lines 411, 421). Central residency and regency capital.
  * **pasisir**: Listed under *Administrative & Geographic Terms* (lines 539, 564). Northern coastal littoral under direct colonial administration.
* **Terms:**
  * **Lentenmaand, Oogstmaand, Herfstmaand**: Agricultural/revolutionary calendar months (March, August, September) adopted under Daendels (see `IMG_0465.md`).
  * **Rijksdaalder (Rix dollar)** (`Rijksdaalders / Rds`): Listed under *Currencies* (line 676). Standard Dutch silver currency / paper currency valued at Dfl 2.40.
  * **last**: Listed under *Weights & Measures* (line 173). Bulk unit of weight/volume for grain and rice, approximately equal to 3,000 Dutch pounds (~1,500 kg).
  * **priests (santri / pradikan / ulama)**: Wandering Islamic students, teachers, and mendicant religious figures subject to Dutch colonial surveillance in the Principalities (see Appendix 7a/7b, lines 555–170).
