# Transcription of IMG_0537_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0537_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-07-08 to 1810-07-13 (Javanese: Sun. 6 Jumadilakir 1737 [8 July 1810] to Fri. 11 Jumadilakir 1737 [13 July 1810]; citing 7 Jumadilakir / 9 July 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Fear of the Sultan's Wrath (6 Jumadilakir / 8 July 1810, Sunday): Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees urges Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to lobby the Crown Prince (CP [later Hamengkubuwana III]) to convince Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] to sanction Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III's immediate mission to Japan [Mojokerto].
* Moorrees returns a formal missive reinforcing this demand, but both Rongga and DR II, together with all court regents, are utterly terrified to deliver the request to the monarch (*Rangga / HB II afraid to tell HB II / Refusal to tell any*). Radèn Tumenggung Pringgakusuma is scheduled for dispatch to the Eastern Mancanagara on the following Monday.
* Prohibition on Sumadiningrat's Mediation (7 Jumadilakir / 9 July 1810, Monday): Moorrees' dispatch of 6 Jumadilakir is formally laid before HB II. The Sultan sternly forbids Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat from visiting the Residency to discuss political state affairs, declaring that political diplomacy is outside his remit. HB II reasserts that Moorrees must deliver all Surakarta missives strictly according to ancient customary ceremonial (*adat*).
* Danureja II's Distress Following Moorrees' Abandonment (11 Jumadilakir / 13 July 1810, Friday / DR II to Acting Resident Driessen):
  - Moorrees' prior dispatch requested an audience with HB II before the Sultan departed for his seaside retreat at Samas.
  - The Sultan had agreed to receive the Resident upon his return, provided the Surakarta missives and royal heralds (*gandhêk*) were received at court.
  - In the interim, Moorrees has secretly evacuated Yogyakarta for Semarang, leaving First Clerk Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen in charge.
  - Upon HB II's return from Samas, DR II is placed in an intolerable dilemma: if the Sultan summons Moorrees and inquires why the Surakarta heralds are still detained, how is DR II to answer? Protesting that the heralds have languished in Yogya for weeks, DR II frantically implores Driessen for an immediate written reply.
