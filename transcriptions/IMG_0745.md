| **Eng - Janssens [Pieter Engelhard to Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens], 5 Sept. 1811** *(See other letter 5 Sept.; AN 'Djokjo Brieven' no. 22)*:<br>Troops march to Semarang yesterday evening at 7 o'clock.<br>DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] speaks to Eng about **Natakusuma** [Pangéran Natakusuma] in name of Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III]: urges Janssens not to let him fall into the hands of the English *(which)* would be very disadvantageous for the peace of the colony.<br>Regent wishes to dispel favourable impression of the Prince entertained in some European circles. Asks Janssens for information on NK / ND [Natakusuma / Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] to pass on to Regent. | **Pieter Engelhard to Governor-General Janssens, 5 September 1811** *(AN Djokjo Brieven no. 22)*:<br>The Yogyakarta troops marched for Semarang yesterday evening at 7:00 p.m.<br>Danureja II spoke to Engelhard on the Regent's behalf, urging Janssens not to let Natakusuma fall into English hands, as that would gravely threaten the colony's peace; the Regent seeks to counter favorable impressions of Natakusuma in European circles and requests updates on Natakusuma and Natadiningrat. |
| Janssens's letter of 3 Sept. asks Eng to make representations to HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] to maintain Regent's peace.<br>Refusal of Janssens to permit one of Princes of the Blood as commander of Troops in Semarang because fears his ambitions — fears would make move on the Throne.<br><span style="color:#a00">|| ? Idea of **Mangkudiningrat** as army commander.</span><br>Regent can place no Trust in his Brothers (espc. MD [Mangkudiningrat]); must be on his guard. Regent cannot take a step without permission of his Father.<br>If HB II chose an army commander, MD = would certainly be MD [Mangkudiningrat] as his oldest and so people say most beloved son.<br>`||` Shrewd + distinguished Prince — not without reason feels he had been treated unjustly; does not become CP [Crown Prince] after Ratu Mas demoted to 2nd wife; feels more right to the Throne than Regent. | Janssens's 3 September letter asks Engelhard to urge Hamengkubuwana II to preserve peace with the Regent, and refuses to allow any Prince of the Blood to command the Semarang force, fearing royal ambitions against the throne *(red note: idea of Mangkudiningrat as army commander)*.<br>The Regent cannot trust his brothers—especially Mangkudiningrat—and cannot act without his father's permission. Were Hamengkubuwana II to choose a commander, he would certainly pick Mangkudiningrat, his eldest and reputedly favorite son—a shrewd, distinguished prince who feels unjustly passed over for Crown Prince after Ratu Mas was demoted to second wife, and believes he has a stronger claim to the throne than the Regent. |
