# Transcription of IMG_0505_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0505_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 27: Archival Research Notes on Yogyakarta Residency Letters, March 1810)
- **Date Range:** 1810-03-10 to 1810-03-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Escalating crisis between Acting Resident J.W. Moorrees, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, and Patih Danureja II regarding cross-border banditry and political fugitives along the Pekalongan frontier. Moorrees presses HB II to arrest and surrender the leader of a notorious bandit gang based at desa Wanadadi who plundered Tersana; Daendels orders road blockades and demands that HB II compel Radèn Dêmang Tirtawijaya to travel from Pekalongan to Yogya to answer for these crimes; Moorrees warns HB II that the colonial administration has completely lost confidence in the Sultan and Patih Danureja II due to premature disclosures that allowed fugitives to escape; and records Daendels' dismissal of the Patih of Surakarta (Radèn Adipati Mangkupraja II) for resisting Dutch directives, followed by the appointment and confirmation of Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II) as the new Patih of Surakarta.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Radèn Dêmang Tirtawijaya, Radèn Adipati Mangkupraja II (dismissed Patih of Solo), Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II / new Patih of Solo).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Surakarta (Sala), Pekalongan, desa Wanadadi, Tersana.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Djokjo Brieven no. 27, Lentenmaand (March), Dêmang, Patih of Yogyakarta, Patih of Surakarta, cross-border banditry.
