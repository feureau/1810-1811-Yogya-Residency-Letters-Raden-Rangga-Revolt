| **14 Oct. 1811:** Impossibility because of sickness to carry out conference with the Patih [Radèn Adipati Danureja II]; does not know what to ascribe events to.<br><span style="color:#a00">**(Circled) 31 Oct. 1811 — Eng [Pieter Engelhard] - Raffles [Thomas Stamford Raffles]:**</span><br>News of dismissal of **DR II / DK** [Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I].<br>**SINDUNEGARA** [Kyai Adipati Danureja III]: a very <span style="color:#a00">'good' (Brave) v. old</span> man but who cannot function as 1st outer Regent for some months because of sickness *(see over copy of letter)*. But breaks **1792 Contract** + HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]; Refusal of Engelhard to accept the News. | **14 Oct. & 31 Oct. 1811 — Engelhard to Raffles:**<br>Illness prevented a conference with the Patih. On 31 October, Engelhard received news of the dismissal of Danureja II and Danukusuma, and the appointment of **Sindunagara**—described in red as a *"good (brave), very old"* man unable to serve for months due to illness. Because this violated the 1792 Treaty, Engelhard refused to accept the notification. |
| **4 Nov. — Eng -> Dutch Sec. Gen. of British Govnmt.:**<br>Letter of 21 Oct. from Dutch Sec.: Lt. G.G. [Raffles] appoints **H. Hope** Civil Commissioner E. G.G. asks for List of European Inhabitants in Yogya to say how long in Fr. [French] service etc., **to give up seals of French government.** | **4 November 1811 — Engelhard to Dutch Secretary-General:**<br>Acknowledges 21 October dispatch appointing **H. Hope** Civil Commissioner for the Eastern Districts; requests a roster of European inhabitants in Yogyakarta (noting length of French service) and orders the surrender of all French government seals. |
