| :--- | :--- |
| `No time to call cavalry from Surabaya.`<br>`Requests of Sunan to Robison:`<br>2. `Return of graves on N. [North] coast`: Sunan [Pakubuwana IV] Returns to situation pre `1757` — Demands cannot be admitted. Places to be Respected by Brit. [British] govnmt. [government] + Sunan's subjects to be allowed to visit them.<br>`p. 31` `3. Strandgeld not to be returned / given.` Of good political measure of Raffles, Sunan not to be allowed to claim title to *pasisir* areas. | **Raffles' Policy on PB IV's Requests (pp. 31–33):**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Graves (Art. 2):** Pre-1757 demands cannot be admitted, though the British Government will respect the graves and allow the Sunan's subjects to visit them.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• ***Strandgeld* (Art. 3):** Not to be restored, as Raffles deems it sound policy not to let the Sunan claim any title to *pasisir* (coastal) areas. |
| `p. 32` *(red `*>`)* `Question of Robison's authority to act at courts`:<br>No authority from Ld. [Lord] Minto, see de Haan. Instruction from Sir Samuel Auchmuty could not extend to places in question.<br>Difficulties at courts due to unguarded / improper conduct of Robison.<br>Only instruction from Minto on leaving Batavia to render assistance in Dutch language on further instructions from Sr. [Senior] officer Col. Wood.<br>`p. 33`: Would not Recommend Robison's appointment as Resident at court. Refusal to acknowledge all agreements made by Robison + Sunan.<br>Farms to be continued to be collected by Resident. Sunan to pay for upkeep of European military force used as Bodyguard. | **Disavowal of Captain William Robison (pp. 32–33):** Notes that Robison had no authority from Lord Minto to negotiate at the courts (his instructions from Sir Samuel Auchmuty did not extend to Surakarta/Yogyakarta, and Minto had only instructed him to assist in Dutch under Colonel Wood). Raffles refuses to recommend Robison as Resident, disavows all agreements Robison made with the Sunan, and confirms that farms will continue to be collected by the Resident while the Sunan pays for the upkeep of his European military bodyguard. |
