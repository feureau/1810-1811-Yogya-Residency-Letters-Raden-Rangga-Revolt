Most powerful Raja Karang Asem — possesses Island of Lombok.<br>Raffles' idea of getting `15,000 men` from Bali + fresh provisions and livestock.<br>Raja sends Black Horse + 5 year old boy `Ketot Jobeling`. | **25 February 1811 (1 Sapar AH 1226) — Raja Gusti Wiyahan Karangasem / Raja of Bali Buleleng (Singaraja) to Thomas Stamford Raffles:** Lists Bali's 8 principalities (Karangasem also holding Lombok); Raffles hopes to obtain `15,000` men, provisions, and livestock from Bali. The Raja sends a black horse and a 5-year-old boy, Ketut Jobeling. |

Four letters from Raffles (for the Sunan, Sultan, Sultan of Madura, and Panembahan of Sumenep) were forwarded from Bali to Java. *(Left margin, `p. 59` / `p. 64`, nos. 3–4: `Ratu Gusti Angloreh Gusti Agung Jaměrang to Raffles, 14 Rabingulawal / 8 Apr. 1811`, sending a young Balinese virgin, & `Ratu Gusti Wiyahan Karang Asem to Raffles, 27 Rab. 1226 / 21 Apr. 1811`, wanting back lands of Blambangan, Besuki, Probolinggo.)* |
