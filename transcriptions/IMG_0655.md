### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rangga at Kèrtasana**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Pillage of Regency* | Rangga's evasive march eastward:<br>• Marches through Suka Bêndha and Katangga [underlined in red], lodging overnight with the Chinese merchant Kang.<br>• Enters Kèrtasana: Finds the regency palace (*dalèm*) abandoned by Radèn Tumênggung Pringgalaya; Rangga's men plunder the compound thoroughly.<br>• Moves to Munpèng [Munèng] and occupies the Chinese tollgate (*bandhar*) for two nights.<br>• Pringgalaya had fled into the surrounding jungle with his wife and family; when Leberfeld's column arrives, Pringgalaya emerges from hiding to attach his force to the royal army.<br>• Rangga intended to seek refuge in Wirasaba, but discovers colonial defenses there are too strong; consequently diverts toward Jipang.<br>• Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara evacuates Munèng barely three hours before Leberfeld's arrival. |
| **Gaupp to Paulus**<br>16 Dec. 1810<br>(Bureau van Oorlog) | Brigadier Gaupp (Commander of the Oosthoek, War Department) to Lt. Thomas Paulus (16 Dec. 1810):<br>• Demands immediate situational updates regarding Purwadipura [PWD]'s column.<br>• Forbids Paulus from permitting Purwadipura to conduct independent military maneuvers near Dutch colonial territory. |
| **Gaupp to Purwadipura**<br>16 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Oosthoek Boundary Warning* | Brigadier Gaupp's stern diplomatic warning to Purwadipura:<br>• The Dutch Brigadier bluntly notifies Purwadipura that **he and his royal expeditionary force may not set foot upon Government territory in the Oosthoek [Eastern Salient]**.<br>• Any unauthorized crossing of the colonial frontier will be treated immediately as an open **act of war** against the Netherlands. |
| **Magetan Front**<br>9 Dec. 1810<br>(11 Dulkangidah 1737) | Radèn Tumênggung Sasradipura and Radèn Tumênggung Sasrakusuma to DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II]:<br>• DR II orders loyal regents to reclaim all lost ground by launching an immediate assault on rebel concentrations around Madiun.<br>• 200 fresh royal *prajurit* arrive from Yogyakarta under Ngabèhi Jayasêntika, Tumênggung Kèrtadirja, and Tumênggung Alap-alap.<br>• The regents confer at Pacalan with Jayasêntika to coordinate an offensive against Rangga's brother, Radèn Prawirapreja, whose rebel detachment holds positions north of Magetan.<br>• Confirms Rangga fled Madiun on 3 Dec. with 90 followers heading toward Nêntul east of the Solo River. |
