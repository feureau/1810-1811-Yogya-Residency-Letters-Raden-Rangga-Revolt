### Physical Artifact Description
1. **Paper Leaf:**
   * Blank verso face of the contemporary 19th-century royal Yogyakarta kraton manuscript leaf transcribed in `IMG_0597_rotated_90.jpg`.
   * Uninscribed cream rag paper surface showing laid paper structure and center horizontal folding line.
   * Top edge retains two filing punch holes fitted with brass grommets/eyelets and showing brown iron oxidation halos.
   * Upper left corner displays mirror-image bleed-through of the pencil/ink filing docket: `[21 Nov. 1810]`.
   * Upper right corner exhibits faint mirror-image show-through of the red ink classification mark: `[VI]`.

2. **Reprographic & Administrative Markings:**
   * Lower central quadrant bears the official circular purple stamp: `ARSIP NASIONAL REPUBLIK INDONESIA` featuring the Indonesian national emblem (*Garuda Pancasila*).
   * Rectangular blue boxed non-commercial distribution warning stamp below the seal:
     ```text
     +-----------------------------------+
     | TIDAK UNTUK DIPERDJUAL-BELIKAN    |
     | NOT FOR COMMERCI L PURPOSES       |
     +-----------------------------------+
     ```
   * Faint offset impression of a neighboring archival seal visible to the right.
