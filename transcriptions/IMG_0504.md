# Transcription of IMG_0504_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0504_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 27: Archival Research Notes on Yogyakarta Residency Letters, February–March 1810)
- **Date Range:** 1810-02-12 to 1810-03-26
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official Yogyakarta Residency correspondence during the transition of colonial residents in early 1810. Minister Gustaf Wilhelm Wiese forwards a letter from Sultan Hamengkubuwana II to Governor-General Daendels asserting the innocence of Yogyakarta border subjects in incidents adjoining Pekalongan; Pinket van Haak reports on village headmen (*lurahs*) of desa Wanakrama (Dukuh Guasur); Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees assumes administrative duties as Acting Minister/Resident upon Wiese's departure; Wiese takes leave of HB II and Patih Danureja II, receiving gifts of court batiks from the royal consorts (*Ratus*) and fine horses from the Patih; delivery of dispatches from Pieter Engelhard (President of the Court of Aldermen / *Schepenen*) presenting two Japanese silk robes (*kabayas*) to HB II, which are coldly received and subsequently passed to Patih Danureja II as only seemly for priests; return of edible bird's nest gatherers (*laxeners* / *rongkob*) from Karangbolong; and Moorrees issues an administrative proclamation on 16 March 1810 strictly regulating the supply and illicit transport of arrack and spirits from Semarang under threat of legal prosecution and a fine of 500 Spanish Dollars (silver).
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Gustaf Wilhelm Wiese, Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh), Radèn Adipati Danureja II, Crown Prince (Pangéran Adipati Anom / later HB III), Pieter Engelhard, Pinket van Haak.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta, Pekalongan, Semarang, Karangbolong, desa Wanakrama (Dukuh Guasur).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI Djokjo Brieven no. 27, Sprokkelmaand (February), Lentenmaand (March), Ratu (royal consort), Patih, Schepenen (Court of Aldermen), kabaya (traditional robe), laxeners (birds' nest gatherers / *rongkob*), Karangbolong birds' nest cliffs, Spanish Dollars (Sp. D.).
