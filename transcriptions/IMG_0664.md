### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Instructions to Lt. Paulus**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br><br>*Standing Orders* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng] issues formal written instructions to Lieutenant Paulus [Lt. Paulus] and interpreter Lucas Leberfeld [L. Leberfeld] for the military pursuit column:<br>• **Article 1:** Cooperate unreservedly with Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura [R.T. Purwadipura], general commander of the Kraton expedition.<br>• **Article 2:** Promulgate and distribute Sultan HB II's [Hamengkubuwana II] royal circular letters to all eastern mancanagara regents, strictly warning them against assisting or sheltering Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga].<br>• **Article 3:** Keep an accurate and uninterrupted operational daybook/journal detailing all troop movements, skirmishes, and intelligence.<br>• **Article 4:** Employ interpreter Lucas Leberfeld for all Javanese diplomatic correspondence, vernacular translations, and local liaison.<br>• Identical instructional circulars are dispatched simultaneously to the colonial Landdrosts of Semarang and Demak. |
| **DR II Searches Dalèm**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br><br>*53 Captives* | Radèn Adipati Danureja II [DR II] conducts an armed search of Rangga's deserted town residence (*dalèm*) in Yogyakarta:<br>• DR II captures 53 household retainers, servants, and followers left behind in the compound and delivers them under escort to Sultan HB II at the Kraton.<br>• Confirmed intelligence that Rangga departed with 350 well-armed cavalry and pikemen on his dash to Madiun. |
| **Eastern Regents Rally**<br>21 Nov. 1810<br><br>*Madiun Concentration* | Intelligence reports indicate the rapid assembly and concentration of eastern mancanagara regents around Madiun:<br>• **Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat II** (Rajêgwêsi)<br>• **Radèn Tumênggung Sasrawinata** and **Radèn Tumênggung Sasradipura** (Magetan)<br>• **Radèn Tumênggung Pringgalaya** (Kèrtasana)<br>• **Radèn Tumênggung Sasranagara** (Grobogan)<br>• **Kyai Ngabèhi Mangundirana** (Kalangbrèt)<br>• **Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara** of Padangan (who had originally been designated by HB II as Rangga's escort to Bogor) is reported marching rapidly toward Madiun. |
