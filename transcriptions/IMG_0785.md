| Also asks Pang. [Pangéran] Aria Prang Wedana's lands to be returned to jurisdiction of Emperor; Sunan says would not abandon prince but give him an income in accordance with his dignity. But Robison particularly insists on good relations between Sunan + Prang Wedana. | He also asked that Pangéran Aria Prangwedana's [Mangkunagara II] lands be returned to the Emperor's jurisdiction, stating he would not abandon the prince but would grant him an income fitting his dignity. Robison, however, insisted on maintaining good relations between the Sunan and Prangwedana. |
| Robison asks Sunan to make a memoir in writing of demands; makes no assurances = only promises to forward petition to Ld. [Lord] Minto. Emperor gives Krijgsman present of 2 Batik Dresses (`? jarongs`), recommended to Mr. Robison. At 1 o'clock Emperor returns to Kraton. | Robison asked the Sunan to prepare a written memoir of his demands, making no commitments beyond promising to forward the petition to Lord Minto. The Emperor gave Krijgsman a present of two batik garments (`jarongs`?) and recommended him to Robison, returning to the Kraton at 1 o'clock. |
| `23 Sept.`: Private visit of Emperor without state ceremony; delivers *Pro Memoria* in Javanese, asks Krijgsman to translate; visit lasts ½ an hour. | On **23 September 1811**, the Emperor paid a private half-hour visit without state ceremony, delivering his *Pro Memoria* in Javanese and asking Krijgsman to translate it. |
