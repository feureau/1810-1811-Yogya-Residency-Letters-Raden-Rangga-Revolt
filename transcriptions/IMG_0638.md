### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DR II's Answers**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Private Reading* | DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II]'s official answers to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels]'s questionnaire:<br>• Confirms that the Crown Prince [Pangéran Adipati Anom / future HB III] and HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] read Daendels's earlier ultimatum entirely alone in private chambers.<br>• The exact contents and instructions were strictly withheld from all other courtiers and ministers. |
| **Ratu Kencana & Rangga**<br>7 Dec. 1810 | Intercession and intrigue surrounding Ratu Kencana [Queen Consort]:<br>• Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] urgently sought to utilize his intimate court influence with Ratu Kencana to discover the contents of the Governor-General's letters.<br>• Ratu Kencana denied any knowledge, replying to Rangga that the Sultan guarded the correspondence with extreme secrecy and suspicion. |
| **The Royal Piyagem**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Rangga Loses His Head* | The secret royal warrant and Rangga's panic:<br>• HB II had issued an extraordinary royal commission (*piyagem*) ordering the escort regents to convey Rangga to Buitenzorg [Bogor] by force of arms should he refuse or hesitate.<br>• When Rangga received leaked intelligence of this armed escort *piyagem*, he completely *"lost his head"* in terror and took flight to Madiun to initiate open rebellion. |
| **Natakusuma Disgraced**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Reorganization* | Total collapse of Natakusuma's court faction:<br>• Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I] was excluded from summons to the royal palace and completely lost the Sultan's trust.<br>• Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] was stripped of his acting *patih* authority and temporary executive powers.<br>• Danureja II was fully reconfirmed as Prime Minister, assisted by Kyai Tumênggung Sindunagara [Sindunagara I] and Radèn Tumênggung Danukusuma I [Danukusuma I] as senior *kliwon* [deputy patihs]. |
| **Inner Circle of Trust**<br>7 Dec. 1810 | Restructuring of Kraton leadership:<br>• HB II's intimate circle of confidence is strictly narrowed to four key figures: Danureja II, Sumadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat], Sindunagara, and Danukusuma.<br>• HB II actively considers appointing Danureja II to lead the grand royal relief column into the eastern mancanagara. |
