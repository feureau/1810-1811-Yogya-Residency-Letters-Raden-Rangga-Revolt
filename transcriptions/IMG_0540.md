# Transcription of IMG_0540_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0540_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 27  
**Date:** 1810-09-10 to 1810-09-16 (10 September to 16 September 1810 / Herfstmaand; Carey header: `1810 / (Sept 1810)`)  
**Key Events:**
* Deployment of Jayèngsekar Cavalry to Singapati (Goldbach to Engelhard, 10 September 1810 / Herfstmaand): Landdrost Goldbach reports on anti-bandit operations against the Demang of Desa Singapati, Jamanggala, and Singajaya; a detachment of Jayèngsekar auxiliary cavalry is deployed directly to the village.
* Judicial Commission to Wirasari (Engelhard to Goldbach, 14 September 1810): Resident Pieter Engelhard conducts a palace visit with Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] on 12 September, commissioning senior Javanese language translator Johannes Godlieb Dietrée to lead an on-site inquiry. Engelhard requests two Javanese officials and an escort of European and Javanese Christian soldiers to forestall any violent disturbances, instructing Dietrée to coordinate closely with Goldbach.
* Midnight Audience in the Kraton (Engelhard to Goldbach, 13 September 1810):
  - Following receipt of Goldbach's intelligence, Engelhard enters the Kraton personally late in the evening (11:00 PM – 12:00 AM), necessitated by Patih DR II's [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] incapacitating illness.
  - Engelhard demands immediate judicial investigation, restitution of the 10,000 Spanish Dollars of plundered goods, apprehension of the Demak *kliwon*, and arrest of the ringleaders and village demangs in Wirasari (specifically desa Gabus and Singapati) for violating colonial territorial sovereignty.
  - Sultan's Cordial Demeanor: Engelhard observes that *"the old gentleman was as previously behaved very well towards me"*; HB II promises to send an urgent royal messenger immediately to track down the fugitives, requesting only that a European officer accompany the royal emissary in conformity with ancient treaty etiquette (*the old adat*).
* Appointment of the Wirasari Commission (P.S. by Patih DR II): The Patih confirms the official appointment of the joint investigative delegation:
  - Radèn Tumenggung Sasrakusuma (Regent of Grobogan);
  - Mas Ngabèhi Singajaya (*Dalêm Kliwon*);
  - The commission departs for Wirasari on Saturday, 16 September 1810.
