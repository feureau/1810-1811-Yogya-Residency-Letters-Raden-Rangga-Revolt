| *A.N. Djogja Letters No. 22, 1811* — **DJOGJA 1811 [Arsip N.]** <span style="color:#a00">**1811 (July-Aug)**</span><br>**22 July 1811 — Janssens [Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens] - Engelhard [Pieter Engelhard]:**<br>TREASURY in a hopeless state = great INDIGNATION if Products [coffee, tea, sugar] NOT paid PROPERLY.<br>Need `300,000` to pay COFFEE products: only `84,000` left in Treasury.<br>All trade cut off by sea. Hopes that E. Indian civil servants would themselves help Govnmt out of their plight and give all support possible. | **22 July 1811 — Governor-General Janssens to Pieter Engelhard:**<br>The colonial Treasury is in a desperate condition, with great anger if colonial produce (coffee, tea, sugar) is not properly paid for. `300,000` is needed to pay for coffee deliveries, while only `84,000` remains in the Treasury and all maritime trade is severed. Janssens hopes East Indian civil servants will themselves assist the Government out of its plight. |
| **29 July 1811 — Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>REPORT MEETING + PATIH [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] over QUESTION of SULTAN'S [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] LANDS [annexed 1810]:<br>**SULTAN *Sepuh*** = Reports: **LAND of JAPAN** (Birthplace of Ratu Kencana [Wulan] + where whole FAMILY buried):<br>Extremely aggrieved at heart for Annexation; also that SOLO [Surakarta] only gives over `15,000` *bahos* [to] Yogja [Yogyakarta] (`16,000`). Hopes that G.G. [Governor-General] would give favourable Reply.<br>Does **NOT** wish to take back LANDS of SOLO (**DJALUR WÉTAN**): large nos of ROBBERS there, people who plunder govnmt lands; would rather not take over this.<br>Engelhard stresses changed SITUATION in EUROPE `||`: UNIFICATION of HOLLAND + FRANCE and the possibility that a large fleet would be sent by NAPOLEON to relieve JAVA.<br>But meanwhile = SILVER MONEY at an end. | **29 July 1811 — Pieter Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>Reports meeting with the Patih over the 1810 annexed territories:<br>Sultan *Sepuh* [Hamengkubuwana II] is deeply aggrieved over the annexation of **Japan** (birthplace of Ratu Kencana Wulan and site of her family tombs), and complains that Surakarta only ceded `15,000` *bahus* out of `16,000` to Yogyakarta, hoping for a favorable reply from Janssens. Conversely, he does **not** wish to take over the Surakarta lands in **Jalur Wétan** due to rampant robbers plundering government lands.<br>Engelhard emphasized the changed European situation—the unification of Holland and France and the possibility of Napoleon sending a large relief fleet to Java—though meanwhile silver currency is exhausted. |
