| *(red double bar)*<br>"As far as the Information of Government at present extends, It appears that It would be an act of Justice, and one that would certainly tend to make a favourable impression on the Native Inhabitants to Restore the Sultan to the government."<br>But Necessary to ascertain How far the same might be effected without endangering the tranquillity of the country.<br>If such a measure practicable: Regent (Heir Apparent) to continue as Heir Apparent.<br>"This Important point Requires your most serious investigation and as early Report as circumstances permit." | **Policy on Restoring HB II:** *"As far as the information of Government at present extends, it appears that it would be an act of justice, and one that would certainly tend to make a favourable impression on the Native Inhabitants to restore the Sultan to the government."* However, Crawfurd must ascertain how far this can be effected without endangering the tranquillity of the country (with the Regent continuing as Heir Apparent), and report as early as possible. *(Continued on [IMG_0826.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0826.md).)* |
