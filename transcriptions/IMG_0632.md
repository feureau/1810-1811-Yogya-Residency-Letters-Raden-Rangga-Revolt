| **Relaas of Wangsatruna (24 Nov. 1810, p. 13):**<br>• Spy deployed by Surakarta to shadow Rangga's movements.<br>• Reports on the alleged assembly of 15 rebel regents at **Desa Tinggil**.<br><br>**Relaas of Surakarta Spy (9 Dec. 1810, p. 87):**<br>• Interrogates **Nuryémangi**, a Sundanese Islamic religious teacher (*priest*) arriving from Panaraga, traveling toward Sunda Sukapura.<br>• Reports that Radèn Rongga bivouacked to the north of **Desa Pakuncèn**; in the open plains (*oro-oro*) he commanded only **200 troops**.<br><br>*(red pointer)*:<br>• `Nuryémangi enters Kutha Pêtik [stronghold of Rangga].`<br>• Observes empty cannon guarding the portal of the fortification; gateway and audience pavilion (*paséban*) stand abandoned.<br>• All surrounding fruit orchards and trees chopped down by roving bandits (*plunderers*). | **Inside the Great Mosque of Pêtik:**<br>• Nuryémangi enters the principal Friday mosque at Pêtik.<br><br>*(double red vertical lines & red underline)*:<br>• `In the women's prayer gallery (pawestren), sees that chests had been broken open & religious manuscripts (kitabs) belonging to Rangga scattered about.`<br>• Wife and young children of Radèn Rongga evacuated under the escort of Islamic clerics (*santri*) into the **Rêdi Kèndhèng** mountains to the northeast of Pêtik.<br>• Intelligence circulates that Rangga is preparing to flee toward Surabaya or deep into the Kèndhèng hills.<br><br>*(red underline & red marginal heading)*:<br>`Kutha Pêtik.` / `New wall around Paseban / Kraton of Rangga at Petik not yet completed.`<br>• Nuryémangi departs Pêtik with **29 disciples (*murid*)**; retreats to the residence of **Kyai Kêtib Sémani**. | **Surakarta Reinforcements from Jagaraga (19 Dec. 1810, p. 89):**<br>• **R.T. Sasrawijaya**, **Radèn Ria Sasradiwiria**, **Radèn Ngabèhi Mangunjaya**, and **Mas Ngabèhi Mèrtalaya** report to Radèn Adipati Cakranagara [Patih of Solo].<br>• Marching with **1,500 *prajurit*** from Jagaraga, equipped with muskets/artillery, to effect a junction with Yogyakarta commander **Tumênggung Purwadipura**.<br><br>**Defense of Caruban (p. 93):**<br>• Statement (*opgave*) of **Kartakusuma**, Patih of Radèn Ngabèhi Kartasari at Caruban.<br>• When Radèn Rongga fled from Madiun, the loyalist inhabitants of Caruban mobilized and manned defensive breastworks inside the fortified town (*kotta*). |
