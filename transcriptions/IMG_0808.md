| `Turunan.`<br>`Pènget ingkang serat parèntah undang-undang Kangdjeng Rahadèn Adipati Tjakranagara, dawuha marang pakenira sarupané wong Negara ing Wirasaba, Kadiri, ing Balora, sarupané wong Mantjanegara, Negarané apadéné desane, gedé tjilik kang pada kabawah ing Surakarta Hadiningrat kabèh.` | **Romanized Javanese Transcript (*Turunan*):** Order of Kangjeng Radèn Adipati Cakranegara addressed to all people of Wirasaba, Kediri, Blora, and all *Mancanegara* towns and villages, great and small, subject to Surakarta Adiningrat. |
| `Lirning lajang manira undang-undang, kang dawuh marang pakenira kabeh, jèn ana pikulané Tuwan Djèndral teka ing Surabaja, utawa gotongan pira-pira kèhé, liwat ana tanah Mantjanegara, pada pakenira angkata, pira-piraha olèhé ndjaluk tundan wong ngangkat pikulan ika mau, pakenira ladènana, kebat teka ing Surakarta, gotongan utawa pikulan ika mau, lan di betjik pambaureksamu, mikareksa, jèn nganti katiwasan ilang mesti pakenira pada nemu bilani kang luwih abanget.` *(margin: `Deliveries for Surabaya`)* | **Content:** Commanding that whenever loads (*pikulan*) or litters (*gotongan*) belonging to the General arrive from Surabaya and pass through *Mancanegara* lands, the inhabitants must carry them, provide whatever relays of porters are requested, bring them swiftly to Surakarta, and guard them well — warning that any loss or mishap will bring severe punishment. |
