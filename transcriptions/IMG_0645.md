### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Paulus to Eng.**<br>9 Dec. 1810<br><br>*3,000 Troops at Paseban* | Lieutenant Paulus to Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] (9 Dec. 1810):<br>• Royal reinforcements dispatched from Yogyakarta under Pangéran Adinagara and Pangéran Dipakusuma successfully unite with Purwadipura [PWD].<br>• The grand royal army assembled at the public square (*Paseban*) of Madiun now totals over **3,000 troops**.<br>• Instructs Purwadipura to provide defensive escorts for the regency of Padagangan to prevent unauthorized incursions or plunder by Mangkunagaran [MN] detachments. |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>13 Dec. 1810 | Resident Engelhard to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] (13 Dec. 1810):<br>• Transmits Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]'s formal replies to three separate ultimatums and dispatches from the Governor-General. |
| **HB II to Daendels**<br>13 Dec. 1810<br>(Reply to 5 Dec.)<br><br>*Hostage Concession* | Sultan HB II to Marshal Daendels (13 Dec. 1810, in reply to letter of 5 Dec.):<br>• The Sultan admits and agrees with Daendels that he had previously been far too indulgent towards Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga].<br>• **Major Political Capitulation:** HB II formally consents to surrender Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I / NK] and Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat / ND] into colonial custody for the duration of Rangga's rebellion.<br>• Daendels makes an explicit exception for Radèn Tumênggung Natapraja [NP].<br>• HB II requests that Natakusuma and Natadiningrat be escorted directly to Semarang, and that DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] swear a formal oath of allegiance before Daendels. |
| **HB II to Daendels**<br>13 Dec. 1810<br>(Reply to 24 & 25 Nov.)<br><br>*Denunciation of NK* | HB II to Daendels (13 Dec. 1810, in reply to letters of 24 and 25 Nov.):<br>• The Sultan asserts that his eyes are now fully opened to the dangerous intrigues of Natakusuma.<br>• Instructs the Crown Prince [CP / future HB III] to maintain strict personal surveillance over Natakusuma.<br>• Expresses earnest hope that he will once again be embraced as an honored friend and ally of Daendels.<br>• Formally vows that Rangga will be hunted down and executed; asks that once Rangga is dead, Natadiningrat and Natakusuma be permitted to return promptly to Yogyakarta. |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>14 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Command Shakeup* | Engelhard to Daendels (14 Dec. 1810):<br>• Reports Daendels's profound anger at the expeditionary force repeatedly delaying an assault on Rangga's mountain hideout.<br>• Daendels demands that Purwadipura be instantly relieved and replaced by Sumadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat].<br>• HB II professes equal fury at Purwadipura's indolence, but decides instead to transfer supreme operational command to Pangéran Adinagara.<br>• In Daendels's name, Engelhard strictly forbids Lieutenant Paulus from joining any further combat operations, ordering him to remain at Madiun to guard Rangga's confiscated palace (*dalêm*). |
