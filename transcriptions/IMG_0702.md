At Cornelis, nearly all Indies troops were captured and all artillery was lost. Janssens fled to Bogor.<br>The British summoned him to surrender honorably—an offer he should have accepted, having no artillery left and losing the confidence of local rulers. He was completely defeated again at Jati-Ngalèh, taken prisoner, and sent to England. |
