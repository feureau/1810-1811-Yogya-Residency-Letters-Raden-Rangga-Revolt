### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eng. to Servatius**<br>15 Dec. 1810<br>(Tues. evening) | Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] to Secretary Servatius (15 Dec. 1810):<br>• The *Patih* of Yogyakarta (Danureja II) will proceed to Semarang unattended by any large armed retinue.<br>• Journey to the northern headquarters scheduled to commence this very afternoon. |
| **Paulus to Servatius**<br>11 Dec. 1810<br>(Maospati)<br><br>*Capture of Rangga's Family* | Lieutenant Thomas Paulus to Servatius (11 Dec. 1810, from Maospati):<br>• Royal troops capture high-value rebel hostages: Two full brothers of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] — Radèn Prawirapreja and Radèn Sumaprawira — together with Rangga's elderly mother.<br>• Expresses earnest hope that this major capture will bring a swift and decisive conclusion to the expedition. |
| **Paulus to Servatius**<br>12 Dec. 1810<br>(Maospati) | Paulus to Servatius (12 Dec. 1810):<br>• The districts of Magetan and Madiun are reported tranquil and peaceful once more.<br>• Purwadipura [PWD] takes comprehensive measures to secure Rangga's fortified *dalèm* [palace residence] against any surprise counter-attack.<br>• **Prisoner Transfer:** Rangga's mother and two captured brothers are ordered sent under heavy armed guard directly to the Kraton court in Yogyakarta.<br>• No precise intelligence regarding Rangga's exact whereabouts; on 15 Dec., the united royal expedition will launch an active sweep into the mountains in pursuit of Rangga. |
| **Paulus to Servatius**<br>13 Dec. 1810<br>(Maospati)<br><br>*Closing the Net* | Paulus to Servatius (13 Dec. 1810):<br>• Pangéran Têpasana arrives at Maospati with a fresh detachment of palace guards from Yogyakarta to reinforce Purwadipura.<br>• Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat [SD] arrives with the northern regency contingent from Rajêgwêsi.<br>• Purwadipura instructs Sasradiningrat to draw an impenetrable cordon around Rangga by blockading all mountain roads, passes, and jungle trails, ensuring the rebel chief cannot break through. |
| **van Braam to Eng.**<br>8 Dec. 1810 | Admiral Pieter van Braam [van B] to Engelhard (8 Dec. 1810):<br>• Confirms that Bupati Prawirayuda of Sekaran has returned from Japan to his home regency. |
| **Prawirayuda's Deposition**<br>6 Dec. 1810<br>(9 Dulkangidah 1737) | Radèn Tumênggung Prawirayuda (Nipang - Pasikaran) to the Bupati of Surabaya (6 Dec. 1810):<br>• Intended to seek refuge in Surabaya with his household and family.<br>• However, his wife, Radèn Ayu Putri, vehemently opposes entering Surabaya.<br>• The Bupati therefore resolves to change course and seek sanctuary in the coastal regency of Tuban. |
