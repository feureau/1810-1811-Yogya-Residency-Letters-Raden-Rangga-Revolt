| **TRANSLATION OF PRO MEMORIA OF SUNAN** | **Translation of the Sunan's *Pro Memoria* (Articles 1–7)** |
| `Art. 1`: My wishes (Sunan's) and desires are: Desires prosperity of my country + content satisfaction of my heart. | **Article 1:** The Sunan desires the prosperity of his country and the contentment of his heart. |
| `Art. 2`: That the graves of my family situated at Java's N.E. [North-East] coast namely at Ampel, Girie, Tubon, Batang, Kudus, Adilaju [Kadilangu], Demak and Tegal Arum with those that keep watch over the same may be again delivered up to me. | **Article 2:** That the royal family graves on Java's North-East coast — at Ampel, Giri, Tuban, Batang, Kudus, Kadilangu, Demak, and Tegal Arum — together with their caretakers, be restored to the Sunan. |
| `Art. 3`: That the farm money of the *Strand* (Beach) on Java's N.E. coast to the amount of `10,000 Sp. Dollars` per annum which serves as a means of support to my family but which has been annulled and withdrawn by G.G. [Governor-General] Daendels may be restored to me. | **Article 3:** That the `10,000` Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars] per annum *Strand* (coastal) farm money for the support of the royal family, annulled and withdrawn by Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels, be restored. |
| `Art. 4`: That all the Chinese residing in the Jurisdictions of Surakarta may be placed under my immediate orders, as from old times this nation can make/cause many troubles as well in the dessas [villages] as in the capital. | **Article 4:** That all Chinese residing within Surakarta's jurisdictions be placed under the Sunan's immediate orders, as from old times they have caused troubles both in the villages (*desas*) and in the capital. |
| `Art. 5`: That all my Toll-gates, Salarans and the Farm of Birds' Nests which I have ceded to the Minister (van Braam) may be restituted to me again as I want to administer them myself. | **Article 5:** That all tollgates, *salarans*, and Birds' Nests farms ceded to Resident Jacob Andries van Braam be restored so the Sunan may administer them himself. |
| `Art. 6`: I request that the Minister van Braam and his Sec. [Secretary] Servatius may the sooner the better depart from Sourakarta. | **Article 6:** Requesting that Resident van Braam and his Secretary Willem Nicolaas Servatius depart from Surakarta as soon as possible. |
| `Art. 7`: That at the exchange of a new Minister who watches or waits over my court, that the old etiquette and honour may be again observed, as it was in the times of the Dutch Company and that whenever the Minister wishes to speak to me he will come together with my... *(continued on `IMG_0787`)* | **Article 7:** That upon the appointment of a new Resident/Minister, the old etiquette and honours of the Dutch Company [V.O.C.] era be observed again, and that whenever the Resident wishes to speak with the Sunan, he come together with the Prime Minister... *(continued on [IMG_0787.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0787.md))*. |
