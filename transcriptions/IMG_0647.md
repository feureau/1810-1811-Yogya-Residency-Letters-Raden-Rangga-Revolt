### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>11 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Scene at the Dhampar* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] (11 Dec. 1810):<br>• Resident carries Daendels's ultimatum of 5 Dec. 1810 directly into the royal presence of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II].<br>• Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I / NK] and Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat / ND] are seated outside among the court regents (*bupatis*).<br>• HB II reads only a few opening lines of the dispatch, then sets it down beside him upon the royal throne dais (*dhampar*).<br>• The customary royal artillery salute is fired from the Dutch fortress (*Fort Rustenburg*) celebrating the formal delivery of the Governor-General's letter.<br>• HB II receives Engelhard with unusually meticulous courtesy and scrupulously observes every formality of court etiquette. |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>12 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Hostage Safe-Conduct* | Engelhard to Daendels (12 Dec. 1810):<br>• HB II charges DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] with a solemn royal injunction: The detention of Natakusuma and Natadiningrat at Semarang must not endure a single day longer than required to defeat Rangga.<br>• NK and ND behave quietly and submit with dignity, declaring their readiness to swear a formal oath of allegiance before Daendels in Semarang.<br>• HB II expresses grave anxiety that other princes might abscond to their regional appanages to recruit insurgent supporters against the Government.<br>• Watching NK and ND depart, Engelhard formally reassures HB II of Daendels's pledge: The princes will only be held in Semarang for the duration of the rebellion, and no harm shall befall them.<br>• Resident strongly urges HB II to dispatch a prompt written reply to the Governor-General. |
| **Note of Daendels**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*(Marked with double red rule)* | **Critical Archival Note by Marshal Daendels:**<br>• Daendels's caustic assessment: *"Why does Paulus just write letters + leave military tactics up to Regents who are the most cowardly + inefficient people imaginable."*<br>• Suspects HB II is being made the dupe of his own court commanders; suggests that Sumadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat] should immediately replace Purwadipura [PWD].<br>• Daendels notes that if HB II desires, he may reinforce the expedition with palace *prajurit* [infantrymen], but demands to know how many troops remain in Yogyakarta should Rangga make a sudden dash against the capital while the expedition is east of Madiun.<br>• **Ominous British War Strategy:** Daendels secretly resolves that despite court promises, **he would keep Natakusuma and Natadiningrat detained until general peace is concluded with England** (*"until peace concluded + England"*). |
