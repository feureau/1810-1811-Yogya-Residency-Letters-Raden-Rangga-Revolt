# Transcription of IMG_0542_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0542_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37  
**Date:** 1810-09-01 to 1810-11-21 (1 September 1810 to 21 November 1810 / Herfstmaand; citing letters of 1 Sept., 6 Sept., and 16 Sept. 1810, and inquest of 21 Aug. 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Commission of Inquiry into the Panaraga Raids (Engelhard to Servatius, 1 September 1810):
  - Government and Surakarta joint investigation into the plundering of Ngêbêl and Sikêdok, directly implicating Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III (*Rangga implicated*).
  - Sultan HB II [Hamengkubuwana II] appoints Pangéran Dipakusuma and Radèn Tumenggung Jayadipura to represent Yogyakarta on the tripartite commission.
  - Dispute over the assembly location: Engelhard refers to Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels as *"my Prince / Ruler"*; HB II adamantly refuses to allow his commissioners to enter Surakarta, insisting upon Kartasura or Boyolali (*Bayalali*).
* Assembly Protocol Agreement (Engelhard to Servatius, 6 September 1810): Sunan Pakubuwana IV agrees to assemble at Kartasura rather than Boyolali.
* The Van Braam Estate Irrigation Inquest (Engelhard to Servatius, 16 September 1810):
  - Examination into accusations that Kyai Mohammad Jayiman of desa Kapan (Panataran) deliberately blocked the water supply feeding the agricultural estate and gardens of Jacob Andries van Braam, aggravated by the extraordinary severity of that year's dry season.
  - Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] submits the official Procès-Verbal, accompanied by a Dutch translation of the judicial report prepared by Radèn Tumenggung Cakranêgara.
  - The inquiry, conducted on Monday Legi, 19 Rejeb Wawu 1737 [21 August / Oogstmaand 1810] by Mas Rêsadiwiria and Mas Putra Dirana of Gondhang, records the depositions of local village elders:
    - Kyai Singarana and Kyai Somêrta at Samboong;
    - Kyai Mêtadita at Kadeli;
    - Kyai Sojadha at Pacukan;
    - Kyai Singatruna of Kanutaran (representing the Gladhag transport corvée).
