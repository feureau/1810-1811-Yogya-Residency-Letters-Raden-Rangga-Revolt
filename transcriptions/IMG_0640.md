### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Relaas of Nicamênggala**<br>Magetan to Eng.<br>4 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Fall of Magetan* | Deposition (*relaas*) of courier Nicamênggala from Magetan to Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] (4 Dec. 1810):<br>• Dispatched by Radèn Tumênggung Sasrawinata and Radèn Tumênggung Sasradipura to notify the Crown Prince [CP / Pangéran Adipati Anom / future HB III] in Yogyakarta that Magetan has been captured by Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga].<br>• The loyal regents have retreated to the mountain fastness of the Hill of Pacalan.<br>• Radèn Kêrtadiwiria (*mantri* [lower official] of Wirasari) and four others killed by Rangga's insurgent forces.<br>• Radèn Cakraprawira and Radèn Cakradrana severely wounded.<br>• Death of rebel commander: The head of Rangga's vanguard *prajurit* [infantry], Desamuka [Rawana], is also killed during the assault.<br>• Bodies of the dead are cast into the river by Rangga's troops.<br>• Rangga burns down public and official buildings in Magetan, then retreats to Maospati, declaring his intention to march on and attack Panaraga. |
| **HB II to Daendels**<br>29 Sawal 1737<br>27 Nov. 1810<br>*(or ? 22 Nov. check date)*<br><br>*Yogya Expedition Roster* | Sultan Hamengkubuwana II [HB II] to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels]:<br>• Informs His Excellency of the armed rebellion and unauthorized departure of Rangga from the capital.<br>• Enumerates the composition of the royal punitive expedition dispatched under:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Inner Regents (*Bupati Lebet*):** Purwadipura [Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura / PWD], Radèn Tumênggung Dipadirja (son of Sindureja), and Radèn Panji Nuryèngrana (*wêdana prajurit* [commander of royal palace guards]).<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Outer Regents (*Bupati Manca*):** Tumênggung Wiranêgara, Radèn Tumênggung Mèrtalaya, and Radèn Ngabèhi Jayasêntika (*wêdana prajurit kadipatèn* [commander of the crown prince's troops]).<br>• HB II formally denounces Rangga as an evil incendiary who has plundered both Surakarta [Solo] and Yogyakarta crown villages.<br>• Reports the dispatch today of heavy royal reinforcements commanded by royal princes: Pangéran Dipakusuma, Pangéran Adinêgara, and Radèn Ngabèhi Tirtadiwiria.<br>• Emphasizes the Sultan's sincere desire that unbroken harmony between Yogyakarta and the Dutch Government be maintained.<br>• Formally requests colonial military assistance against Rangga before the insurgents can attack and violate Mangkunagaran [MN] lands. |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>10 Dec. 1810<br>11 p.m. | Resident Engelhard to Marshal Daendels (10 Dec. 1810, 11:00 p.m.):<br>• Reports that HB II has dispatched another urgent royal letter to Purwadipura, strictly commanding him to press forward and engage Rangga without further delay. |
