## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `18 Nov` (`Raffles -> Crawfurd - Buitenzorg -`):<br>"Under the Necessity of Intimating to you my entire Disapprobation of the Measures you have adopted for the Detenting [detaining] the Light Infantry Corps at Semarang" | **18 November 1811 — Thomas Stamford Raffles (Buitenzorg) to John Crawfurd:** Expresses *"my entire disapprobation"* of Crawfurd's measures in detaining the Light Infantry Corps at Semarang. |
| `24 Nov` (`Raffles -> Crawfurd Buitenzorg`):<br>"The state of affairs in Djocjakarta which appear to have been Brought to a Crisis by the measures you have pursued calls peremptorily for the Immediate interference of the Government, and I have in consequence taken the Resolution of proceeding thither in person as early as the necessary previous arrangements can be made in this quarter for the Dispatch of public Business." | **24 November 1811 — Raffles (Buitenzorg) to Crawfurd:** States that Yogyakarta's affairs, *"brought to a crisis by the measures you have pursued"*, call peremptorily for immediate Government interference, and announces his resolution to proceed to Yogyakarta in person as soon as arrangements permit. |
| `6 Dec 1811` (`Raffles -> CR [Crawfurd] Semarang`):<br>R. [Raffles] Arrived in Semarang `5 Dec. 1811` = In Letters of CR of `26/28 Nov` = also gets Letters from HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] / CP [Crown Prince].<br>But Immediately Returned: "As it appears that Neither the style of address nor mode of communication is conformable to what has been Heretofore customary." *(red star)* | **6 December 1811 — Raffles (Semarang) to Crawfurd:** Having arrived in Semarang on 5 December, Raffles received Crawfurd's letters of 26/28 November along with letters from Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and the Crown Prince [HB III], which he immediately returned because *"neither the style of address nor mode of communication is conformable to what has been heretofore customary."* |
| `Asks FROM HB II / Prince Regent:`<br>"Should send ambassadors to meet near Semarang without Delay, as Required by the 24th Article of the Contract of the year 1792." These ambassadors must be Here on the 12th or at the furthest the 14th!!<br>*(left margin: `Valck Notes in Pencil: "Grote Bok!"`)*<br>"At which latter Date I propose proceeding in person to Sala where if the ambassadors from the court of Yogya have not previously met me, I shall require their attendance." | **Demand for Ambassadors:** Raffles demands that HB II and the Prince Regent send ambassadors to meet him near Semarang without delay under Article 24 of the 1792 contract by 12–14 December, failing which he requires their attendance at Solo [Surakarta] *(pencil note by Valck: "Grote Bok!")*. |
| `7 Dec` Raffles to MADURA / SURABAYA to Return to Sala on `14 Dec. 6 a.m.`<br>Shall await Sala / Yogya -> messengers / ambassadors: Col. [Colonel] Watson + 14 Regiment to go with Him -> to Yogya on the `18 December 1811`. | **7 December 1811:** Raffles departs for Madura and Surabaya, planning to return to Solo on **14 December at 6 a.m.** to await the Solo/Yogya ambassadors, and proceed with Colonel Watson and the 14th Regiment to Yogyakarta on **18 December 1811**. |
