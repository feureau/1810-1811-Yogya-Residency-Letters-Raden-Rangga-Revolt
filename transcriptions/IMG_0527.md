# Transcription of IMG_0527_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0527_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 (R. Rangga 1810–1811 / Letters of Van Braam, Engelhard, DR II, and Daendels)  
**Date:** 1810-08-18 to 1810-09-05 (Javanese: 30 Rejeb 1737 to 5 Ruwah 1737; reporting 2 September 1810 King Louis' Birthday festival)  
**Key Events:**
* Jacob Andries van Braam writes to Pieter Engelhard (18 August 1810) regarding Sunan Pakubuwana IV's formal complaint to Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels over Radèn Rongga's incursions into Ngêbêl and Sikêdok, set against Rongga's counter-accusations against Panaraga regents.
* Daendels decrees the appointment of a Tripartite Commission (Government, Surakarta, Yogyakarta) to travel to the devastated border desas; appoints Semarang auction master (*vendumeester*) Piepers and Government Javanese translator Krijgsman as Government Commissioners.
* Resident Pieter Engelhard officially assumes office following Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees' departure from Yogya.
* HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] formally appoints Pangéran Dipakusuma (Outer Regent of the Left Side) and Radèn Tumenggung Jayadipura (Inner Regent) as Yogyakarta's commissioners, but firmly refuses Surakarta as the assembly point, proposing Boyolali or Kartasura instead; departure fixed for 10 September 1810.
* Engelhard reports to Daendels (3 September 1810) on the lavish celebration of the Birthday of King Louis Napoleon of Holland on 2 September 1810 in Yogyakarta:
  - Dutch tricolor flags hoisted on the four bastions of Fort Rustenburg and throughout the Residency gardens.
  - Morning military review and two 45-gun artillery salutes fired at 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
  - Yogyakarta Inner and Outer Regents visit Resident Engelhard at 9:00 AM to present the Sultan's felicitations; DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] is excused due to illness.
  - Evening garden gala with all Dutch military and civil officers, attended in state by Sultan HB II, the Crown Prince (CP [later Hamengkubuwana III]), and senior court dignitaries.
  - Grand fireworks exhibition; HB II offers solemn toasts to the King's health and departs, while the Crown Prince and courtiers remain for supper, observing health toasts strictly in accordance with Daendels' 1808 Ceremonial Edict until the party concludes harmoniously at 2:00–3:00 AM.
