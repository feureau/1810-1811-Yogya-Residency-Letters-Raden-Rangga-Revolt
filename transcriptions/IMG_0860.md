The Bali ruler claimed Blambangan and Besuki in East Java (complicated by Marshal Herman Willem Daendels' sale of Banyuwangi estates to Chinese). The Raja of Bali Buleleng initially showed great friendship toward Thomas Stamford Raffles, but in early 1814 seized a British East India Company ship and raided Banyuwangi — prompting a British punitive expedition (attributed by John Bastin, *JMBRAS* 1954, p. 111, to revenue losses from slave-trade abolition and opium regulation). |
