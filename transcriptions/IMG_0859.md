| `p. xii`: Raffles stationed at Malacca from Dec. 1810 to help launch & forward operations for invasion of Java. Dispatches agents + Malay letters to Rulers of Perak, Riau-Lingga, Siak, Pontianak, Banjermasin, Sambas, Jambi, Palembang, Cirebon, Banten, Bali, Madura, Surakarta, Yogyakarta.<br>*(red: `Sent via Bali`)* No evidence that last 3 letters reach Rulers concerned, `||` But Bali Rajas inform Raffles that they were conveyed.<br>Raffles asks for supplies of food, + loyalty to English + not to aid Dutch.<br>`p. xiii`: Palembang ruler asked to drive Dutch out. Spies sent to Java to ask about state of inland military fortifications, defensive plans of Daendels / Janssens. Agents `Greigh`, `Smith`, `Scott` find out sea routes + Best passage for Java. | **Raffles' Malacca Mission (pp. xii–xiii):** Stationed at Malacca from December 1810 to prepare the invasion of Java, Raffles dispatched agents and Malay letters to rulers across the archipelago, including Cirebon, Banten, Bali, Madura, Surakarta, and Yogyakarta (the last three sent via Bali; Bali Rajas informed Raffles they were conveyed), requesting food supplies and loyalty against the Dutch. Spies and agents (`Greigh`, `Smith`, `Scott`) were sent to investigate inland fortifications, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels' and General Jan Willem Janssens' defensive plans, and sea routes to Java. *(Continued on [IMG_0860.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0860.md).)* |
