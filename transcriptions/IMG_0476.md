### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I**: Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 199). Father of Danureja II and Dipanagara's father-in-law; strangled in a mosque near Pacitan and buried in the dishonored cemetery of Banyusumurup in 1812.
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III**: Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). Instigator of cross-border bloodshed at Delanggu.
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus)** (`PB IV`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 18). Central figure during the 1790 Pakepung crisis and subsequent court purges.
  * **Nicolaus Engelhard**: Listed under *Governors of Java's Northeast Coast* (line 301). Governor at Semarang (1801–1808) who met PB IV at Salatiga in 1801.
  * **Johan Frederik baron van Reede tot de Parkeler** (`van Reede`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta / Governors of Java's Northeast Coast* (lines 300, 303). Dutch official who oversaw the court until 1801.
  * **Hendrik Mauritz MacGillavry** (`MacGillavry`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta / Yogyakarta* (lines 319, 346). Later Resident of Surakarta whose historical narrative was contested by van Pabst.
  * **Radèn Adipati Mangkupraja II** (`Mangkupraja / MP`): Listed under *Patih of Surakarta* (line 212).
  * **Cakrawinata** (`CW / Cakrawinata`): Surakarta official whose support for the Dutch in the 1790 Pakepung crisis led to his ultimate political downfall and strangulation.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Banyusumurup**: Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 410). Dishonored royal cemetery south of Yogyakarta where royal criminals, traitors, and disgraced officials were buried.
  * **Pacitan**: Southern coastal regency.
  * **Delanggu**: Border station between Klaten and Surakarta where Rangga's men killed a Surakarta subject.
  * **Secang**: Town north of Magelang in Kedu.
  * **Salatiga**: Hill station south of Semarang used for inter-state diplomatic summits.
  * **Sana Sewu**: Site of Cakrawinata's execution.
  * **Lodaya & Ayah**: Remote coastal exile destinations.
* **Terms:**
  * **Pakepung affair (1790)**: Famous political crisis where Dutch VOC forces and allied rulers encircled (*pakepung*) the Surakarta Kraton to compel PB IV to dismiss his anti-Dutch religious advisers.
  * **wèdana mantri bumi**: Senior royal land and revenue administrator.
  * **langgar**: Small Islamic prayer house / rural mosque.
