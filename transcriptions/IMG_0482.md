- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Daendels formally concludes Danuningrat's dismissal and orders the Minister at Surakarta to seek Pakubuwana IV's nomination of a replacement; salary increase for post-messengers (*rapport-gangers*); pay parity for indigenous Christian officers in the 2nd Garrison Regiment; regulations on coffee interplanting and eradication of unproductive pepper vines; construction of regional coffee warehouses across Java.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Herman Willem Daendels, Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Sunan Pakubuwana IV
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta / Soerakarta, Cianjur / Tjic-andjoer, Java
