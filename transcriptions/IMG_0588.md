### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Center (Red Bleed-through):** `18 Nov. 1810 / - 26 Nov. 1810` (bleed-through from recto)
* **Physical Characteristics:** Lined notebook leaf, 33 blue ruled horizontal lines, red vertical margin line; 2 punch holes at right edge (verso of leaf); blue ballpoint pen text. Documents military preparations and regional mobilizations against Radèn Rongga: the Grobogan regents' hesitation, cancellation of ammunition shipments from Klaten, Van Braam's actions in Surabaya, Pangéran Prangwedana's (Mangkunagara II) march with 500 men through Blora to Rembang, and the dispatch of 1,000 royal Yogyakarta troops under Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura joined by Lieutenant Paulus.
