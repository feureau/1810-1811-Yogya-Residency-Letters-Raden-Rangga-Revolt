### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III** (`R. Rangga / Rangga`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). *Bupati wedana* of Madiun who sought Surakarta's covert backing before launching his insurrection.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danuningrat (Mertawijaya / Cakrajaya)** (`Mertawijaya / MW / Cakrajaya / Patih Danuningrat`): Listed under *Patih of Surakarta* (lines 202, 212). Promoted from *kliwon* to *tumenggung*, and later served as *patih* of Surakarta; brother of PB IV's favourite concubine.
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus)** (`PB IV`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 18). Ruler of Surakarta (reigned 1788–1820) involved in clandestine anti-Dutch court diplomacy.
  * **Radèn Adipati Mangkupraja II** (`Patih Mangkupraja / Mangkupraja`): Listed under *Patih of Surakarta* (line 212). Prime minister of Surakarta courted by Radèn Rongga.
  * **Cakrawinata** (`CW / Cakrawinata`): Surakarta official who resisted supporting Radèn Rongga and was consequently marginalized by Mangkupraja.
  * **Tumenggung Mangkuyuda** (`Tum. Mangkuyuda`): Surakarta royal official serving as *wèdana mantri bumi*.
  * **Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst** (`van Pabst`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 348). Colonial official whose investigative report provided the intelligence on Rangga's Surakarta connections.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Kartasura**: Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (not in current index, 400). Former capital of the Mataram empire west of Surakarta; site of illicit nocturnal meetings between Rangga and Surakarta emissaries.
  * **Surakarta (Sala)**: Capital of the Sunanate.
* **Terms:**
  * **kliwon**: Administrative deputy to a court *patih* or senior minister.
  * **wèdana mantri bumi**: Senior court official directing agrarian administration and royal land registers.
  * **patih**: Grand vizier / prime minister of the realm.
  * **Spanish dollar (Real batu / SpD)** (`Sp.D.`): Listed under *Currencies* (line 674). Standard currency used for court gifts and political subventions (`2,000 Sp.D.`).
