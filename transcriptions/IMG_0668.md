### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Adimenggala to Christiaans**<br>1 Dec. 1810<br>(4 Bakda Mulud, Wawu)<br><br>*Sukawati Devastation* | Kyai Tumênggung Adimenggala [bupati of Demak] to A.M. Christiaans [President van de Raad van Justitie & Acting Landdrost of Semarang, Demak, Kendal, and Kaliwungu]:<br>• Local inhabitants along the great Bengawan Solo [Sala River] in Sukawati report that Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] passed through on Tuesday, 27 November 1810.<br>• Rangga traversed Tarik, Usmawih, Sebuh, and Sangir, burning down over 200 houses of local villagers east of the Solo River.<br>• Rangga was met by an additional reinforcement of 300 retainers from Madiun, marching eastward while felling trees to obstruct the roads behind him. |
| **Four Yogya Regents in Pursuit**<br>Late Nov. – 1 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Dipakusuma in Command* | Four senior Yogyakarta regents lead the pursuit column against Rangga:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Radèn Aria Sindunegara** [later Danureja III]<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Radèn Tumênggung Mertalaya**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Major Tumênggung Wiranagara** [Wiraguna II]<br>• Accompanied by European dragoon detachments and 4 field artillery pieces.<br>• **Pangéran Dipakusuma** is formally placed as Supreme Commander over the four regents, currently holding positions west of the Solo River at Desa Pinggir.<br>• The Regent of Demak maintains scouts and spies along the borders of Grobogan and Jayèngsekar cavalry posts, coordinated with Patih Mangundipura. |
| **van Pabst to Winckelmann**<br>30 Nov. 1810<br>(Rembang Residency)<br><br>*Maospati Stronghold* | Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst [van Pabst, Resident of Rembang & Forestry Commissioner] to Brigadier F.C.P. von Winckelmann:<br>• Letter received from Pangéran Prangwedana [Mangkunagara II], who sends auxiliary troops via Blora to reinforce the front.<br>• Rumours claimed Rangga had 600 men in the regency of Padangan, but reconnaissance confirms he has only approximately 60 men in his personal escort.<br>• Local villagers in Padangan are forcibly conscripted to cut bamboo canes and supply oil to manufacture torches for nocturnal marches.<br>• **The Fortress of Maospati:** Rangga arrives at Maospati in the Madiun area, basing himself at his newly constructed *dalèm* [palace].<br>• The old town and palace of Madiun are completely ruined and fallen down; however, Maospati is protected by massive, thick stone walls recently constructed by Rangga, encompassing an extensive fortified compound. |
