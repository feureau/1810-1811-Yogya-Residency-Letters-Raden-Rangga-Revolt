| `6 Dec 1811` / `State of the Court of Djocjacarta By Mr. Crawfurd:`<br>(1) **The Sultan [HB II]:**<br>HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] when ascends throne conduct such as to gain the Heart of His subjects; in recent years alters v. [very] much for the worse.<br>Fear and not affection now the tie of the people's allegiance to Him:<br>Conduct of Sultan of late v. oppressive:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(1) Fines levied on the Rich on the most trivial pretexts.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(2) Sons seldom allowed to succeed Fathers without the payment of a Heavy Fine:<br>Chief of Gladhak pays `2,500 Sp.D.` [Spanish Dollars] for the privilege of taking His Father's Rank.<br>V. [Very] few Friends — Results in easy Dethronement by Daendels. Since then Sultan tries to win affection of the people — successfully. | **State of the Court of Yogyakarta by John Crawfurd (6 December 1811, `Mack. Pr. No. 21`):**<br>**Sultan Hamengkubuwana II:** Initially won his subjects' affection, but in later years ruled by fear through oppressive fines on the rich and heavy succession fines (e.g., the Chief of *Gladhak* paying `2,500` Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars] to inherit his father's rank). Having few friends led to his easy dethronement by Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, after which he worked successfully to regain popular affection. |
| **Sultan's Brothers:** Would be glad to see Him restored.<br>(1) `Pangeran Ngabehi`: a quiet, inoffensive person, a very old man.<br>(2) `P. Mangkukusuma`: Brother of Sultan (? HB II) 34 years old. A good man who meddles at no time in Public matters *(red: `of literature`)*.<br>(3) `P. Adikusuma`: a man of similar Disposition, Brother to this above.<br>(4) `P. Demang`: a Brother of HB II, 50 years old — different disposition, prepared to thwart HB II.<br>(5) `P. Panular`: Waterloo (`1808`) 26 years old, a widower. A man of a very good character who seeks to better His fortunes by the pursuit of mercantile affairs.<br>(6) `P. Adiwijaya: (Abubakar)` *(left margin: `(P. Abubakar.)`)*: a man of a good character. He has of late assumed the manners and Habits of a Devotee taking Arabic name of Abubakar. | **HB II's Brothers:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Ngabèhi:** Quiet, inoffensive, very old.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Mangkukusuma:** 34, good man, literary, uninvolved in public affairs.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Adikusuma:** Similar disposition.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Demang:** 50, prepared to thwart HB II.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Panular:** Widower (Waterloo noted age 26 in 1808), good character, pursues mercantile affairs.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Adiwijaya (Abubakar):** Good character, recently adopted devotee habits under the Arabic name Abubakar. *(Continued on [IMG_0844.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0844.md).)* |
