## Metadata
**Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0541_rotated_90.jpg`  
**Archival Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37 (Ampèl Water Rights / Procès-Verbal of Cakranêgara)  
**Date:** 1810-09-13 (Javanese: Thursday 13 Ruwah, Wawu, 1737 [13 September 1810]; citing inquest of Monday Legi 19 Rejeb / 21 August [Oogstmaand] 1810)  
**Key Events:**
* Judicial Examination of the Ampèl Canal Blockade: Kyai Mohammad Jayiman of desa Panataran (Kapantaran) appears before Patih DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to answer charges of illegally damming up the strategic irrigation channel supplying water to the Ampèl agricultural estate. In punishment for the reported disruption, his substantial monthly stipend of 214 Spanish Dollars [Sp. D.] was stopped.
* The Surakarta Joint Inquest (21 August [Oogstmaand] 1810 / Mon. Legi 19 Rejeb 1737): Jayiman recounts that emissaries from the Surakarta Prime Minister's department (*priyayi kepatihan Sala*) headed by Mas Risadiwiria and Kyai Dêmang Patra Dino (Patra Dirana) of Gondang, accompanied by boundary elders (*matapat*) from both kingdoms, visited Panataran to investigate the drought at Ampèl.
* Evidence on Upstream Water Theft: Jayiman explains that neither he nor his villagers blocked the sluice; rather, unauthorized upstream cultivators diverted the current before it reached Ampèl.
* Testimony on Ancient Water Allocations (*Adat*): Kyai Mêrta Dinata (father of Kyai Mohammad Jayiman) was summoned to elucidate ancient water entitlements. He stated that the canal volume had historically been divided into three equal portions:
  1. One portion to Yogyakarta;
  2. One portion to Surakarta;
  3. One portion to the Gladhag transport guild of Ampèl (*Gladhag of Ampèl*).
* Revised Water Protocol: The joint delegation ruled that to ensure sufficient volume reaches Ampèl, the watercourse must henceforth be regulated into six shares, with two portions each distributed to Surakarta, Yogyakarta, and the Gladhag of Ampèl.
