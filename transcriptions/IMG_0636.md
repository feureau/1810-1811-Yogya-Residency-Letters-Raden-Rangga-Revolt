### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eng. to v. Pabst**<br>3 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Pagêlaran Decree* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] to P.H. van Pabst [Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst] (3 Dec. 1810):<br>• HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]'s official royal decree denouncing Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] as a rebel and traitor is formally proclaimed and posted at the *Pagêlaran* [grand public audience pavilion of the Kraton].<br>• The decree instructs all subjects and officials to arrest Rangga on sight.<br>• *(Note)*: Eng. also writes to **Capt. Wellenberg** at Klatèn. |
| **Eng. to Snippnaert**<br>3 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Chinese Outreach* | Engelhard to Resident Snippnaert (3 Dec. 1810):<br>• In light of Rangga's proclamations urging Chinese communities in the mancanagara to rise and unite against the colonial Government, Engelhard orders immediate counter-measures.<br>• HB II's royal anti-rebel decree is translated into Chinese characters (*karakter Hanzi*).<br>• Certified copies are ordered sent to all eastern kabupatèns to be publicly posted in Chinese quarters and toll-stations to reassure Chinese merchants and prevent defection. |
| **Eng. to Paulus**<br>5 Dec. 1810 | Engelhard to Lieutenant Paulus (5 Dec. 1810):<br>• Resident encloses an official letter from DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] addressed directly to Purwadipura [Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura / PWD].<br>• Expresses HB II's intense anger and indignation at the extraordinary slowness of the royal punitive expedition.<br>• Proposes the deployment of fresh elite Kraton reinforcements under the direct personal command of the Prime Minister, Danureja II. |
| **DR II to PWD**<br>5 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Reprimand & Battle Orders* | Danureja II to Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura (5 Dec. 1810):<br>• DR II notes that Radèn Tumênggung Sasrawinata and Radèn Tumênggung Sasradipura have already reported the rebel assault on Magetan, yet Purwadipura failed to send any word.<br>• Demands why PWD has failed to report or engage the rebels.<br>• Proposes dispatching five senior royal princes/officials: Pangéran Aria Mangkubumi, Pangéran Kusumayuda, Pangéran Jayakusuma, Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat], and Panji Suryéngrana, leading 200 elite *prajurit* [infantrymen].<br>• Issues strict royal orders: Rangga must be captured alive or killed immediately if offering resistance; any cowardly deserters from royal ranks are to be shot without mercy. |
| **Eng. to Snippnaert**<br>5 Dec. 1810 | Engelhard to Snippnaert (5 Dec. 1810):<br>• *Oppasser* [orderly/messenger] sent by Engelhard to Rajêgwêsi on 21 Nov. arrives and is able to put Bupati Sasradiningrat on his guard. |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>6 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Defense of HB II* | Engelhard to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] (6 Dec. 1810):<br>• Daendels vehemently suspects Natadiningrat, Pangéran Natakusuma, and even HB II himself of treasonous complicity, threatening harsh military retribution against the Sultanate.<br>• Engelhard actively defends HB II's personal loyalty and innocence, citing the public proclamations, the official deployment of troops, and the Sultan's readiness to execute the rebel leader. |
