| `Report of Mangunliksana in Kedhu` (`29 Oct. 1812, p. 1081`):<br>Paku Alam / Sepoys: `wong cilik` in Kedhu affected by Rumour that HB III [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] had given orders to his subjects to put family (wife, children) + goods in safe keeping, because not able to oppose wilfulness of Sepoys; also `wong cilik` fear that grievance of PA [Paku Alam] that he could not get Kedhu back would cause a war. | **Report of Mangunliksana in Kedu (29 October 1812, p. 1081):** *Wong cilik* (commoners) in Kedu alarmed by rumours that HB III ordered subjects to safeguard their families and goods against Sepoy wilfulness, and fearing war over Paku Alam's grievance at not recovering Kedu. |
