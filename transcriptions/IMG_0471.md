### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III** (`Rangga`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (lines 173, 195, 213). *Bupati wedana* of Madiun whose secret departure from Yogyakarta on the night of 20 November 1810 marked the outbreak of the revolt.
  * **Pieter Engelhard** (`Eng`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 331). Resident of Yogyakarta (1808, 1810–1811) who coordinated the immediate colonial and court military mobilization.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 12). Reigned 1792–1810. Outwardly dispatched a royal punitive expedition against his son-in-law Radèn Rongga while secretly suspected of sympathizing with him.
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana III (Pangéran Ratu / Crown Prince)** (`PR II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 13). Reigned 1812–1814. Intermediary communicating through his court secretary.
  * **Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura** (`Purwadipura`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 190). Appointed supreme commander of the Sultan's 1,000-man punitive expedition to Madiun.
  * **Radèn Aria Sindunegara (Kyai Adipati Danureja III)** (`Sindunagara / Tum. Sindunagara`): Listed under *Javanese Officials, Commanders, and Local Leaders* (line 183). Yogyakarta court official and courier between the Kraton and the Residency; later *patih* (1811–1813).
  * **Tan Jin Sing (Kapitan Cina / Radèn Tumenggung Secadiningrat)** (`Tan Jin Sing`): Listed under *Javanese Officials / Chinese Captains of Yogya* (lines 193, 194). Prominent head of the Yogyakarta Chinese community who escorted Chinese officials and fugitives to the Resident.
  * **Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II)** (`MN`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 27). Head of the Mangkunagaran house whose mancanagara regents in East Java were alerted against Rongga's proclamations.
  * **Jacob Andries van Braam** (`v. Braam`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 307). Resident at Surakarta and Government Commissioner.
  * **Willem Nicolaas Servatius** (`Servatius`): Listed under *Residents at Surakarta* (line 308). Assistant Resident at Surakarta (1810–1811).
  * **Marshal Herman Willem Daendels** (`D / Daendels`): Listed under *Colonial Officials: Governors-General* (line 282).
  * **Lt. Paulus & Coachman Lucas Leberfeld**: Dutch military and court liaisons attached to Purwadipura's force.
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Madiun**: Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 401). Destination of Radèn Rongga's flight and capital of the eastern mancanagara.
  * **Yogyakarta Kraton**: Seat of the Sultan of Yogyakarta.
  * **Klathèn** (`Klaten`): Listed under *Places: Key Sites* (line 423). Colonial garrison post commanded by Müller.
  * **Grobogan**: Listed under *Places: Mancanagara* (lines 259, 390). Strategic district north of Solo alerted against insurgent recruitment.
  * **Semarang**: North coast military headquarters.
  * **Surakarta** (`Sala`): Capital of the Sunanate.
  * **Cirebon**: Listed under *Places: North Coast* (line 458). West Java coastal residency.
* **Terms:**
  * **prajurit (Kraton troops)**: Royal palace infantry armed with traditional pikes (`700 + pikes`) and flintlock muskets (`300 + muskets`).
  * **dragoons**: Mounted colonial cavalry providing armed escort.
  * **oppassers**: Colonial administrative couriers and native police constables.
