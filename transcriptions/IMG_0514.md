## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0514_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46: *R. Ronggo 1810–1811*)
- **Date Range:** 1810-03-25 to 1810-11-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Archival research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 46 tracking the trajectory of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III; top dispatch records Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees writing to Willem Nicolaas Servatius on 25 March 1810 requesting a postponement of judgment on the Rangga affair for several days; main lower section extracts intelligence from Wangsataruna sent to Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Cakranagara on 24 November 1810, detailing surveillance of Radèn Rongga's movements following his armed departure from Yogyakarta; reports Rongga's arrival on Thursday at 9:00 AM at desa Tinggil, where he convened a major war conference with 15 Yogyakarta eastern *mancanagara* regents (*bupati*): Radèn Tumenggung Yudakusuma (Wiwasari), Radèn Tumenggung Sasrakusuma (Grobogan), Radèn Tumenggung Sasradiwiria (Grobogan), Radèn Tumenggung Natawijaya (Panolan), Mas Tumenggung Sumanêgara (Padhangan), Radèn Tumenggung Prawirayuda (Sêkaran), Radèn Tumenggung Natadiwiria (Puri), Radèn Tumenggung Purwadiningrat (Magêtan), Radèn Tumenggung Cakradiwiria (Magêtan), Radèn Tumenggung Prawirasêntika (Bauwêrna), Radèn Tumenggung Pringgalaya (Kêrtasana), Radèn Tumenggung Pringgakusuma (Rawa), Mas Tumenggung Sumadipura (Japan), and Ngabèhi Jayadirana (Kalangbrèt); documents Rongga proposing to the assembled regents an immediate offensive campaign to capture Pasisir (north coast) towns by marching first on Jagaraga and advancing via Blora, before proceeding eastward on Friday morning.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Wangsataruna, Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II), Radèn Tumenggung Yudakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Sasrakusuma, Radèn Tumenggung Sasradiwiria, Radèn Tumenggung Natawijaya, Mas Tumenggung Sumanêgara, Radèn Tumenggung Prawirayuda, Radèn Tumenggung Natadiwiria, Radèn Tumenggung Purwadiningrat, Radèn Tumenggung Cakradiwiria, Radèn Tumenggung Prawirasêntika, Radèn Tumenggung Pringgalaya, Radèn Tumenggung Pringgakusuma, Mas Tumenggung Sumadipura, Ngabèhi Jayadirana.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Desa Tinggil, Wiwasari, Grobogan, Panolan, Padhangan, Sêkaran, Puri, Magêtan, Bauwêrna, Kêrtasana, Rawa, Japan, Kalangbrèt, Jagaraga, Blora, Pasisir (North Coast of Java).
