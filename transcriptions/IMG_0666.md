| **Engelhard to Winckelmann**<br>29 Nov. 1810<br>(1:00 a.m. Express) | Resident Engelhard to Brigadier F.C.P. von Winckelmann:<br>• Confirms that Radèn Tumênggung Sasrakusuma [R.T. Sasrakusuma] and the Grobogan district forces have openly gone over to Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga]. |
| **Semarang Landdrost to Engelhard**<br>29 Nov. 1810 | Landdrost of Semarang [UMS / J.M.S.] to Resident Engelhard:<br>• Reports rumours that Sasrakusuma of Grobogan has joined Rangga, though queries whether he is still opposing Rangga alongside the Sultan's troops. |
