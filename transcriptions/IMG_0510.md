# Transcription of IMG_0510_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0510_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37)
- **Date Range:** 1810-05-23 to 1810-08-25
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes summarizing dispatches from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37 between 23 May and 25 August 1810; records Crown Prince Pangéran Adipati Anom sending an investigative commission to look into the armed clashes initiated by Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III; documents Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels issuing a direct warning letter to Radèn Rongga holding him personally accountable for the cross-border attack in Panaraga; notes the arrival in Yogyakarta on 19 July 1810 of Sunan Pakubuwana IV's court royal herald (*gandhêk*), Prayamênggala, bearing an official royal missive dated 9 May regarding the appointment of Radèn Tumenggung Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II) and the deposition of former Solo Patih Radèn Adipati Danuningrat (noting that the Patih was not permitted to visit); records Sultan Hamengkubuwana II's formal reply of 19 July (16 Jumadilakir 1737) acknowledging the letter of his "Son" Sunan Prabu concerning Danuningrat's dismissal and Cakranagara's succession; documents Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees officially stepping down and resigning his post on 24 August 1810, handing over temporary charge of the Yogyakarta Residency to Acting Resident Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Jacob Andries van Braam, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Crown Prince (Pangéran Adipati Anom, later Sultan Hamengkubuwana III), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Willem Nicolaas Servatius, Sunan Pakubuwana IV, Prayamênggala (royal herald / *gandhêk*), Radèn Tumenggung Cakranagara (Radèn Adipati Sasradiningrat II), Radèn Adipati Danuningrat, Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Bartholomeus Jacobus Driessen.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Jogya / Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Panaraga (Ponorogo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37, Gandhêk (court messenger / herald), Patih succession dispute, Deposition / dismissal, Jumadilakir 1737 / Rabingulakir Jimawal to Wawu (Javanese calendar), Acting Resident, Residency handover.
