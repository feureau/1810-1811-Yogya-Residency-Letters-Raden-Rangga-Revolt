| `p. 19` `4. Chinese in Sala to be placed under Sunan's jurisdiction`:<br>Would not allow this — oppression of Chinese — to remain under European control. Wills / titles of Chinese to property protected under Dutch Law — so also to be `" "` by British `" "`. Old quarrel of Javanese - Chinese (`Prang Pacinan`). | **Article 4 (Jurisdiction over Chinese):** Refused; Chinese in Solo must remain under European control, with their wills and property titles protected under British law as under Dutch law (citing the old Javanese–Chinese conflict, *Perang Pacinan*). |
| `p. 20` `5. Revenues from Bird's Nests to be Restored`:<br>But not to be allowed by British. Otherwise Native princes would become too strong. As + Birds' Nests Rocks Belong to Sunan / Sultan would be a constant source of friction. | **Article 5 (Birds' Nests Revenues):** Refused, lest native princes grow too strong and because birds'-nest cliffs belonging to the Sunan/Sultan would be a constant source of friction. |
| `p. 21` `6. Mr van Braam to be Recalled`:<br>Has Been Done, But duplicity of Sunan to van Braam — outwardly friendly. | **Article 6 (Recall of van Braam):** Already done, though Raffles notes the Sunan's duplicity in appearing outwardly friendly toward Jacob Andries van Braam. |
| `7. New Resident to observe old ceremonial + come to court + Mangkubumi [Patih]`:<br>Sunan probably idea of Breaking contact Eur. [European] Res. [Resident] — Patih by designating Mangkubumi as sole source to Sunan.<br>Dependence of Patih on Company to be enforced as far as possible *(cf. like situation in Lucknow)*.<br>British Resident not to sit lower than Dutch Resident — representative of conquered [conquering] power. | **Article 7 (Court Ceremonial & Resident–Patih Relations):** Raffles suspects the Sunan wants to sever contact between the European Resident and the *Patih*; the *Patih*'s dependence on the Company must be enforced as far as possible (cf. Lucknow), and the British Resident must not sit lower than the Dutch Resident had. *(Continued on [IMG_0852.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0852.md).)* |
