MS 12341)*:<br>**The Prince Regent's revision of Hamengkubuwana II's policies:**<br>The official duties of the Yogyakarta Bupatis and deliveries of goods (timber, stone, etc.) by Yogyakarta inhabitants are to continue on the old footing as under Hamengkubuwana II. If Raja Putra Narendra requires construction, *uang kerigaji* (*sirap*) is to be used to pay for the materials.<br>A larger number of *prajurits* [soldiers] are being requisitioned than earlier in Hamengkubuwana II's reign (now 1,000 *cacahs* yielding 140–170–200 *prajurits*). Inhabitants must not misunderstand this: *prajurits* are only to be called up for the *Garebeg* festivals and special commissions. Previously, Hamengkubuwana II had recruited a small corps of *prajurits* from among his cousins, grandsons, and *panakawans* [retainers] who held no *sawah* lands. |
