### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Daendels to HB II**<br>Semarang, 23 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Abdication Ultimatum* | Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] to Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]:<br>• Acknowledges three letters from HB II (all dated 13 Dec. 1810) answering earlier ultimatums.<br>• Notes the Sultan admits he had been made the dupe of Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I] and Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III.<br>• Daendels and European officials are convinced that peace across Java becomes increasingly precarious as HB II ages, given the Sultan's deeply suspicious and jealous nature.<br>• **Denunciation of Ratu Kencana Wulan:** Out of diplomatic etiquette, Daendels refrained earlier from naming the most dangerous figure at court — Queen Consort Ratu Kencana Wulan, who was actively maneuvering to displace the Crown Prince and install Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] in his place.<br>• **Dynastic Peril:** Warns of further instability: If Ratu Kencana, currently advanced in pregnancy, gives birth to a male child, the legitimate Crown Prince [Pangéran Adipati Anom / future HB III] might be summarily cast aside.<br>• Declares that so long as this court intrigue festers, neither peace in Java nor the security of the Sultanate can be guaranteed.<br>• **The Demand for the Crown:** Daendels bluntly urges HB II to remove this existential danger: **HB II must place the royal crown directly into Daendels's hands, who will formally confer it upon the Crown Prince** *(emphasized with underline and exclamation mark)*.<br>• Announces Daendels will march on Yogyakarta in person to receive the Sultan's capitulation, traveling strictly *incognito*, with all customary court ceremonial waived during the visit. |
| **HB II to Daendels**<br>4 Bêsar 1737<br>(31 Dec. 1810)<br><br>*Royal Capitulation* | Sultan HB II to Marshal Daendels (31 Dec. 1810):<br>• The Sultan thanks Daendels for his dispatch.<br>• **Formal Abdication:** HB II formally consents to surrender the executive administration of the kingdom into the hands of his son, the Crown Prince (Prince Regent).<br>• Pledges to proclaim this change of sovereignty publicly to all Yogyakarta subjects and officials. |
| **Daendels to HB II**<br>2 Jan. 1811<br><br>*(Marked with red bars & underline)* | Marshal Daendels to HB II (2 Jan. 1811):<br>• Daendels initially insists upon physically removing the deposed Sultan from the Kraton precinct entirely, to insulate the new Prince Regent from undue paternal interference.<br>• **Filial Intervention of the Crown Prince:** The Prince Regent earnestly implores Daendels that **his father should be permitted to continue residing inside the Kraton** alongside the new court.<br>• Daendels concedes to the Crown Prince's passionate petition, agreeing to allow the Sultan Sepuh to remain within the palace, provided it does not disrupt the peace and tranquility of Mataram. |
