| Sunday 22 Sept. Breakfast + van Braam.<br>Detachment of 14th Light Infantry<br>1000s Javanese crowd streets to look at the English men.<br>Sunan + Courtiers await arrival since 18 Sept. Joyful Reception.<br>10 am Visits Sunan. | On Sunday, 22 September, breakfasted with Resident Jacob Andries van Braam. A detachment of the 14th Light Infantry accompanied them; thousands of Javanese crowded the streets to see the English. Sunan Pakubuwana IV and his courtiers had awaited their arrival since 18 September and gave them a joyful reception. Robison visited the Sunan at 10 a.m. |
| Evening Sunan visits Rob [Robison] at Residency -> conference + Interpreter / 2 Sunan's Ministers present.<br>Sunan produces 2 Letters sent from Tegal / Pekalongan. By Robinson. Does not Receive them via the Minister.<br>Sunan - says would not have given Dutch assistances *(red: `President Rob / Dutch / ? corr.`)* Bound to govt. [government]. | In the evening, the Sunan visited Robison at the Residency for a conference with an interpreter and two of the Sunan's ministers present. The Sunan produced two letters sent from Tegal and Pekalongan by Robison, noting he had not received them through the Resident/Minister, and stated he would not have given assistance to the Dutch beyond what he was bound to provide the government. |
| Complaints v. Daendels -<br>non payment of *strandgeld* / seizing of Lands He was not entitled to<br>`||` *Despoliation of Tombs / Burial places of ancestors sits hard on PB IV* *(red)*. | Complaints against Marshal Herman Willem Daendels: non-payment of *strandgeld* (coastal money), seizing of lands he was not entitled to, and especially the despoliation of ancestral tombs and burial places, which sat hard on Sunan Pakubuwana IV. |
