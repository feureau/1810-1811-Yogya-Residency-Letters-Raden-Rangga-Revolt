| **Sono & Wirasari Border**<br>Dec. 1810 | Burning of Solo *bekel* [village head] houses in Sono reported.<br>• Wirasari border area reported peaceful; local farming and weekly *pasar* [market] completely undisturbed.<br>• Radèn Tumênggung Prupakusuma (*patih* [chief administrator] of Grobogan) actively administering the regency and maintaining border security. |
| **Eng. to v. Braam**<br>5 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Bupati Wedana* succession | Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] to Admiral Pieter van Braam (5 Dec. 1810):<br>• Resident holds urgent discussions with DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] regarding the vacant appointment of *Bupati Wedana* [Senior Eastern Governor] of the Mancanagara following Rangga's rebellion.<br>• HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] adamantly refuses to appoint a single unified successor to Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga].<br>• The Sultan insists that each eastern regency continue to be administered independently under its respective younger regents (*tumênggung-tumênggung anem*). |
| **Solo Report no. 37**<br>(Wirataningrat to Cakranagara)<br>5 Dec. 1810 | Surakarta field intelligence report no. 37 (Radèn Tumênggung Wirataningrat to Radèn Adipati Cakranagara, 5 Dec. 1810):<br>• Magetan completely occupied and conquered by Rangga's rebel force; the bupati flees into the surrounding hills.<br>• Surakarta postal and relay station at Panaraga attacked and plundered.<br>• HB II severely reproaches Purwadipura [Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura / PWD] for his total lack of zeal, military inaction, and sluggish pursuit. |
| **Royal Mission Contemplated**<br>5 Dec. 1810 | HB II contemplates sending high-ranking royal envoys to persuade Rangga to submit:<br>• Sultan considers dispatching DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II], Pangéran Mangkubumi, or Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] to get Rangga back.<br>• Resident Engelhard strongly warns HB II against it. |
| **PB IV Accusation & Engelhard's Assessment**<br>*(Boxed in red ink with large red asterisk)*<br>5 Dec. 1810 | **Crucial Archival Entry:**<br>• Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] receives an urgent official letter from PB IV [Sunan Pakubuwana IV of Surakarta].<br>• PB IV explicitly accuses HB II of being secretly united and in full political collusion with Rangga against the colonial government.<br>• *(Double red line)*: `But attitude of HB II since flight of R arouses no suspicion.`<br><br>**Eng. to Daendels (5 Dec. 1810):**<br>• Reporting news of van Braam [or Sandjaert]; notes that on 24 Nov., Radèn Prawiralaya / Prawiradigdaya abandoned Rangga. |
| **Battle of Pusekan**<br>(Purwadipura to DR II, 1 Dec. 1810 / 4 Dulkangidah 1737)<br>Magetan district | Skirmish / Battle at Pusekan (Magetan):<br>• At **Desa Tarik**, Purwadipura receives a messenger from **Tumênggung Kêrtadirja** reporting the coming attack of Rangga on Magetan.<br>• Royal defending forces clash with rebel detachment under Rangga's direct banner at Pusekan of Magetan.<br>• Radèn Cakraprawira wounded during the engagement; 3 Javanese defenders killed.<br>• 8 of Rangga's insurgent followers killed, and 2 severely wounded, including the commander of the elite *prajurit Pananukta* [marksmen / vanguard escort]. |
