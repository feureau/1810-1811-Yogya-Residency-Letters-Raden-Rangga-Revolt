### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eastern Population Shifts**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Nala & Sekaran Flight* | Shifting loyalties in the borderlands:<br>• Inhabitants of the Nala crown lands spanning Jagaraga, Caruban, Blora, and Panaraga initially submitted to Rangga under duress, but promptly reaffirmed allegiance to their legitimate regents once royal columns advanced.<br>• The Regent of Sekaran flees his capital accompanied by 7 *mantris* [lower officials] and 60 civilians after insurgent infiltrators spread panic throughout the regency. |
| **van Pabst's Dispatch**<br>28 Nov. 1810<br>(corrected from 7 Dec.) | Landdrost Pieter Albertus van Lawick van Pabst to loyal Mancanagara [MN] regents:<br>• Transmits official text of Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II]'s royal proclamation (*undhang-undhang*) of 26 Nov. 1810 outlawing Rangga. |
| **Paulus to Eng.**<br>17 Dec. 1810<br>(Maospati)<br><br>*Prisoner Transfers* | Lieutenant Thomas Paulus to Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] (17 Dec. 1810):<br>• Dispatches high-ranking prisoners under armed escort to Yogyakarta:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Two captured brothers of Rangga (Radèn Prawirapreja and Radèn Sumaprawira)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Rangga's elderly mother<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Severed head of rebel vanguard general Dêsamuka (killed at Magetan)<br>• The European cavalry detachment of Dragoons is relieved and sent back to base.<br>• Royal princes and commanders in the field yearn for Rangga's swift demise so the expedition may disband. |
| **Paulus to Eng.**<br>18 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Pacitan Rumor Debunked* | Paulus to Engelhard (18 Dec. 1810):<br>• HB II's letter urging rapid offensive action is read aloud before Purwadipura [PWD]'s assembled troops.<br>• **Pacitan Threat Dismissed:** Bupati Radèn Tumênggung Pringgakusuma of Rawa provides definitive intelligence that **no Balinese vessels could land at Pacitan** due to treacherous surf and naval vigilance.<br>• Royal troops prepare to leave Maospati to scour the countryside.<br>• **Chronic Javanese Delay:** Paulus vents frustration that whenever he attempts to hasten the march, Purwadipura and native officers invariably reply: *"Nanti"* [later / presently].<br>• Pangéran Adinagara remains behind guarding Rangga's *dalèm* [palace]. |
| **Leberfeld to Paulus**<br>13 Dec. 1810<br>(Kèrtasana) | Sergeant / Coachman (*Koetsier*) Lucas Leberfeld to Lt. Paulus (13 Dec. 1810, written in native Javanese ink and pen):<br>• Reports that Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara had narrowly slipped past him at Kèrtasana.<br>• Leberfeld arrived at Kèrtasana on 12 Dec. only to find that Rangga had evacuated moments earlier, leaving the surrounding district deserted. |
