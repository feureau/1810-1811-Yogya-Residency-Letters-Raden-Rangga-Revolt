[Pieter Engelhard] to Daendels,<br>10 Nov. 1810.<br><br>*Note of Daendels:*<br>Instead of departing Eng. should have sat down again & remained sitting until HB II gets up.<br>Hope that would observe this ceremonial by sending over 2nd letter of Daendels. | Letter of Daendels concerning cases of Rangga [Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III] / 3 Patihs of Japan / + Robbery in Dêmak } presented by Eng. at court + usual ceremonial.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1) But postpones reading it because v. voluminous.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2) Because of indisposition cannot drink toast.<br><br>HB II says *sudah minister* [that is enough, Minister].<br>Eng. stands up / pays compliments to HB II but HB II remains sitting on dhampar [royal throne].<br>Eng. extremely amazed about it. |
| Eng. to Daendels,<br>6 Nov. 1810 | Peaceful in Yogya [Yogyakarta]: yesterday friendly visit from DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] + Kliwons = Sindunagara [Kyai Adipati Danureja III] / Danukusuma [Radèn Tumenggung Danukusuma I].<br>Kliwons go to kraton for sêdêkah (slamet-an) for wife of Rangga, Ratu Maduretna.<br>Kêparaks in kraton tell HB II as much afraid of own princes as of govmt.<br>except for Mangkudiningrat [Pangéran Mangkudiningrat I] = favourite of HB II.<br><br>Sumadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Sumadiningrat] urging HB II on against Dutch.<br>CP [Crown Prince / Pangéran Adipati Anom, later HB III] counsels ND [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] to keep his mouth shut.<br>Rangga expected in Yogya following Thursday. |
