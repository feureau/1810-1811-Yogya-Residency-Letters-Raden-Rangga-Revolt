| AN *'Djokjo Brieven' no. 24* — <span style="color:#a00">**Yogya. 31 Aug. 1811**</span><br>**Eng -> Janssens [Pieter Engelhard to Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens], 31 Aug.:**<br>Informs Regent [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] / DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] / *Capitan Cina* [Tan Jin Sing] of Proclamation by Ld. Minto (26 Aug.) `||`.<br>Troops ready to march to Semarang -> increased to **2,400** by Regent + **400** cavalry from the Kraton.<br>Yesterday salute from Fort for Sultan's promotion to the throne. | **Pieter Engelhard to Governor-General Janssens, 31 August 1811:**<br>Informed the Prince Regent, Danureja II, and the *Kapitan Cina* [Tan Jin Sing] of Lord Minto's proclamation (26 August).<br>The troops ready to march to Semarang were increased to **2,400** by the Regent, plus **400** cavalry from the Kraton. Yesterday a Fort salute was fired marking the anniversary of the Sultan's accession. |
| **Eng - Janssens, 31 Aug.:**<br>After deliberation in Kraton + knowledge of HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II], Regent + *Nayakas* decide to increase troops by `1,400`.<br>Refusal of Regent to send more troops because not certain of allegiance of princes *(would be exposing himself)*.<br>Eng would **NOT** put pressure on Regent to send more troops: *"Incomprehensible fear of Regent for his Father."*<br>Eng. **NOT** in a position to play on evil disposition of Father for Son: Regent cannot decide anything without HB II concerning the troops. | **Pieter Engelhard to Janssens, 31 August 1811 (second dispatch):**<br>Following Kraton deliberations with Hamengkubuwana II's knowledge, the Regent and *Nayakas* decided to add `1,400` troops, but the Regent refused to send any more, uncertain of the princes' allegiance (fearing he would expose himself). Engelhard declined to press for more, noting the *"incomprehensible fear of the Regent for his father"* and his own inability to exploit the father's ill-will toward his son: the Regent cannot decide anything regarding troops without Hamengkubuwana II. |
