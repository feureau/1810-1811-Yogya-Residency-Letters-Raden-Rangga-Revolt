Janssens, June/July/August) — **1811 DJOGJA:** <span style="color:#a00">**1810 (April-Nov) (1810-1811)**</span> — *No. 4 Correspondentie Tusschen den Minister aan het Hof van Yogyakarta en den Gouverneur-Generaal in Anno 1811*<br>Letters found in Library of P. Decision to keep them bound together by **van Braam** [Jacob Andries van Braam] (son-in-law of Engelhard). | **Djokja Letters No. 22 (AN 1811) — Retrospective Summary (April–November 1810):**<br>Correspondence found in Pieter Engelhard's library, kept bound together by Jacob Andries van Braam (Engelhard's son-in-law). |
| **P. Engelhard to Janssens [Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens]:**<br>Glad that Janssens had improved situation of **Natakusuma** [Pangéran Natakusuma] / **Natadiningrat** [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat] because Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels] had promised that they would be treated according to RANK. | **Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>Expresses satisfaction that Janssens improved the conditions of Natakusuma and Natadiningrat, fulfilling Daendels's promise that they would be treated according to their rank. |
| **30 APRIL 1810 ->**<br>SULTAN [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] sends **Morris** a letter for Daendels (*Res. of Djogja*) asking that Patih **Danu Redja** [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] be dismissed, saying that D.R. still a Boy; wishes to replace him by outer Bp. = **SINDA NEGARA** [Kyai Adipati Danureja III / Sindunagara].<br>Daendels refuses: Sinda Negara too old.<br>But Ratu Kentjana Wulong wishes to see **NATA DININGRAT** (son-in-law) as PATIH. | **30 April 1810:**<br>Sultan Hamengkubuwana II sent a letter via Resident Morris to Daendels requesting Danureja II's dismissal (calling him "still a boy") and replacement by outer Bupati **Sindunagara**. Daendels refused on grounds that Sindunagara was too old, while Ratu Kencana Wulan wanted her son-in-law **Natadiningrat** appointed Patih. |
| **JULY 1810:**<br>Pres. of Govnmt. **van Braam** comes on commission to Djogja. Negotiates Restitution of Patih D.R.<br>Nata Diningrat remains Chief canal to Sultan for affairs of state.<br>Patih only allowed to handle govnmt. affairs / relations + courts `||`; when meets Res. must be attended by a Bp. *Diaba* [`||`].<br>CROWN PRINCE [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] -> loses all influence with Sultan, but Nata Kusuma remains boss in the KRATON. | **July 1810:**<br>President van Braam arrived on commission in Yogyakarta and negotiated Patih Danureja II's reinstatement. However, Natadiningrat remained the Sultan's chief channel for state affairs; the Patih was restricted to government/court relations and had to be accompanied by a Bupati *diaba* [witness] when meeting the Resident. The Crown Prince lost all influence with the Sultan, leaving Natakusuma master in the Kraton. |
