| `Memoir for Adams: Signed Raffles, Semarang, 6 Dec. 1811` (`p. 17`):<br>Impress Sunan + British strength; every treaty + govnmt [government] to be held in complete force until new Treaty made. | **Memoir for Colonel Alexander Adams, signed by Thomas Stamford Raffles, Semarang, 6 December 1811** (`Mackenzie Private Coll. Vol. 2`, pp. 17–18): Instructs Adams to impress British strength upon Sunan Pakubuwana IV and uphold all existing treaties until a new treaty is concluded. |
| Sunan sends messengers to seize Birds' Nests after sending letter to Ld. [Lord] Minto via Robison asking for them back — v. [very] improper conduct. Does not inform van Braam, although van Braam pays rent of Birds' Nests in advance to `31 Dec. 1812`.<br>Sunan tells Chinese who Rent tolls under van Braam not to pay vB [van Braam] their rents.<br>`p. 18`: endeavours to get money for 2nd time in same year from tolls.<br>"it seems not to be good policy to trust anything to contingent recovery from a person of that stamp." | **Improper Conduct by PB IV:** After petitioning Lord Minto via Captain William Robison, the Sunan sent messengers to seize the birds' nests without informing Resident Jacob Andries van Braam (who had already paid rent in advance through 31 December 1812), and told Chinese toll-farmers not to pay van Braam, attempting to collect toll money twice in the same year. Raffles remarks: *"it seems not to be good policy to trust anything to contingent recovery from a person of that stamp."* |
| `Raffles' Remarks on Sunan's Requests:`<br>1. `to give peace + quiet to subjects` — already done.<br>2. `Return of Kramats [Holy grounds] on N. coast + those in charge of them` — more explanation needed — seems that Sunan asks for cession of territory usually looked after by *jurukuncis* appointed by Sunan.<br>3. `Restoration Sp.D. 10,000 Strandgeld` -> Refusal of govnmt [government] to pay this, but would pay double for Rent of Farms from Sunan. | **Raffles' Remarks on the Sunan's Requests (Arts. 1–3):**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1. Peace and quiet: already granted.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2. Return of sacred graves (*kramats*) on the N. coast: needs explanation, as the Sunan appears to be asking for territorial cessions beyond his usual *jurukunci* caretakers.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3. Restoration of `10,000` Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars] *strandgeld*: refused, though Government would pay double rent for farms leased from the Sunan. *(Continued on [IMG_0851.md](file:///f:/GitHub/1810-1811%20Yogya%20Residency%20Letters%20Raden%20Rangga%20Revolt/transcriptions/IMG_0851.md).)* |
