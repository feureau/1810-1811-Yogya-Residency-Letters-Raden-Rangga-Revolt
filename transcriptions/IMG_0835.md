## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Raffles -> Crawfurd 25 Dec 1811 [Surakarta]`:<br>acknowledges Crawfurd's letter `24th dated (12 p.m.)` arrives Solo `2 p.m. 25 Dec.` | **25 December 1811 — Thomas Stamford Raffles (Surakarta) to John Crawfurd:** Acknowledges Crawfurd's letter of 24 December (12 p.m.), which arrived at Solo at 2 p.m. on 25 December. |
| `Private and Confidential letter written yesterday:`<br>..."in which I stated what appeared to me to be the Best and only line of policy which under existing circumstances should be pursued and I have much satisfaction in finding from your communication now before me that the same has so much the appearance of success."... | Refers to his private and confidential letter of the previous day outlining the best policy under existing circumstances, expressing satisfaction that Crawfurd's report shows it appearing to succeed. |
| "It will be for me to effect by Force what may not be managed by negotiation — But I sincerely trust that your influence added to the measures taken by the commission without whose concurrence you are in no case to act, may supersede the immediate effect of hostilities which under no circumstances short of the most absolute and peremptory necessity can be omitted." | Raffles states he will effect by force what cannot be managed by negotiation, but trusts that Crawfurd's influence and the Commission (without whose concurrence Crawfurd must in no case act) will avoid immediate hostilities unless under absolute necessity. |
| `Much confidence in Crawfurd's assurances`: that could achieve everything they might wish and that peace of countryside would not be materially affected... *notwithstanding the present unpleasant appearances.* | Expresses confidence in Crawfurd's assurances that their aims can be achieved without materially affecting rural peace, despite unpleasant appearances. |
| `Troops not to proceed beyond Klaten` / `But advance escort to Prambanan`: necessary measures to be taken by Crawfurd to receive `100 Cavalry + 300 Infantry` at PRAMBANAN `26 Dec` or early `27 Dec 1811`.<br>`Letter to be shown To gentlemen of the Commission:` | **Troop Movements:** Main troops not to proceed beyond Klathèn, while an advance escort of `100` cavalry and `300` infantry is to be received by Crawfurd at Prambanan on 26 or early 27 December 1811.
