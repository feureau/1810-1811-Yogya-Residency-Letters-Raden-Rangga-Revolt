# Transcription of IMG_0513_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0513_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (Mas Rangga Kartawiria Dispatches to Radèn Adipati Cakranagara)
- **Date Range:** 1810-11-24
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes transcribing critical military intelligence dispatches of 24 November 1810 from Mas Rangga Kartawiria to Surakarta Patih Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II) reporting the armed breakout and flight of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III from Yogyakarta; records that Rongga fled Yogya pursued by royal Yogyakarta commanders Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura, Radèn Tumenggung Sindurêja, Radèn Tumenggung Mêrtalaya, Radèn Tumenggung Wirianêgara, Radèn Panji Jayêngrana, and the Regent (*bupati*) of Kêrja; notes that Rongga spent Friday night at desa Pinggir near the Bengawan Sala river, where his forces burned down the appanage village (*desa*) of Pondhok belonging to Pangéran Mangkubumi; documents Rongga arriving at Kêbon Rarèt and intercepting Mas Tumenggung Sumanêgara (Bupati of Padhangan), who was travelling towards Yogya and joined Rongga's rebellion; notes that Surakarta villages of Piji, Pêdes, and Poedak were seized by the Dêmang of Tarik, a partisan of Rongga; records an armed encounter at desa Tinggil where Rongga met 100 mounted subjects (70 cavalry), summoned the troop commanders (Radèn Tirtadirja, Radèn Mangunrana, Radèn Jayanintara, Radèn Mangundirana, Radèn Wangsakusuma, Radèn Nicajaya, and Radèn Kêrtadiwiria), seized 70 horses, pikes, and krises by force, and warned the leaders that those wishing to live should return to their villages rather than continue to Surakarta; concludes with Rongga announcing his advance toward Blora to liberate and recapture the Pasisir (north coast) towns, celebrated upon the commanders' assent with a two-cannon artillery salute and ringing shouts of "Hurrah!"
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Mas Rangga Kartawiria, Radèn Adipati Cakranagara (Sasradiningrat II), Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Radèn Tumenggung Purwadipura, Radèn Tumenggung Sindurêja, Radèn Tumenggung Mêrtalaya, Radèn Tumenggung Wirianêgara, Radèn Panji Jayêngrana, Pangéran Mangkubumi, Mas Tumenggung Sumanêgara, Dêmang of Tarik, Radèn Tirtadirja, Radèn Mangunrana, Radèn Jayanintara, Radèn Mangundirana, Radèn Wangsakusuma, Radèn Nicajaya, Radèn Kêrtadiwiria.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Desa Pinggir, Bêngawan Sala (Solo River), Desa Pondhok, Kêbon Rarèt, Padhangan, Desa Piji, Desa Pêdes, Desa Poedak, Desa Tinggil, Blora, Pasisir (North Coast of Java).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Outbreak of the Radèn Rongga Revolt (1810), Patih of Surakarta, Bupati of Kêrja, Appanage desa destruction, Cavalry (*kuda*), Confiscation of pikes (*tumbak*) and krises, Anti-colonial liberation march, Two-cannon salute, "Hurrah!" acclamation.
