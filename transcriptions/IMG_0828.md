## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Meets van Braam / Waterloo -> in Semarang (6 Dec. 1811)`<br>`Difference in Copying of Dutch / Javanese CONTRACT + THE SULTAN HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] in 1792` | **6 December 1811 (Semarang):** Raffles meets Jacob Andries van Braam and Waterloo in Semarang; notes a discrepancy in the copying of the Dutch and Javanese versions of the 1792 contract with Sultan Hamengkubuwana II. |
| `Requests CR's [Crawfurd's] REPORT as soon as possible -` `ON the Raising of 2 Yogya Princes (NK [Pangéran Natakusuma] / ND [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat]) previously Exiled by Daendels -`<br>"Although I still have it in contemplation eventually to Re-establish the Sultan under certain conditions as a necessary measure. This must happen without putting the might of the Brit. [British] Govnmt. [Government] into Doubt."<br>"I shall conclude the Letter by observing that If the Sultan proves Intractable and his continuance in the government dangerous I am much inclined to lean to the policy of subjecting the native administration of the Sultan's Lands to the court of the Emperor."<br>`HOPES TO CONCLUDE A TREATY:` *(red star)*<br>"I expect to be able with the assistance of Col. [Colonel] Adams and yourself to fix finally the Necessary Treaties with the Emperors and Sultans court on my intended Arrival, and I request you will be prepared accordingly with everything that may be Requisite and conducive in point of Formality and local information for the purpose." | **Raffles to Crawfurd on HB II & Natakusuma / Natadiningrat:** Requests Crawfurd's report on the recall of Pangéran Natakusuma and Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat (exiled by Marshal Herman Willem Daendels). Raffles states he still contemplates eventually re-establishing HB II under conditions without compromising British authority, but warns that if the Sultan proves intractable, he is inclined to place Yogyakarta's native administration under the Sunan's court. Hopes to conclude final treaties with both courts upon arrival with Colonel Alexander Adams and Crawfurd. |
| `14 Dec 1811` (`CR <- Raffles -> CR (1) 2 letters Semarang`):<br>`On Receipt of CR's Letter 11 Dec - DECISION TO RETURN NK -> Yogya`<br>"I have had much communication with him on all points relating to the affairs of Djocjakarta and I am so favourably impressed with his superior talents and favourable sentiments that I expect the most satisfactory result for his mission." | **14 December 1811 — Raffles (Semarang) to Crawfurd (No. 1):** Upon receiving Crawfurd's letter of 11 December, Raffles decides to return **Pangéran Natakusuma** to Yogyakarta, praising his superior talents and favourable sentiments after extensive discussions. |
