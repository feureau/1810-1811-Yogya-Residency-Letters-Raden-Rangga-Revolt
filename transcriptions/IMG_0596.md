### Physical Artifact Description
1. **Paper Leaf:**
   * Single loose leaf of off-white reprographic copy paper serving as the official archival target and order header for the microfilm / photostat reproduction of the *Jogja 27* archive.
   * Central horizontal handling fold crease.
   * Top edge displays two binder punch holes with oxidised brass eyelets/rings and dark brown ferrous rust staining along the upper right margin.
   * Faint orange crayon/pencil notation at center: `(vol) ->`.

2. **Institutional Form Stamp:**
   * Prominent rectangular blue-inked administrative form stamp executed by the Reprography Division of the National Archives of Indonesia (*Arsip Nasional Republik Indonesia / Bagian Reprography*), filled in with blue ballpoint handwriting:
     ```text
     +-------------------------------------------------------------+
     |             ARSIP NASIONAL REPUBLIK INDONESIA               |
     |                    BAGIAN REPROGRAPHY                       |
     +-------------------+-----------------------------------------+
     | No. Arsip         | Jogja 27                                |
     | Jenis             | brieven (Holl. tekst)                   |
     | Kelompok arsip    | Jogja                                   |
     | Tanggal           | 28 Mei 1973.                            |

     ```
