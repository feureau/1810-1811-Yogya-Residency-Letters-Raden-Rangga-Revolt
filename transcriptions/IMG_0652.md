### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DR II to Purwadipura**<br>12 Dec. 1810<br>(15 Dulkangidah 1737)<br><br>*Demolition of Fortress* | Radèn Adipati Danureja II [DR II] to Purwadipura [PWD] (12 Dec. 1810, Wednesday):<br>• Urges Purwadipura to assault and destroy Rangga without delay wherever he can be tracked.<br>• Pangéran Adinagara and Radèn Tumênggung Sasradiningrat [SD] assigned to guard Rangga's palace (*dalèm*) in Madiun.<br>• **Sultan's Vindictive Decree:** HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] orders that the new stone fortress city (*kutha*) of Madiun constructed by Rangga be completely razed to the ground.<br>• **Desacralization of Tomb:** Mount Pacalan, recently renamed *Gunung Giri Purna* by Rangga, is ordered restored to its ancient name.<br>*(Carey notes in red: Burial place of Ratu Maduretna)*<br>• **Ultimatum to Commanders:** If Purwadipura and the Mancanagara [MN] regents fail to capture or kill Rangga before the expiration of the sacred month of Dulkangidah, they shall all be stripped of office and replaced by new regents.<br>• HB II proclaims an immense royal reward for whoever delivers Rangga dead or alive. |
| **Relaas of Sumawijaya**<br>16 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Padagangan Resistance* | Deposition (*relaas*) of Sumawijaya (16 Dec. 1810):<br>• Sumawijaya and his brothers — Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara and Sumawêrdaya — were initially coerced by Rangga into retreating to Madiun.<br>• On 22 Nov., Sumanêgara was dispatched back to Padagangan to organize local military resistance.<br>• Four days later (c. 26 Nov.), HB II's royal anti-rebel proclamation arrives, prompting active cooperation with Sasradiningrat in Rajêgwêsi for regional defense.<br>• On 11 Dulkangidah 1737 (8 Dec. 1810), Sumawijaya was sent by Natadiningrat with dispatches for DR II in Yogyakarta; finding DR II away in Semarang, the letters were delivered to Kyai Tumênggung Sindunagara.<br>• Notes that Sumanêgara remains temporarily detained with Rangga, but Sumawêrdaya has successfully escaped. |
| **Proclamation of Rangga**<br>Cabian in Jipang<br>15–17 Dec. 1810<br><br>*(Red: To Capitan Cina)* | **Rangga's Proclamations to Chinese Coastal Authorities:**<br>Issued from his mobile encampment at the *pesanggrahan* [resthouse] of Cabian in Jipang (dated Friday night 14/15 Dulkangidah / 15 Dec. 1810):<br>• Proclaimed under imperial regnal style: **Sunan Prabu Ingalaga**, of the *Kraton Pinarjurit* [Fortress of Warriors] at Maospati/Madiun.<br>• Declares himself now: *"wandering about to wage war"* (*lêmbara ing aprang*).<br>• Formally addresses imperial mandates to coastal Chinese leaders:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Kyai Kapitan Kuci** (Head of Chinese community in Lasêm)<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Baba Baeng Sing** (Head of Chinese community in Rêmbang)<br>• Urges the Chinese merchant leadership to recognize his imperial authority and sever financial ties with the colonial Government. |
