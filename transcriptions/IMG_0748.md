| *Djokja Letters (Secret) No. 22, A.N. 1811* — <span style="color:#a00">**1811 (July)**</span> — *1811 DJOGJA:*<br>**P. Engelhard [Pieter Engelhard] writes to Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels] (`2 Dec. 1810` / `? 30 Nov. 1810`):**<br>Persuades SULTAN [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] to agree to transport of N.K. [Pangéran Natakusuma] / N.D. [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat].<br>Assurances that N.K. / N.D. to be treated according to their Rank with full Honours + complements of PRADJURID.<br>Says that N.K. would make up following in Kedhu and N.D. in Mataram to oust SULTAN and set up RANGGA [Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III]. | **2 December 1810 (`? 30 Nov. 1810`) — Engelhard to Daendels:**<br>Persuaded the Sultan to agree to the transport of Natakusuma and Natadiningrat, assuring him they would be treated according to their rank with full honors and *prajurit* escorts, while warning that Natakusuma planned to raise followers in Kedhu and Natadiningrat in Mataram to oust the Sultan and install Radèn Rongga. |
| **MEETING + SULTAN RADJA [Sultan Hamengkubuwana III] [29 July 1811] OVER SURRENDER OF LANDS:**<br>**DJAPAN** (`1,000 cacahs`): BIRTHPLACE of Mother **RANU KENTJANA WULAN** [Ratu Kencana Wulan]; concern over burial places of family which would be lost.<br>*(Margin note: ? Regent of Sumadipura chap. / See Poensen p. 187, Mantri in Japan (Puspadipura) rekening Ratu Kidhanan)*<br>Complains that SOLO [Surakarta] only cedes `1,500`–`1,600` [to] Djogja [Yogyakarta].<br>But at **BOEJOLALIE**: Daendels orders N.K. / N.D.'s following to be cut down. | **Meeting with the Sultan / Regent (29 July 1811) over Land Surrenders:**<br>Concern over **Japan** (`1,000 cacahs`), birthplace of Ratu Kencana Wulan's mother, and the loss of ancestral family burial sites; complains that Surakarta only ceded `1,500`–`1,600` to Yogyakarta. (Notes that earlier at Boyolali, Daendels ordered Natakusuma and Natadiningrat's retinue cut down.) |
| **Letter from N. [Matthijs] Waterloo = *opperkoopman* CHERIBON (30 July 1811):**<br>Sends 5 wives of N.K. back to Djogja.<br>BOTH N.K. and N.D. have high fevers: extremely dangerous at this time of year.<br>**12 July 1811:**<br>N.K. VISITS SULTAN IN KRATON (+ RANU KENTJANA WULAN); had already been in touch via Intermediary, a PRIEST.<br>Dangerous because since murder of PATIH [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] *(important lack)*, PATIH SINDU NEGARA [Kyai Adipati Danureja III] too old to have much effect.<br>Power of Dutch BROKEN in KRATON. | **Letter from M. Waterloo (*Opperkoopman*, Cirebon), 30 July 1811:**<br>Sending Natakusuma's five wives back to Yogyakarta; both Natakusuma and Natadiningrat suffer from high fevers, very dangerous at this season.<br>**12 July 1811:** Natakusuma visited the Sultan and Ratu Kencana Wulan in the Kraton (having already communicated via a priest intermediary). This is dangerous because since the murder of Patih Danureja II, Patih Sindunagara is too old to be effective, and Dutch power in the Kraton is broken. |
