### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HB II to Daendels**<br>12 Nov. 1810<br>(Slagtmaand)<br><br>*Dutch Translation* | Sultan HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] (translated by Dutch translator C. Krijgsmann):<br>• HB II receives an official letter from Daendels demanding formal satisfaction for the aggressive conduct of Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] against Surakarta.<br>• The Sultan avoids specific mention of the border violations, responding evasively that Daendels must place complete trust in HB II's royal honor and integrity. |
| **van Braam & Eng. to D.**<br>13 Nov. 1810<br>(in Yogyakarta)<br><br>*(Red bracket: Escort)* | Extract of joint report from Commissioner Jacob Andries van Braam [J.A. van Braam] and Resident Pieter Engelhard [P. Eng] to Marshal Daendels (13 Nov. 1810):<br>• Demands that Rangga be sent in person to Buitenzorg [Bogor] to account directly to the Governor-General for his hostile actions against Sunan Pakubuwana IV [PB IV].<br>• **Four Escort Regents Designated by HB II:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Radèn Tumênggung Danukusuma I**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Pangéran Dipakusuma**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Radèn Tumênggung Pringgakusuma**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Mas Tumênggung Sumanêgara**<br>• These senior nobles are formally commissioned by HB II to escort Rangga under heavy guard to Buitenzorg. |
| **Escort Planning Conference**<br>14 Nov. 1810<br>(Slagtmaand)<br><br>*Auspicious Day* | Diplomatic conference with Resident Engelhard (14 Nov. 1810):<br>• DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II], Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat], Danukusuma, and Pangéran Dipakusuma confer with Engelhard to finalize logistical and security details for Rangga's overland journey to Bogor.<br>• **Auspicious Departure Date:** **Monday, 26 Nov. 1810** is formally selected as the departure date, having been calculated by royal court priests (*abdi-dalem pamêthan*) as spiritually propitious and lucky (*dina prayoga*) for the Sultan.<br>• **Command Substitution:** Pangéran Dipakusuma urgently requests to be excused from the escort mission; HB II appoints Radèn Tumênggung Martalaya in Dipakusuma's place. |
