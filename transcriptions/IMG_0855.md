## Diplomatic & Standardized Transcription

| Left Column (Original / Diplomatic Notes) | Right Column (Standardized / Narrative Flow) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Adams to Raffles, 3 Dec. 1811` (`p. 26`):<br>Sunan [Pakubuwana IV] fully aware that van Braam would object + hopes would escape Adams' knowledge.<br>`p. 27`: Brit. [British] govnmt [government] should express disapproval.<br>Some Relaxation of Dutch policy called for: if govnmt takes over farms to pay more for them than Dutch did. Value of farms would Rise `∴` more should be given to Sunan.<br>"It will be politic to make him more independent in his circumstances for at present he is certainly very poor and may be thereby rendered desperate."<br>European inhabitants lend money to Sunan — should be prohibited by British govnmt. (`p. 28`: `? Removal of Europeans concerned to Batavia.`) | **3 December 1811 — Colonel Alexander Adams to Thomas Stamford Raffles (pp. 26–28):** Urges the British Government to express disapproval of the Sunan's conduct, yet relax Dutch fiscal policy by paying the Sunan more for revenue farms: *"It will be politic to make him more independent in his circumstances for at present he is certainly very poor and may be thereby rendered desperate."* Recommends prohibiting Europeans from lending money to the Sunan and potentially removing offending Europeans to Batavia. |
| `p. 29`: `Raffles to Adams, 6 Dec. 1811 / 7 Dec. 1811 (late at night at Semarang)`:<br>Instructions Regarding Treaty + Court of Sala.<br>Pressing need to settle + princes because of temper of courts.<br>Raffles to Surabaya on `11 Dec.` to Return to Semarang on `14 Dec. 1811` -> to Solo `15 Dec.` -> `18/19 Dec.` to Yogya.<br>Col. [Colonel] Watson + `100` of Light Infantry Battalion to Solo immediately. | **6–7 December 1811 — Raffles (Semarang) to Adams (p. 29):** Instructions on the Solo treaty, stressing the urgent need to settle with the princes given the temper of the courts. Outlines Raffles' itinerary (Surabaya 11 Dec., return Semarang 14 Dec., Solo 15 Dec., Yogyakarta 18/19 Dec.) and orders Colonel Watson with 100 Light Infantry to Solo immediately. |
