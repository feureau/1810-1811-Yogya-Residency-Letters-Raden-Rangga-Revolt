# Transcription of IMG_0509_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0509_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37)
- **Date Range:** 1810-05-01 to 1810-05-22
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Research notes from ANRI *Djokjo Brieven* no. 37 recording correspondence between Resident Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Jacob Andries van Braam, and Governor-General Herman Willem Daendels during May 1810; notes the detention at regional tollgates (*bandhar*) of 150 *kodhis* (corges / bundles of 20 pieces) of Bagelèn textiles destined for the *Groote Oost* (Great East / eastern islands) and urges free transit; documents the mandated construction of dedicated secure storehouses at tollgates for transit opium and ammunition; details diplomatic protocols communicated by Crown Prince Pangéran Adipati Anom indicating that Sultan Hamengkubuwana II would receive Moorrees under Daendels' *Nieuwe Reglement* (New Ceremonial), while raising the crucial protocol question of whether Moorrees should sit alongside the Sultan upon the royal stone throne (*gilang*), matching the status formerly accorded the Governor of Java's Northeast Coast in Semarang; transcribes reports from Surakarta regent Tuan Aria Wiriadiningrat in Panaraga detailing the initial armed incursions and border clashes caused by Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III; documents Daendels' initial instruction to defer decisive action pending further clarification, proposing that Hamengkubuwana II and Sunan Pakubuwana IV establish a joint investigative commission.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Johannes Wilhelmus Moorrees, Jacob Andries van Braam, Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Crown Prince (Pangéran Adipati Anom, later Sultan Hamengkubuwana III), Sultan Hamengkubuwana II, Tuan Aria Wiriadiningrat, Radèn Rongga Prawiradirdja III, Sunan Pakubuwana IV.
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Bagelèn, Groote Oost (Eastern Archipelago), Yogyakarta (Yogya), Surakarta (Sala), Semarang, Panaraga (Ponorogo).
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** Kodhi (corge / 20 textiles), Bandhar (tollgate / customs post), Opium storage depot, Ammunition cache, Gilang (royal stone throne seat), Nieuwe Reglement (ceremonial etiquette), Javanese Governor of Semarang, Joint court commission.
