[Prime Minister] DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] from his offices and dignities 'for reason of his manifest bad conduct and management'... In his place — eldest son of former P.M. (DR I) **Kyai Adipati Danureja**... 'May it induce the prosperity of Java in particularly Yogyakarta Adiningrat'... 'HB II Sultan begs that confidence may be placed in His Highness and His son the Kangjeng Raja Putra Narendra.'" | **Summary & Excerpts:** Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and Crown Prince Raja Putra Naréndra (*Pangéran Adipati Anom Amangkunegara*) notify Raffles that His Highness has dismissed his Prime Minister, Radèn Adipati Danureja II, from his offices and dignities *"for reason of his manifest bad conduct and management"*, and appointed in his place the eldest son of the former Prime Minister (Danureja I), **Kyai Adipati Danureja [Danureja III / Sindunagara I]**, praying it may bring prosperity to Java and particularly Yogyakarta Adiningrat, and requesting confidence in both the Sultan and the Crown Prince. |
