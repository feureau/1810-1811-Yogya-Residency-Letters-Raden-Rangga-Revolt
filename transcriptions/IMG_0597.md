## Metadata

* **Archetype:** Archetype 1 (Royal Kraton Decree / Javanese Script Archival Manuscript)
* **Date:** 1810-11-21 (23 Sawal 1737 / Tuesday Wage / Malêm Rêbo)
* **Archive Reference:** ANRI *Djokjo 27* (Original Javanese Text of HB II's Royal Order)
* **Author / Scribe:** Royal Kraton Scribe (*Carik Dalêm*); Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (royal command)

### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Center (Pencil):** `45` (faint, to the left of the left eyelet).
* **Top Right (Modern Ink):** `21 Nov. 1810`
* **Right Margin (Line 9):** `810)`
* **Top Left (Red Ink):** `VI` (underlined); circular blind royal/archival embossed seal at upper left corner.
* **Bottom Left (Purple Archival Stamp):** `ARSIP NASIONAL REPUBLIK INDONESIA` (Garuda seal).
* **Physical Characteristics:** Contemporary 19th-century Javanese court rag paper folio leaf; written in flowing, elegant Yogyakarta kraton chancery script (*aksara Jawa*) in black/brown ink; two binder punch holes across the top edge with brass grommets and oxidised rust halos (plus a rust/staple mark at the upper left corner); central horizontal fold crease. Original Javanese text corresponding to the official Dutch translation preserved in `IMG_0593` and `IMG_0595`.
