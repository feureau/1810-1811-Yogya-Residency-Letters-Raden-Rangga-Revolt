### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Center (Red Bleed-through):** `23 Nov. - 24 Nov.` (bleed-through from recto)
* **Physical Characteristics:** Lined notebook leaf, 33 blue ruled horizontal lines, red vertical margin line; 2 punch holes at right edge (verso of leaf); blue ballpoint pen text with red ink underlines and margin bracket. Documents the immediate aftermath of Radèn Rongga's rebellion: royal family abjurations, safeguarding Pacitan artillery, Yogya troop mobilizations under Radèn Tumênggung Purwadipura and Outer Regents, and reports from Assistant Resident Servatius.
