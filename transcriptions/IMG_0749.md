## Annotated Transcription

| Original Text & Annotations | English Translation / Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **15 July 1811 — Janssens [Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens] -> Engelhard [Pieter Engelhard] (Batavia):**<br>Engelhard when first takes over post of Minister (1808): borrows large sum of money (`50,000 guilders`) from SULTAN [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II].<br>Reason for recent trouble with SULTAN: `?` due to preferential treatment of SOLO [Surakarta] `||`.<br>Unnecessary to push on with the line.<br>FINANCIAL STRAITS of the GOVNMT.: *(double red bar)* all silver money have dried up; copper money not enough to fill in gap.<br>**LOAN from SULTAN or from PATIH [Radèn Adipati Danureja II]:**<br>Only way the govnmt. could get by financial problems: **500,000 Rijks Dollars [Rijksdaalders]** needed. Please use all your influence to secure this (`300,000` would do to start with). Stress need — and close ties + govnmt. -> VL [*Vorstenlanden*].<br>`||` **DO NOT** ALLOW SULTAN to gain too great an Influence in affairs of STATE, but accord all privileges you can.<br><u>Question should be kept SECRET.</u> | **15 July 1811 — Governor-General Janssens (Batavia) to Pieter Engelhard:**<br>Recalls that upon taking office in 1808 Engelhard borrowed `50,000 guilders` from the Sultan, and notes recent Sultanate friction (perhaps due to preferential treatment of Surakarta).<br>Amid acute government financial straits—with silver coinage exhausted and copper insufficient—Janssens asks Engelhard to use all his influence to secretly obtain a loan of **500,000 Rix-Dollars** (`300,000` initially) from the Sultan or Patih, stressing close ties with the *Vorstenlanden* while granting privileges without letting the Sultan gain undue influence over state affairs. |
| **22 July 1811 — Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>Engelhard promises to do best to get a LOAN; said would use the annexed territories (`1810`) as BARGAINING POINT.<br>But: should point out that since 1808 relations with the SULTAN had deteriorated; already <span style="color:#a00"><u>`194,000`</u></span> guilders extracted after troop operations in 1810 `||`. | **22 July 1811 — Pieter Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>Engelhard promises to do his utmost to secure a loan, proposing to use the 1810 annexed territories as a bargaining chip, while cautioning that relations with the Sultan have deteriorated since 1808 and `194,000` guilders were already extracted following the 1810 military operations. |
