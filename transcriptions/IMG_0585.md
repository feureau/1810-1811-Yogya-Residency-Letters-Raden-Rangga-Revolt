Documents the outbreak of Radèn Rongga's rebellion: assuming the sovereign title *Kangjêng Susuhunan Prabu Ingalaga*, distributing royal regalia and payungs, absconding with Ratu Kencana Wulan's loaned jewels and Natakusuma's Bimanese warhorse, and triggering HB II's wrath.
