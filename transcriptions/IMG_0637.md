### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Daendels to Eng.**<br>1 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Marshal's Warning* | Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] to Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] (1 Dec. 1810):<br>• The Governor-General forcefully rejects Engelhard's exoneration of the Yogyakarta court: *"You have been deceived by the court — HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] cannot be excused."*<br>• Reports rapid military escalation: colonial cavalry and horse artillery squadrons are already marching overland, while regular infantry regiments are embarking at Batavia for Semarang. |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>6–7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Court Atmosphere* | Engelhard to Daendels (6–7 Dec. 1810):<br>• Submits a detailed confidential report on internal Kraton tensions, factional divisions, and the atmosphere of intense suspicion prevailing between the Sultan and his senior ministers. |
| **Court Council**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Council Deliberation* | High Court Council held in the Kraton (7 Dec. 1810):<br>• Senior Yogya regents, the Crown Prince [Pangéran Adipati Anom / future HB III], Pangéran Mangkudiningrat, and Pangéran Mangkubumi deliberate upon Batavia's stern demands.<br>• All princes and ministers unanimously agree that Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga] must be apprehended and delivered directly to Daendels at Buitenzorg [Bogor]. |
| **HB II's Reaction**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Bulging Eyes & Anger* | Eyewitness account of the Sultan's dramatic response during the assembly:<br>• Sultan HB II displays extreme visceral agitation: *"Rangga opgezeten; bulging eyes / + look of anger."*<br>• With repressed fury, the Sultan formally answers only: *"nandika"* [it is well / as spoken].<br>• The royal assembly consents to Daendels's legal demands regarding the extradition/punishment of the 3 *patihs* of Japar and the *bekel* of Desa Gabus.<br>• However, HB II raises difficulties over permitting the return of stolen goods. |
| **Reading Daendels' Letter**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Scene at the Dhampar* | Reading of Daendels's second stern missive (dated 3 Nov. 1810) before the assembled court:<br>• The court secretary reads the document aloud until reaching the contentious clause: *"whilst the Patih has fallen into disgrace."*<br>• In a sudden burst of rage, Sultan HB II physically snatches the letter out of the secretary's hands, summarily dismisses the secretary from the royal throne dais (*dhampar*), and commands DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] to step forward and continue reading.<br>• When the reading reaches the direct mention of Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I], HB II abruptly snatches the letter back once more.<br>• Terminating the royal audience without a word, the Sultan retreats immediately into his private inner chambers (*gedhong prabayeksa*), accompanied solely by his trusted heir, the Crown Prince. |
