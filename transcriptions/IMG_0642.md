### Two-Column Transcription (Margin Notes / Javanese Script vs. Main Text / Dutch Translation)

| Margin Notes / Javanese Script | Main Text / Dutch Translation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>7 Dec. 1810 (cont.)<br><br>*Crown Prince Interview* | Resident Pieter Engelhard [Eng.] to Marshal Daendels [Herman Willem Daendels] (7 Dec. 1810, continuation):<br>• The Crown Prince [CP / Pangéran Adipati Anom / future HB III] acknowledges to the Resident that Daendels's suspicions regarding the Kraton are understandable, given that HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] had previously been far too attached and partial to Radèn Rongga Prawiradirja III [R. Rangga].<br>• The Prince emphasizes that following Rangga's abrupt flight, HB II has become exceedingly bitter and hostile towards him.<br>• CP expresses complete certainty that the Sultan had no prior knowledge of Rangga's flight, nor that Rangga would leave treasonous letters addressed to Natakusuma [NK] or Natadiningrat [ND]. |
| **Assessment of NK**<br>7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Age & Indolence* | Resident interrogates the Crown Prince regarding Pangéran Natakusuma [Pakualam I / NK]:<br>• CP observes that had Natakusuma been younger, he might conceivably attempt an adventurous intrigue against the throne.<br>• However, Natakusuma's advanced age and comfortable lifestyle provide a firm guarantee that he will remain peaceful and passive.<br>• CP reveals that Natakusuma was summoned to the palace multiple times after Rangga's escape on trivial pretexts simply so HB II could closely monitor his secret reactions.<br>• In spite of suspicions, HB II has placed no formal restrictions upon Natakusuma's movements.<br>• Engelhard actively urges the Crown Prince to make every effort to influence and moderate HB II's political obstinacy while current circumstances and his royal rank still offer the opportunity.<br>*(Carey notes: [Letter incomplete])* |
| **Eng. to Daendels**<br>? 7 Dec. 1810<br><br>*Second Expedition Plan* | Proposal for a secondary Kraton punitive expedition:<br>• The Kraton deliberates sending out a new column commanded by Pangéran Jayakusuma, Natawijaya, Natadiningrat [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat], and Radèn Jayengrana.<br>• The Sultan requests Engelhard to furnish six European artillery cannon with accompanying ammunition to bombard and capture Rangga's fortified residence (*dalêm* / kraton). |
| **Engelhard's Refusal**<br>Dec. 1810<br><br>*Rejection of DR II's Departure* | Resident Engelhard's strategic assessment and veto:<br>• Engelhard deems the extra expedition and Dutch artillery entirely unnecessary.<br>• Argues that despite the sluggishness of Purwadipura [PWD], once the columns of Dipakusuma, Adinagara, and Pringgakusuma unite with the loyal Mangkunagaran [MN] regents, Rangga will be swiftly crushed due to the extreme smallness of his following and the widespread local hatred against him in Madiun.<br>• Counsels HB II to solicit Daendels's formal opinion before dispatching any fresh expedition commanded by DR II [Radèn Adipati Danureja II].<br>• Margin note: The departure of Danureja II from the capital would create severe administrative and political difficulties for Engelhard.<br>• Sultan HB II concurs with Engelhard's advice and suspends the secondary expedition. |
