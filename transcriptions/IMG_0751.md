| PATIH [Radèn Adipati Danureja II] replies that SULTAN [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] could NOT extend a LOAN because CAPITAL [Treasury] personal property of KRATON; could NOT part with it because of Javanese LAW `||`.<br>`|` **DIAMOND ORDER** *(Nederlandsche Leeuw)* given to Emperor of SOLO [Sunan Pakubuwana IV] and PATIH, but NOT SULTAN — makes Sultan jealous...<br>Kedhu NOT to be given back under scheme for returning annexed territories. | The Patih replied that the Sultan could not extend a loan because Kraton treasure is personal/dynastic property that cannot be parted with under Javanese law.<br>Meanwhile, the **Diamond Order** (*Nederlandsche Leeuw*) was awarded to the Sunan of Surakarta and the Patih, but not to the Sultan, provoking the Sultan's jealousy. Kedhu is not to be returned under any territory-restoration scheme. |
| **Djogja [Yogyakarta], 2 Aug. 1811 — Engelhard [Pieter Engelhard] -> Janssens [Lieutenant-General Jan Willem Janssens]:**<br>No silver money available over and above monthly payment of MILITARY Establishment.<br>**TAN DJIN SING** [Tan Jin Sing] in charge of taxation of ceded lands: pays DIRECT to govnmt [Government] per month -> `2,000 Sp. Dollars` [Spanish Dollars] + `1,000` for opium chest.<br>**EUROPEAN INHABITANTS** find themselves PAUPERS in lending sums of money at high interest to Javanese for RENT purposes.<br>**TAN DJIN SING to Kedhu:** Brother's wedding -> would be able to procure thousands of Sp. guilders from Tan Jin Sing.<br>W.T. [?] Eng. already owes = `15,000` -> MORRIS on personal account; `30,000` for BANDAR's RENT. | **Yogyakarta, 2 August 1811 — Engelhard to Janssens:**<br>No silver remains beyond the monthly military payroll.<br>**Tan Jin Sing**, overseeing taxation of the ceded lands, pays `2,000 Spanish Dollars` monthly directly to the Government plus `1,000` per opium chest. European residents are impoverished by lending at high interest to Javanese for rent obligations. Tan Jin Sing travelled to Kedhu for his brother's wedding, where thousands of Spanish guilders might be raised from him; Engelhard already owes `15,000` to Morris personally and `30,000` for *bandar* rent. |
| **Semarang, 5 Sept. 1811 — Janssens -> Engelhard:**<br>Does NOT know whether letter will get through but wishes to let Engelhard know about SITUATION of N.K. [Pangéran Natakusuma] / N.D. [Radèn Tumenggung Natadiningrat]:<br>CHERIBON: learns that Waterloo [Matthijs Waterloo] [had] orders from Daendels [Marshal Herman Willem Daendels] to murder N.K. / N.D. `||` | **Semarang, 5 September 1811 — Janssens to Engelhard:**<br>Uncertain whether the letter will get through, Janssens informs Engelhard of Natakusuma and Natadiningrat's situation: at Cirebon, he learned that Waterloo had received orders from Daendels to murder Natakusuma and Natadiningrat. |
