# Transcription of IMG_0503_rotated_90.jpg

## Metadata
- **Image File:** `jpg/IMG_0503_rotated_90.jpg`
- **Source:** Peter Carey Collection / Archival Research Notes (*Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek 1602–1811*, vol. XVI [1808–1811], ed. J.A. van der Chijs [Batavia: Landsdrukkerij, 1897], pp. 76–77: Government Decrees of H.W. Daendels, 14–20 February 1810)
- **Date Range:** 1810-02-14 to 1810-02-20
- **Key Events & Entities:**
  - **Key Events:** Official printed decrees of Governor-General Daendels issued at Batavia and Buitenzorg. Concludes the financial reconstruction plan for Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II), requiring the Minister at Surakarta to schedule final liquidation of all residual princely debts within 15 years from Mangkunagaran revenues. Considers report from the Drossaard of the Batavia Environs (*Bataviasche Ommelanden*) regarding the collection of native navigation passes on the Krawang and Tjidané rivers, salt trade permits, food-stall (*warong*) licences, ronggèng performances, firearm registration fees, and marriage registration by district schouten, pending commission review; increases daily wages for 12 coolies at the central medical warehouse (*magazijn van geneesmiddelen*) from 3 to 12 stuivers; and enacts a maintenance toll for the Eastern Highway (*Ooster rijweg*) between Batavia and Buitenzorg.
  - **Key Entities (Persons):** Marshal Herman Willem Daendels, Pangéran Prangwedana (Mangkunagara II / Ario Praboe Prang Wedono).
  - **Key Entities (Places):** Surakarta (Souracarta), Batavia, Bataviasche Ommelanden, Krawang, Tjidané (Cisadane), Buitenzorg.
  - **Key Entities (Institutions / Terms):** *Nederlandsch-Indisch Plakaatboek*, Sprokkelmaand (February), drossaard (bailiff / judicial magistrate), schouten (constables / bailiffs), warong, ronggèng, rijksdaalders (RD), stuivers, koeli (coolies), magazijn van geneesmiddelen (medical depot), Ooster rijweg (Great Post Road eastern branch).

| **14 Sprokkelmaand<br>[14 Feb. 1810]<br>Batavia Environs** | **Heffingen van inlanders in de Bataviasche Ommelanden:**<br><br>• The *drossaard* (magistrate) of the Batavia Environs (*Ommelanden*) reports resuming collection of local native administrative fees (navigation and license passes on the Krawang and Tjidané rivers, salt transport, *warong* licenses, *ronggèng* dance permits, firearm stamps), yielding 500 to 1,000 rijksdaalders [RD] annually, used for office expenses and clerk salaries.<br>• Reports on native marriage registrations: district *schouten* authorized to issue stamped marriage certificates (costing 5 RD on 6-stuiver stamped paper) to prevent extortion by native commandants.<br>• Supreme Government defers action pending findings of the Ommelanden Commission appointed 23 Louwmaand [Jan.] 1810. |
