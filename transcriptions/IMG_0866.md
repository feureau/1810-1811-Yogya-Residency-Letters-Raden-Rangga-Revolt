| `2.` Concerting + Rajahs of Java to know who are true friends of English King and who are still desirous of remaining under Dutch.<br>British desirous of establishing + supporting all legitimate authority according to immemorial customs.<br>Rajahs of Java after war to enjoy peace / quiet. | **Point 2 (pp. 84–85):** Concerting with Javanese rulers to distinguish friends of the English King from Dutch partisans, pledging to uphold legitimate authority according to immemorial customs and ensure post-war peace. |
| `p. 85` `3.` British great territories in Hindustan `∴` Java of no importance to them. Main object of going to Java to attack Dutch + relieve Regents of oppressive govnmt. [government]. `∴` expel enemies + act as friends to all Rajahs + legal authorities. | **Point 3 (p. 85):** Asserts that given Britain's vast territories in Hindustan, Java is sought not for conquest but to expel the Dutch/French and relieve the Javanese rulers from oppressive rule. |
