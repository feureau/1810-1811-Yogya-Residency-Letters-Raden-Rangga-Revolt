| `Art. 9`: That my subjects at Singsir + Kalingsas who are living on Java's N.E. [North-East] coast with all that has any relation to it may again be restored to me. | **Article 9:** That the Sunan's subjects at Singsir and Kalingsas on Java's North-East coast, with everything pertaining to them, be restored. |
| `Art. 10`: That as experience has taught that the questionable affairs or differences between the Government and subjects, the Javanese, are always troublesome, I request on that account that Javanese Justice may be at liberty to manifest their affairs and sincerely and according to truth that expenses which they occasion may be equitable and not exhorbitant to the security of Java's prosperity. | **Article 10:** Requesting that Javanese justice be permitted to handle disputes between the Government and Javanese subjects openly and truthfully, with equitable rather than exorbitant expenses. |
| `Art. 11`: That in case I may have anything else to Request likewise as equitable and reasonable as that which I have done heretofore, and hoping herewith to have pleasantly set out my views & fancies no harm to name myself. Signed `KRIJGSMAN` (`Copy in Eur F. 148/17, 'Captain Robinson Java 1811'`). | **Article 11:** Reserving the right to make further equitable and reasonable requests in the future. Signed by C.
