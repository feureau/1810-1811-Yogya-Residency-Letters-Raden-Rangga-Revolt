| "The Emperor upon this sighed disconsolated and for a moment spoke not a word, the other princes sitting on the floor likewise did the same." | Upon hearing Robison's question, the Emperor sighed disconsolately and remained silent for a moment, as did the princes seated on the floor. |
| Robison asks if received letters from Raffles sent from Malacca. At first Emperor says "NO", red margin (`+ Minto's Proclamation sent via Sultans of Ciribon`): `||` But later admits receiving 2 letters from Raffles enclosing letter -> Sultan of Djogja, which Emperor does **not** forward. Emperor asks whether destroy letters = esp. [especially] keen to destroy letter to Sultan of Djogja. | Robison asked whether the Sunan had received letters from Thomas Stamford Raffles sent from Malacca (along with Minto's Proclamation sent via the Sultans of Cirebon). Initially the Emperor denied it, but later admitted receiving two letters from Raffles enclosing a letter for the Sultan of Yogyakarta [HB II], which he had **not** forwarded. The Emperor asked whether to destroy the letters, especially keen to destroy the one addressed to Yogyakarta. |
| Princes urge Emperor to be frank about grievances (`Cakranegara?` -> "speak together with Robison" / conversation passed on to Robison by Krijgsman). Emperor complains of van Braam: way tollgates / Bird's Nests administered and treats Emperor very hard. "Van Braam never satisfied although draws farm of 60,000 Sp. Dollars p.a. [per annum] + other advantages." `∴` asks for van Braam + Servatius to be dismissed. | The princes urged the Emperor to speak frankly about his grievances (with Cakranegara advising him to speak openly with Robison through Krijgsman). The Emperor complained of Resident van Braam's harsh treatment and his administration of tollgates and Birds' Nests farms, noting van Braam was never satisfied despite drawing `60,000` Sp. D. [Spanish Dollars] per annum plus other advantages. Consequently, he asked for van Braam and Servatius to be dismissed. |
| Robison promises to pass on complaints to Minto: urges van Braam to be relieved by an Englishman. Robison then gives pocket watch to Emperor as a sign of friendship (looked at by all princes); asks Emperor if wants manufactured goods from Bengal, Madras. Emperor requests following: Persian or Arab Horse, Elephant, English Broad Swords, English Broadcloths, Velvet + silk goods. Gives Robison Javanese Kris + Golden Scabbard. | Robison promised to convey these complaints to Lord Minto and urged that van Braam be relieved by an Englishman. Robison presented a pocket watch to the Emperor as a token of friendship and asked what manufactured goods from Bengal or Madras he desired. The Emperor requested a Persian or Arab horse, an elephant, English broadswords, English broadcloths, velvet, and silk goods, and presented Robison with a Javanese kris in a golden scabbard. |
