### Header & Archival Classification
* **Top Center (Red Ink):** `20 Feb. 19 Mar. 1811`
* **Top Right:** `AN 'Djokjo Brieven' No. 46 R. Ronggo 1810-11`
* **Margin (Left):** `Anon. Report, Jav. letter, 9 Sura, 20 Feb. 1811` / `Reports by Spy about Yogya` / `Report of Yogya Spy, 19 Mar. 1811`
* **Middle Right Annotation:** `AN 'Bundel Solo Rapporten 1810-16' no. 37`
* **Bottom Center (Red Ink):** `Set in Yogya Jan. - Feb. 1811`
* **Document Subject:** Appointments of Danureja II's brothers (Mèrtawijaya and Mèrtadiwiria) to lead the Raja's *prajurits*; day-and-night polarization between the *Kraton* and *Kadipatèn*; rumours of civil war between father and son and Purwadipura's exile to Selamanik; HB II's refusal to grant *sawah* lands to the Prince Regent's new bupatis; and 40 *hajis* praying for the Prince Regent's death.
* **Physical Characteristics:** Ruled white notebook folio paper; two binder punch holes on left margin with faint rust halos; blue ink handwriting with red ink date headings, margin vertical mark, and red underline.
