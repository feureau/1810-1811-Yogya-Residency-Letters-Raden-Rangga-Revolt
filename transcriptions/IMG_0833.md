| `p. 378 / fn. 1. / 15 Dec 1811` (`Raffles to Crawfurd 15 Dec. 1811`):<br>Crawfurd To Tell HB II [Sultan Hamengkubuwana II] / Regent that assumption of authority in opposition to Treaty `31 Dec. 1810`.<br>Crawfurd Instructed only to Address the Regent:<br>After ambassadors sent from HB II -> Semarang, HB II should write a letter to Raffles explaining his conduct, acknowledge errors / express regrets.<br>HB II should Resign Throne - leave it up to Eng. [English] govnmt. [government] To Decide.<br>Raffles tells NK [Natakusuma] on those conditions HB II could be Received Back into Favour again:<br>"Letter to be signed by the Sultan to be Dictated by You: and you will express it in such words as to adequately express the atonement intended. The letter should contain the best excuse for his conduct that can be adduced... The above will in my opinion be sufficient atonement on the part of the Sultan and a Justification for my again placing him in power."<br>*(underlined in red: `It must be laid down as a principle in all our proceedings with the Native Courts that in no case we must demand what we cannot enforce in case of Refusal.`)* | **15 December 1811 — Thomas Stamford Raffles to John Crawfurd** *(cited in D.M. Campbell, Java: Past and Present, Vol. I, p. 378, fn. 1)*:<br>Crawfurd is to inform Sultan Hamengkubuwana II and the Regent [HB III] that HB II's assumption of authority violates the Treaty of 31 December 1810, and address only the Regent. After sending ambassadors to Semarang, HB II must sign a letter dictated by Crawfurd expressing atonement and resigning the throne to the English Government's decision, upon which Raffles may restore him to power. Closes with the principle: *"in no case we must demand what we cannot enforce in case of Refusal."* |
