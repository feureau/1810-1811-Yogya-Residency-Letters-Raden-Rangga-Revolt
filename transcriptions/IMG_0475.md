### Cross-Reference with `39_Expanded_Keyword_Index.md`

* **People:**
  * **Iman Sampurna (Iman Jaya Sampurna)** (`Iman Jaya Sampurna`): Listed under *Religious Leaders, Scholars, Mystics, & Teachers* (line 132). Mystic from Lodaya who prophesied the arrival of the *Ratu Adil* and a general slaughter of Europeans and Chinese in 1819.
  * **Ratu Kidul**: Listed under *Spiritual Figures & Deities* (line 146). Goddess of the Southern Ocean and spiritual protector/consort of the royal houses of Mataram.
  * **Radèn Adipati Danureja I (Yudanegara / Gandakusuma)** (`PR I / R.T. Yudanegara`): Listed under *Patih of Yogyakarta* (line 181). First *patih* of Yogyakarta (1755–1799); founding father of the Danurejan lineage.
  * **Sultan Mangkubumi (Hamengkubuwana I)** (`HB I`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 11). First Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1749–1792).
  * **Sultan Hamengkubuwana II (Sultan Sepuh)** (`HB II`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 12). Second Sultan of Yogyakarta (reigned 1792–1810).
  * **Sunan Pakubuwana IV (Sunan Bagus)** (`PB IV`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 18). Ruler of Surakarta (reigned 1788–1820) who orchestrated the 1815 Sepoy conspiracy against British rule.
  * **Pangéran Mangkubumi (Surakarta)** (`Mangkubumi`): Listed under *Javanese Royalty and Princely Families* (line 80). Brother of PB IV, framed as the instigator of the Sepoy Plot and exiled to Ambon (1818–1824).
  * **Pieter Herbert Baron van Lawick van Pabst** (`Lawick van Pabst`): Listed under *Residents at Yogyakarta* (line 348). Author of the secret memorandum (*Nota*).
* **Places & Institutions:**
  * **Lodhaya (Lodaya)**: Listed under *Places: South Coast / Mystical Sites* (line 132). Remote forested district in southern Blitar associated with tiger spirits and millennial mysticism.
  * **Surakarta (Sala)**: Capital of the Sunanate.
  * **Yogyakarta**: Capital of the Sultanate.
* **Terms:**
  * **Sepoy plot (1815)**: Widespread anti-British conspiracy organized by Sunan Pakubuwana IV and mutinous soldiers of the Bengal Native Infantry stationed in Central Java.
  * **Ratu Adil**: The messianic 'Just King' expected in Javanese millenarian movements.
  * **patih**: Royal grand vizier.
